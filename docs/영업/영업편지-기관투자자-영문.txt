Korean filings data in English — three datasets nobody else compiles

You hold Korean equities. The filings behind them are published in Korean,
on DART, one company at a time. We compile them into English panel data.

What we publish
  Ownership and insider filings   Large-holding reports and executive share
                                  filings — who moved a stake in a listed
                                  company, and when.
  Convertibles, warrants, EB      Korean CB, BW and EB issues as filed —
                                  private versus public placement, coupons,
                                  refixing floors.
  Sector workforce panel          Headcount, tenure, pay and the gender gap,
                                  as filed. The axis nobody else counts.

Also open, free, as a JSON API:
  Annual financial statements for 2,709 listed companies (2024 and 2025)
  Korean brokerage reports and the analyst ranking, as published
  Dated history of KRX index levels, with English index names

  https://seoulmarkets.com/v1

How we handle what we cannot measure
  A company with no statement on file carries nulls, never zeros.
  A report with no previous target price carries null, not "unchanged".
  An index low the source prints as 0 is marked not-measured.
  Every response names its source and its coverage.
