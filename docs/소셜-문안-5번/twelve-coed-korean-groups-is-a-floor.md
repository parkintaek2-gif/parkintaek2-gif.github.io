## Threads
```
Only 12 of 379 Korean groups have both men and women

We sorted every Korean music group on Wikidata by the sex recorded for each member. 185 are all-men, 182 all-women, 12 mixed. 49 could not be sorted at all.

But 12 is a floor, not a count. 127 of the sorted groups still contain a member with no sex recorded, and filling in one record could move any of them into the mixed column.

Anyone quoting 12 as the answer is quoting a number we know to be too low: https://www.kculturewire.com/article/twelve-coed-korean-groups-is-a-floor
```
