Chinese Wikipedia, not Japanese, is where Korean stars are read most

We asked Wikimedia how many people opened 478 Korean entertainers' articles in each of 15 language editions over three months. With English set aside, Chinese leads for 306 and Japanese for 142. For 59 people, Chinese is read at least three times as much.

One thing this cannot mean: Chinese Wikipedia is blocked in mainland China. These are languages, not countries.
