A Seoul court suspended the exclusive contract between EXO members Chen, Baekhyun and Xiumin —
known together as EXO-CBX — and their agency, INB100, on 15 September, according to Korea
JoongAng Daily and STARNEWS. The trio had sought the injunction after accusing the agency, whose
head Cha Ga-won faces a separate, reported ₩30 billion fraud allegation, of failing to pay them.
The court sided with the three artists while their main lawsuit over the contract's validity
continues.

We don't hold the case file. What we hold is how many people looked the three of them up on
English Wikipedia — and as of publication, we can't yet check the one day that matters most.

## What we measured

We pulled daily English Wikipedia pageviews for Baekhyun, Chen and Xiumin over the 30 days ending
14 September 2026 — the freshest complete window the Wikimedia API had at the time of writing.
Over that stretch, Baekhyun's page averaged **293 views a day**, Chen's **187**, and Xiumin's
**159**.

| | 30-day avg | 7 Sep (filing reported) | vs. avg | 14 Sep (freshest data) | vs. avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baekhyun | 293 | 363 | 1.24x | 443 | **1.51x** |
| Chen | 187 | 245 | 1.31x | 190 | 1.02x |
| Xiumin | 159 | 209 | 1.31x | 126 | 0.79x |

On 7 September, when STARNEWS reported the trio's application to suspend the contract, all three
pages moved together — a consistent 1.24x-to-1.31x rise. That's the shape we'd expect from one
shared news event touching three people equally.

## The bigger spike hit only one of them

14 September looks different. Baekhyun's page had its highest day in our entire 30-day window —
443 views, 1.51x his baseline — while Chen's page barely moved and Xiumin's was actually below
his average. We hold no headline dated 14 September that names Baekhyun specifically, so we are
not guessing at what drove it. It may be connected to his role founding INB100 in 2023, or to
something we simply don't have a matched source for. Either way, a rise that hits one member and
not the other two is a different kind of signal than the shared 7 September move, and we're not
going to paper over that difference by averaging it away.

## What we can't tell you yet

The court granted the injunction on 15 September — the actual news this article is about — and
we cannot yet say whether that moved reading at all. Wikimedia's pageviews API typically lags
1-2 days, and as of publication it has no rows for 15 September for any of the three pages. If
you're reading this expecting us to connect the court ruling to a reading spike, we don't have
that number. We have the smaller, earlier filing story instead, and we're telling you which is
which rather than filling the gap with the wrong day's data.
