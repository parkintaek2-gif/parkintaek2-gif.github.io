Search for zodiac compatibility and you will find a great many pages telling you which signs belong
together. What you will not find is anyone checking.

K-pop is an unusually good place to check. Groups are assembled deliberately, members' birth dates are
recorded, and there are hundreds of groups. If signs paired off in any way at all, it would show up here.

So we counted.

## What we counted

Every group in our list with at least two members whose birth date is on record. Within each group, every
member paired with every other member, and the two signs tallied.

| | |
|---|---:|
| Groups | 259 |
| Members counted | 1,513 |
| Member-to-member pairings | 4,651 |
| Members left out, no birth date | 65 |

That is 78 possible sign pairs, from Aries + Aries through Pisces + Pisces.

## The result

The most common pairing is **Leo + Aquarius**, 93 times. Chance alone would give 82.1.

That is the whole finding. Of the 78 pairs, the one furthest above chance is **Leo + Scorpio at 1.25×**,
and the one furthest below is **Virgo + Libra at 0.75×**. Everything else sits between.

With counts of this size, gaps of that size are what randomness produces. Run 78 comparisons and a few
will land two standard deviations out; that is not a signal, it is arithmetic.

| Pair | Together | By chance | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Leo + Aquarius | 93 | 82.1 | 1.13× |
| Gemini + Aquarius | 91 | 77.3 | 1.18× |
| Libra + Aquarius | 90 | 75 | 1.20× |
| Gemini + Leo | 86 | 72.6 | 1.18× |
| Leo + Scorpio | 85 | 68.1 | 1.25× |
| … | | | |
| Pisces + Libra | 59 | 74.5 | 0.79× |
| Libra + Libra | 25 | 32.1 | 0.78× |
| Virgo + Libra | 49 | 65.3 | 0.75× |

[The full 78-row table is here](/star-sign-pairs).

**No two signs find each other, and no two avoid each other.**

## Why Leo and Aquarius top the table

Because they are two of the three commonest signs among K-pop members — Aquarius 147, Pisces 146, Leo 138 — and not because
anything draws them together. This is exactly why the *by chance* column exists. A ranked list of raw
counts is an invitation to read meaning into the top row. Putting the expected figure beside it takes
that invitation back.

## The one thing that does bend

Birthdays are not spread evenly through the K-pop calendar. July is the thinnest month, at 0.69 times
what an even year would give. [We measured that separately](/kpop-birthdays), and it is a fact about when
people are born in Korea, not about star signs — the same skew shows up in populations that have never
heard of Leo.

## What this is not

It is not a compatibility reading, and it is not a claim that astrology is wrong about anything. It is a
count of who stood on a stage with whom, which is the only part of this question a birth date can settle.

One limitation cuts in our favour and is worth naming for that reason: because a member sits in many
pairings at once, the pairings are not independent, so any test treating them as independent overstates
how surprising a gap is. In other words the real spread is even less remarkable than the table makes it
look. A limitation that only ever helps the writer is one to say out loud.
