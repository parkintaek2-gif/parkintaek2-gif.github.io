A Reddit post on 14 September reported that Chung Ha had parted ways with her agency, MORE
VISION, after three years. We don't hold the underlying business story — no contract terms, no
statement from either side — but we do hold one thing ourselves: how many people looked her up
on English Wikipedia that day.

## What we measured

We pulled Chung Ha's daily English Wikipedia pageviews for the 30 days ending 14 September
2026, the most recent complete window available at publication. Over that month her page drew
**11,824 views, averaging 394 a day**, in a range of **167 to 1,505**.

On 14 September — the day the split was reported — her page drew **648 views**, which is real
movement: **2.2 times** the average of the four days right before it (10-13 September, 294/day)
and **1.6 times** the 30-day average.

## But it wasn't the biggest day in the window

| | Views | vs. 30-day average |
|---|---|---|
| 30 August | 1,505 | 3.8x |
| 31 August | 1,030 | 2.6x |
| 14 September (agency-split report) | 648 | 1.6x |

The two largest days in our 30-day window were 30 and 31 August — three weeks before the
reported agency split, and larger than the split-report day by a wide margin. We do not know
why. Our community-desk keyword tracker only began surfacing Chung Ha posts with this run, so
we hold no matched headline for late August and are not guessing at a cause we can't source.

## What this does and doesn't show

The 14 September rise is consistent with the agency-split news moving real reading — the dates
line up and the size (2.2x a four-day baseline) is not trivial. But "consistent with" is not
proof, and it is smaller than a spike we can't explain at all. Anyone citing this month's reading
numbers as evidence the split news "went viral" should know the same page had a bigger,
unexplained day three weeks earlier for a story we never saw.
