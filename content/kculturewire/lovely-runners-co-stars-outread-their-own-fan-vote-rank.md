STARPOLL, the fan-voting app run by STARNEWS and official partner of the AAA 2026 awards, ranks
Korean actors the way it ranks idols — a running public vote, visible without logging in. On 16
September 2026, its Male Actor category put Kim Soo-hyun first, Lee Jun-ho second, Byeon Woo-seok
third. Its Female Actor category put Kim Hye-yoon first, Park Eun-bin second.

We hold a different number for the same names: English Wikipedia pageviews, which count people
who looked a name up rather than people who cast a vote. We checked whether the two orders agree.

## What we measured

We pulled 30 days of daily English Wikipedia pageviews (18 August-16 September 2026) for the top
three men and top two women in STARPOLL's actor vote.

| | STARPOLL vote | Vote rank | 30-day Wikipedia total | Wikipedia rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kim Soo-hyun | 29,713 | 1 | 52,236 | 1 |
| Lee Jun-ho | 16,265 | 2 | 25,841 | 3 |
| Byeon Woo-seok | 6,180 | 3 | **43,808** | **2** |
| Kim Hye-yoon | 15,698 | 1 | 16,471 | 2 |
| Park Eun-bin | 12,010 | 2 | **95,956** | **1** |

Kim Soo-hyun holds first place on both counts. Below him, the order breaks. Byeon Woo-seok drew
well under half of Lee Jun-ho's votes but 1.7 times his Wikipedia reading. Among women, the gap is
larger: Park Eun-bin placed second in the vote, behind Kim Hye-yoon, but her Wikipedia total runs
5.8x higher.

## Two names, one show, opposite gaps

Kim Hye-yoon and Byeon Woo-seok played the two leads of *Lovely Runner*, a 2024 series
[Byeon has called one of the "greatest achievements"](https://www.rappler.com/entertainment/celebrities/byeon-woo-seok-korean-drama-lovely-runner-greatest-achievements/)
of his career and a documented international breakout for him. In STARPOLL's vote, Kim
Hye-yoon outranks him — but on our reading count, his total (43,808) runs more than 2.6 times
higher than hers (16,471). Same show, same audience moment, and the domestic vote and the
English-language reading count still don't agree on who it moved more.

Park Eun-bin's gap has a similarly specific, confirmed driver: her 2022 series *Extraordinary
Attorney Woo* won the Grand Prize at the 59th Baeksang Arts Awards and drew wide English-language
press coverage internationally. We don't hold an equivalent confirmed international moment for
Kim Hye-yoon or Lee Jun-ho — which doesn't mean one doesn't exist, only that we haven't found a
source for it.

## What this does and doesn't tell us

A fan vote counts people motivated to vote, repeatedly, for a name they already follow. A
pageview counts someone who looked a name up — often because of a specific show, award, or news
event, not sustained fandom. Both are real measurements of real behavior. They are not measuring
the same thing, and this month, for these five names, they don't point the same direction.
