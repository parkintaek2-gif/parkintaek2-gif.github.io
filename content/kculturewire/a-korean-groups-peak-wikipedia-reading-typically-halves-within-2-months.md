Among 774 Korean music acts with at least two years of monthly English Wikipedia reading history, 731 — 94.4% — have already fallen to half or less of their all-time peak month's reading. The typical act got there in 2 months.

## What we counted

Starting from a Wikidata list of Korean music groups, we pulled each act's monthly English Wikipedia pageviews from July 2015 through August 2026, found each act's single highest-reading month, and measured how many months later reading first fell to half that peak or below. 847 acts were identified; 73 didn't have enough reading history (fewer than 24 usable months) to measure reliably and are set aside, not counted as zero.

| | Acts |
|---|---|
| Measured (24+ months of history) | 774 |
| Already fallen to half peak or below | 731 (94.4%) |
| Not yet at half peak | 43 |

## How fast — and how much it varies

| Percentile | Months from peak to half-peak |
|---|---|
| 25th | 1 |
| 50th (median) | 2 |
| 75th | 5 |
| 90th | 13 |

Three examples show the range. NewJeans, with 50 months of reading history, halved from its peak in 3 months. BTS, with 134 months of history, took 13 months — near the top of the distribution, one of the slower fades measured. Stray Kids, with the longest history in this sample at 108 months, took 65 months to halve, the outlier at the far end.

## What this does not show

This is not a claim that these acts became less popular in any commercial sense — we count Wikipedia reads, not streams, sales, or ticket demand. An early article also tends to read lower partly because it is thinner before an act is well known, which can inflate how sharp some peaks look. And 43 acts, mostly recent peaks, have not yet reached half — we report where they stand today, not where they are headed.
