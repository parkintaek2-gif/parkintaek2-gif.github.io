Blackpink's Rosé released "new trick" on 18 September — a single whose music video, directed by Dave Meyers, was filmed entirely on an iPhone 18 Pro as part of Apple's "Shot on iPhone" campaign. The release had been announced earlier in the month, with fans already discussing the track online on 17 September, the day before it came out.

We can't yet check the release day itself: Wikimedia's pageviews API had not published 18 September's numbers as of this writing, and we are not estimating them.

What we can check is the day before. Over the 29 days ending 16 September, Rosé's English Wikipedia page averaged 2,631 views a day. On 17 September, it drew 3,303 views — 1.26 times that average. That's a real rise, but it lands on the eve of the release, not the release itself, which fits a run-up to a known date rather than a reaction to news that broke that day.

This is a different shape from the other two Spotify-milestone stories we checked this week. Jimin's and Riize's streaming records were announcements about numbers already tracked by Spotify, and neither moved their reading. Rosé's rise, by contrast, sits ahead of an unreleased single — anticipation for something that hadn't happened yet, not a reaction to something that had. We will check the actual release day once the data allows.
