**Squid Game** entered a Netflix weekly top 10 in **93 countries**, across 60 weeks and 3,228 chart
places. It is the widest-travelling Korean title we hold.

We can see thirteen of its cast in our data. For each, we counted the countries reached by their
**next** biggest charting title — the one that is not Squid Game.

The median is **69 countries**. The distribution is not a slope — it is two clusters. **Seven cast
members have a second title above 50 countries. Five have one at five or fewer.**

## The two leads are on the low side

This is the part that is easy to get backwards. **Lee Jung-jae**, who played Gi-hun, has nine
charting titles — more than most of the cast — and the widest of them apart from Squid Game is
*Hunt*, at **four countries**. **Jung Ho-yeon**'s is *Hope*, at **one**.

Meanwhile **Park Hae-soo** and **Lee You-mi**, further down the call sheet, each have a second title
that also reached 93.

Being the face of the most-travelled Korean title in the data does not put your other work on more
charts. In this cast it is mildly the other way round.

## Count of titles and reach of titles are different things

**Lee Byung-hun** has **thirteen** charting titles, the most of anyone here. His widest one outside
Squid Game reached **19 countries**. A long list and a wide list are separate facts, and "prolific"
gets used for both.
