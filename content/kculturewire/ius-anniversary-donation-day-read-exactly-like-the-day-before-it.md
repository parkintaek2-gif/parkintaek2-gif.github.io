We wrote yesterday that IU's 18 September debut-anniversary donation — 200 million won under the fan-tribute name "IUaena," bringing her total giving this year to 600 million won per Maeil Business Newspaper — had landed in the middle of a reading rise that started roughly a week earlier, and that we couldn't yet check what the announcement day itself did, because Wikimedia's pageviews API hadn't published 18 September's numbers.

It has now. The number is 2,101 views on her English Wikipedia page — 1.08 times the 1,937-view baseline we calculated from the 30 days before. That's the same ratio, to two decimal places, as 17 September, the day before the news broke: 2,099 views, also 1.08x. The two days are two views apart.

The announcement did not lift her reading above where the pre-existing rise had already put it. Whatever moved English-language Wikipedia readers to look her up more over the past two weeks, it wasn't this specific piece of news — the day it broke reads identically to the day before it.
