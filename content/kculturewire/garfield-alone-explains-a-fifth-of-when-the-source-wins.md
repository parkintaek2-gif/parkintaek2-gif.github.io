Our earlier count of 51 comic-to-screen adaptation pairs found that Korean webtoon adaptations read a median 14 times more than their source comic, and non-Korean adaptations read 3.7 times more. That piece was about the 37 pairs where the adaptation won. This one is about the 14 where it didn't — and one franchise accounts for three of them.

## The 14 times the source won

| Adaptation | Source | Source read more by |
|---|---|---|
| Garfield's Fun Fest | Garfield | 18.6&times; |
| Liar Game | Liar Game (manga) | 11.8&times; |
| Garfield's Pet Force | Garfield | 11.6&times; |
| Garfield Gets Real | Garfield | 6.8&times; |
| Cantabile Tomorrow | Nodame Cantabile | 5.0&times; |
| To the Beautiful You | Hana-Kimi | 3.8&times; |
| Late Night Restaurant | Shin'ya Shokudō | 2.7&times; |
| Blade of the Immortal | Blade of the Immortal | 2.1&times; |
| You're My Pet | Tramps Like Us | 2.1&times; |
| City Hunter | City Hunter | 1.9&times; |
| Yumi's Cells: The Movie | Yumi's Cells (webtoon) | 1.6&times; |
| Parasyte: The Grey | Parasyte | 1.6&times; |
| Antique | Antique Bakery | 1.3&times; |
| Sweet Combat | Girls of the Wild's | 1.1&times; |

Three of the 14 rows are Garfield. All three are lower-profile spin-off specials (Gets Real, Pet Force, Fun Fest), and all three lose to the long-running original comic strip by wide margins — 6.8x, 11.6x and 18.6x.

## What this adds to the earlier finding

The earlier piece's medians (14x for Korean pairs, 3.7x for others) describe the typical case, not every case. Fourteen of 51 pairs — including 2 of the 18 Korean-original pairs, Sweet Combat and Yumi's Cells: The Movie — went the other way. A single long-running, widely recognized source franchise like Garfield can supply a disproportionate share of those exceptions, because a newer or lower-profile adaptation of a famous original has more room to read below its source than a newer adaptation of a lesser-known one does.
