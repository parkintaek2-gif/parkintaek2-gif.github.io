Take the seven members of **BTS**. Take the four Southeast Asian Wikipedias where both the member
and the group have an article — Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai and Malay. That is **28 checks**.

The group page wins **all 28**.

| Member | Reads per million, four editions | BTS |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| **V** | 233.3 | 380.8 |
| **Jungkook** | 139.7 | 380.8 |
| **Kim Seok-jin** | 74.4 | 380.8 |
| **Suga** | 72.0 | 380.8 |
| **RM** | 59.4 | 380.8 |
| **J-Hope** | 58.8 | 380.8 |
| **Jimin** | 55.5 | 380.8 |

V comes closest, at 61% of the group's figure. Jimin is at 15%. Nobody crosses.

## That is not how the rest of K-pop reads

Of the 23 member-group pairs we could measure, **six lose to their own member in every country
where both can be checked**:

| Member | Act | Countries the member wins | Member ÷ act |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| **Nana** | A.S. Red & Blue | 2 of 2 | 188× |
| **Gong Myung** | 5urprise | 3 of 3 | 6.8× |
| **Mark Lee** | SuperM | 3 of 3 | 6.1× |
| **Kai** | SuperM | 3 of 3 | 3.8× |
| **Lee Hye-ri** | Girl's Day | 4 of 4 | 3.9× |
| **Jay Park** | 2PM | 4 of 4 | 1.4× |

Thirteen pairs lose in every country, seven of them BTS. The rest split.

## The same man, two answers

**Mark Lee** is listed in two acts, and he is the clearest case in the data because the person does
not change — only the act beside him does.

| | Indonesia | Vietnam | Thailand |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Mark Lee** | 48.4 | 6.9 | 31.4 |
| SuperM | 8.0 | 3.2 | 3.2 |
| NCT Dream | 36.6 | 12.8 | 38.4 |

Against **SuperM** he wins all three countries, by six times overall. Against **NCT Dream** he wins
Indonesia and loses Vietnam and Thailand. Same reads, same year, same four encyclopaedias.

So "is the member bigger than the group?" has no general answer. It is a question about a
particular act, and the number moves by country.

## What this does not say

It does not say BTS is more popular than its members, or that Nana is 188 times anything. It says
that when a reader in these four countries went looking, the page they opened was more often the
group's for BTS and more often the member's for SuperM.

Reading an encyclopaedia article is a floor on interest, not a measure of it. Readers in Southeast
Asia also use the English Wikipedia, which cannot be split by country, so every figure here is
smaller than the real attention.
