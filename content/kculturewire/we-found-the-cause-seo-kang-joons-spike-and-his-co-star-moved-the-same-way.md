Last week we published a piece we titled honestly: Seo Kang-joon's English Wikipedia reading had
risen 2.59x in a week, and we said we could not find a news event that explained it. We were
wrong to say we could not find one — we had not looked for the right one.

A new KBS2 weekend drama, *You, Romance Other Than You* (너 말고 다른 연애), premiered on
12 September 2026. Seo Kang-joon plays one of the two leads. His single highest reading day in
our full 30-day window was 13 September — the day after the premiere.

## The check we didn't have last week: a co-star moved too

His co-star, Ahn Eun-jin, plays the other lead. Her reading rose as well, peaking on
14 September — two days after the same premiere.

| | 21-27 Aug (before), views/day | 13-19 Sep (after), views/day | Rise |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Seo Kang-joon | 772 | 4,821 | **6.24x** |
| Ahn Eun-jin | 907 | 3,872 | **4.27x** |

Both actors' 30-day-window peak-view day falls in the two days immediately after the same
premiere date. A single actor's reading rising after a drama airs could be coincidence, a
different news item, or noise. Two credited co-stars in the same new drama, peaking within a day
of each other and of the premiere, is a harder coincidence to construct.

## What this means for how we report "unexplained" rises

Last week's piece marked the cause as "excluded" — something we looked for and didn't find,
not something we ruled out. That distinction held: the cause existed, we simply hadn't searched
for a premiere date instead of a news event. We are not deleting or quietly editing that piece;
we are publishing this one as a follow-up, the same way we handled an earlier case where we
could not check a same-day reaction and later could.

The two actors' rises are not identical in size — 6.24x against 4.27x — which is itself a
finding: shared exposure to the same premiere does not move two people's reading by the same
amount. We are not attaching a reason to that gap; we are reporting that it exists.

## Method

We took a 30-day window (21 August-19 September 2026) of daily English Wikipedia pageviews for
both actors from our K-pop and actor panels, compared the first 7 days against the last 7, and
checked each actor's single highest-viewed day against the drama's public premiere date. Not
investment advice; not a viewership-rating claim.
