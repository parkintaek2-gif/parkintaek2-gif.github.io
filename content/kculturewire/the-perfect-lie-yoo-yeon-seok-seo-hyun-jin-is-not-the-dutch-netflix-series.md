If you search for ***The Perfect Lie*** this week you will find two different series.

- **MBC's *The Perfect Lie*** is a Korean psychological thriller starring **Yoo Yeon-seok** and **Seo Hyun-jin**. It premieres on **30 October 2026** at 9:50 p.m. Korea time ([Soompi](https://www.soompi.com/article/1872302wpp/watch-yoo-yeon-seok-and-seo-hyun-jins-upcoming-drama-the-perfect-lie-confirms-premiere-date-with-glimpse-of-script-reading)).
- **Netflix's *The Perfect Lie*** is a **Dutch** series, *De Eetclub* in the original, based on Saskia Noort's novel. It launched worldwide on 10 September 2026 ([What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/the-perfect-lie-netflix-release-date-first-look/)).

The Netflix one is the hit. On Netflix's weekly lists it reached **79 of 93 countries** and was number 1 on the global non-English TV list in its first two weeks. A Korean drama that has not aired yet cannot have done that.

## What this does and does not say

Chart positions are not audiences. These lists cover only what charted from July 2021, and a title appears here only when the cast lists we use name the actor. None of this predicts how MBC's *The Perfect Lie* will do; it has not aired.

If a page credits the Dutch series' numbers to Yoo Yeon-seok or Seo Hyun-jin, that page has matched by title alone. The same name is not the same show.
