**T1** are one win from the quarter-finals of the 2026 World Championship. They beat **CTBC Flying Oyster** in a single game at 02:00 Korea time on 27 October and moved to **2-1** in the Swiss Stage. Their only loss so far was to Dplus Kia.

| Kills | **14** | 6 |

| Dragons | 1 | **2** |
| Barons | **1** | 0 |

T1 played Doran, Oner, Faker, Peyz and Keria. CTBC Flying Oyster drop to 1-2.

## How 2-1 teams have done

From 2-1, a team plays a best-of-three: win it and it is through. Lose, and it has one more best-of-three at 2-2. We put every Swiss Stage match of 2024 and 2025 in order from Riot's schedule and followed every team that reached 2-1.

| Year | Teams at 2-1 | Qualified | Went out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 6 | 4: Top Esports, Hanwha Life, FlyQuest, **T1 (won the title)** | Dplus Kia, G2 |
| 2025 | 6 | 5: Hanwha Life, Gen.G, G2, CTBC Flying Oyster, Top Esports | FlyQuest |
| Both | **12** | **9** | 3 |

Read the nine with care. Six 2-1 teams meet each other in three matches, so exactly three go through at once in every year; that half is built into the format. The real test is the other half: of the six teams that lost at 2-1 and fell to 2-2, **three** recovered and three went out.

Compare yesterday's count for teams at 1-2: three of twelve got through ([Hanwha Life at 1-2](/article/hanwha-life-fall-to-1-2-at-worlds-2026-three-of-twelve-came-back)).

## T1's own two years

In 2024 T1 were at 2-1, won their next series, and went on to win the title. In 2025 they were at 1-2 and still won it. Neither year says what happens in this one.

## Who T1 could meet

Six teams are at 2-1 after round three: **T1, Invictus Gaming, Top Esports, Bilibili Gaming, Movistar KOI and LYON**. Riot's schedule lists nine best-of-three matches, all still "TBD", from 29 October to 1 November (KST) for rounds four and five; the pairings had not been published when we wrote. Gen.G and Dplus Kia are already through at 3-0 ([the order they qualified in](/article/gen-g-beat-lyon-2-1-join-dplus-kia-worlds-2026-quarter-finals-lck-first-two)).
