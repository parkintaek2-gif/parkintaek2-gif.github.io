On 12 August 2026, BTS members RM and J-Hope appeared backstage at a Chris Brown and Usher
concert in Toronto, posing for photos with Brown and posting videos of themselves singing along.
Thirteen days later, Filipino Broadway star Lea Salonga — Tony winner for *Miss Saigon*, the
singing voice of Disney's Jasmine and Mulan — explained on Instagram why she'd unfollowed both
men: "I recognized that I was waking up sad and/or upset, or find myself way too affected. This
was unhealthy so I took a step back, for me," she wrote, adding she wasn't trying to punish
either one and remained grateful for what BTS had meant to her. Philippine outlets including
PEP.ph, the Daily Tribune, GMA and PhilStar covered the story the same day.

It's a rare crossover: a Southeast Asian entertainment icon and two K-pop stars, in the same news
cycle. We hold English Wikipedia pageviews for all three, so we checked whether either date —
the concert or the statement — actually moved anyone's reading.

## What we measured

We pulled daily English Wikipedia pageviews for RM, J-Hope and Lea Salonga from 13 July through
16 September 2026, and set the two dates that made this a story — 12 August and 25 August —
against each person's 30-day average in the month before the concert.

| | 30-day baseline | 12 Aug (concert) | vs. avg | 25 Aug (statement) | vs. avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RM | 90.5/day | 93 | 1.03x | 70 | 0.77x |
| J-Hope | 2,296.3/day | 1,923 | 0.84x | 1,634 | 0.71x |
| Lea Salonga | 1,288.3/day | 1,188 | 0.92x | 3,112 | 2.42x |

RM and J-Hope's reading didn't move on either date — both sat inside, or below, their normal
range. Salonga's reading on the statement date reads elevated at 2.42x her baseline. But that
number is misleading on its own: it's the tail end of a decline that had already been running for
eight days.

## The bigger story is a different two days

Salonga's single highest day in our window wasn't 25 August. It was **17 August — 10,368 views,
8.05x her baseline** — five days after the concert and eight days before her statement. We
looked for a news event tying that date to BTS, the concert, or the unfollow story, and did not
find one. We're reporting the number and saying plainly: we don't know what caused it.

Her second, larger spike came later and has a confirmed, unrelated cause. On **4 September
2026, Salonga became the first Filipina honored with a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame** —
covered by Rolling Stone, ABC News and Philippine outlets alike. Her reading hit **12,714 views
the next day, 9.87x her baseline** — higher than anything tied to the BTS story.

## What this does and doesn't tell us

We found this story through a real search signal — a "Rising" related query on Google Trends for
BTS, Philippines, past 30 days — which is how we knew people were searching for it. But English
Wikipedia reading, which we measure directly, tells a narrower story than the search interest or
the news coverage suggests. RM and J-Hope's own pages show no measurable reaction to either date.
Salonga's page moved sharply twice in this period — and both times, for reasons that either
predate her statement or have nothing to do with BTS at all.

That doesn't mean the story didn't matter to the people who read and shared it. It means the one
number we hold — who looked a name up on English Wikipedia, and when — didn't register it the way
the headlines did.
