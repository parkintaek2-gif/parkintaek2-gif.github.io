Two BTS members were in the Korean entertainment headlines this morning: one for a
131-week run at the top of a fan vote, the other for a 22nd number one on a global song
chart. Both stories are about where the audience is. So we went to our own measurement of
exactly that, and it disagrees with the usual answer.

Across our whole panel — **478 Korean entertainers, 15 language editions, June to August
2026** — the edition that reads them most, with English set aside, is **Chinese for 306 of
them**. For the seven members of BTS, Chinese leads for **none**.

## Which edition reads each member most, English set aside

| Member | Top edition | Reads | Second | Reads | English |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Jungkook | Spanish | 86,913 | Japanese | 74,910 | 340,956 |
| V | Spanish | 83,578 | Japanese | 77,657 | 354,780 |
| Jimin | **Japanese** | 43,832 | Spanish | 39,163 | 209,359 |
| Jin | Spanish | 33,302 | Japanese | 31,760 | 199,368 |
| RM | Spanish | 33,159 | German | 23,765 | 194,021 |
| Suga | Spanish | 35,269 | Japanese | 25,311 | 189,302 |
| J-Hope | Spanish | 28,856 | German | 23,035 | 161,693 |

## The concentration is the finding

Spanish leads for only **15 of the 478 people** in our panel — 3.1%. **Six of those 15 are
BTS members.** A pattern that is nearly absent across Korean entertainment as a whole is the
normal case inside this one group.

That is a stronger statement than "BTS is big in Latin America," and it is stronger because
it is comparative. The same 15 editions, the same three months and the same method give
Chinese for two-thirds of Korean stars and Spanish for six of these seven.

## Jimin's margin, printed as a margin

Jimin is the exception, and the exception is narrow: **Japanese 43,832 against Spanish
39,163**, a gap of **4,669 reads** over three months. On a different window it could go the
other way.

We are publishing the gap rather than only the winner, because "Japanese leads for Jimin" is
a sentence that would survive being wrong, and the number underneath it would not. Anyone
who wants to quote this should quote the margin with it.

Jimin is also the member whose third edition is Russian (32,212) rather than German or
Chinese — the only one of the seven for whom that is true. We have no explanation and offer
none.

## What we are not saying

**Not that Chinese readers ignore BTS.** Jungkook's Chinese reads are 43,772 and V's 33,781
— substantial numbers. The claim is only about which edition is *highest*, and rank is a
different thing from volume.

**Not that a language is a country.** Chinese Wikipedia is blocked in mainland China, so its
readers are in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia and the diaspora. Spanish spans Spain
and Latin America. Every row above is a language edition.

**Not why.** Fan organisation in Latin America, the editing culture of Spanish Wikipedia and
years of promotion decisions would all produce this shape. Pageview counts cannot tell them
apart, and we are not going to pick one and call it a finding.

**Not the fan-vote or chart claims.** Those are why we looked today. We did not check them,
and no number in this article comes from them.
