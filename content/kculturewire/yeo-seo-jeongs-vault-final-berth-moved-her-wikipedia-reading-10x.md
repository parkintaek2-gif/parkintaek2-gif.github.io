Yeo Seo-jeong averaged 7.8 English-language Wikipedia reads a day for the ten days from 10 to 19 September. On 22 September — the day she topped vault qualifying at the 2026 Asian Games and reached her first Asian Games final in eight years — that reached 78 reads, 10.0 times her baseline.

## What happened

Newspim reported that Yeo, competing at her first Asian Games since winning vault gold at the 2018 Jakarta-Palembang Games, posted a two-vault average of 14.466 at the Aichi-Nagoya Games qualifying round in Nagoya, Japan, finishing first ahead of North Korea's Ahn Chang-ok (13.900). The result put her into the vault final for the first time in eight years.

## Why the timing points at qualifying

Reading sat close to baseline through 19 September, ticked up to 18 on 21 September — plausibly early Games-adjacent interest — and then jumped to 78 the day qualifying results were reported. The size of the jump on the exact reporting day is consistent with the qualifying result driving it, not a slower build.
