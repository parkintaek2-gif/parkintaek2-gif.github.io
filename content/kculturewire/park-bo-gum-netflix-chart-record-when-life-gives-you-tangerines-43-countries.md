**Park Bo-gum** returns in November with the second season of tvN's variety series *The Village Barber*, now with Ko Kyung-pyo, his co-star from *Reply 1988* ([Soompi](https://www.soompi.com/article/1854200wpp/the-village-barber-season-2-confirms-premiere-plans-and-cast-park-bo-gum-lee-sang-yi-and-ko-kyung-pyo)). His drama record on Netflix is lopsided: one title carries almost all of his reach.

Netflix publishes a weekly top 10 for each of 93 countries. We matched every title Park Bo-gum appears in, as listed in his English Wikipedia filmography, against every list since July 2021.

## Lead roles

| Title | Type | Countries | Weeks on any chart | Best position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **When Life Gives You Tangerines** | Series | **43** | 21 | 1 |
| Encounter | Series | 16 | 10 | 2 |
| Wonderland | Film | 9 | 8 | 2 |
| Reply 1988 | Series | 5 | **39** | 3 |
| Seobok | Film | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Love in the Moonlight | Series | 1 | 2 | 6 |

*When Life Gives You Tangerines* reached 43 countries, 42 of them in the same week. Nothing else of his passed 16.

*Reply 1988* is the opposite shape. It never went beyond five countries, but it stayed 39 weeks, longer than any other title of his. *Encounter*, with Song Hye-kyo, charted in 16 countries, mostly in Asia and the Gulf.

Chart positions are not audiences, and these lists start in July 2021. A title is matched by its exact name on Netflix's lists; where a different title shares the name, we left it out and said so above.
