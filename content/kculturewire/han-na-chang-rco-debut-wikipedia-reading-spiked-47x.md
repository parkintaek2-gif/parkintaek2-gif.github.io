Han-na Chang, the cellist who won the Rostropovich International Cello Competition at age 11 and
has conducted full-time since 2007, averaged 25.3 English Wikipedia reads a day in the nine days
before 11 September, 2026. Two days after she conducted the Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra's
season-opening concert in Amsterdam, her reading hit 1,186 &mdash; 46.9 times that baseline, the
largest multiple we have recorded in this series.

## What happened

Korean press reported that Chang led the Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra's outdoor "Opening Night
2026" at Oosterpark in Amsterdam on 11 September &mdash; her first collaboration with the
orchestra, one of the ensembles regularly ranked among the world's best. Reports describe a
standing ovation from roughly 2,000 attendees after the closing movement.

## Why we're not claiming a single, dated cause for the peak day

The concert itself is well documented and lines up with the first jump, on 11 September. What we
could not pin down is why the reading kept climbing and peaked two days later rather than on the
concert date or the day right after, which is the pattern we usually see in this series (a same-day
or next-day peak, as with a drama cameo or a stage fall we've covered previously). We looked for a
13 September article specifically and did not find one. Rather than invent a tidier story, we are
reporting the concert as the clear trigger and the exact shape of the two-day climb as unexplained.
