The Korea Corporate Reputation Institute's monthly brand reputation ranking for September 2026 put girl group Re:Scene first among all K-pop groups — ahead of BTS in second place and BLACKPINK in third. Re:Scene's individual members also took the top spots in the institute's rookie-idol ranking, sweeping the top five places, according to STARNEWS.

We don't reproduce that index's score here — it's a third-party measurement built from Korean-language media and community mentions, and we don't redistribute numbers we haven't verified ourselves. What we can measure is how often people looked Re:Scene up on English Wikipedia over the same period.

## What we measured

We pulled daily English Wikipedia pageviews for Re:Scene and, as a reference point, for BTS — the group the reputation index placed just behind them — over the 29 days ending 17 September 2026.

| | 29-day average | Highest day | Lowest day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Re:Scene | **11** | 19 | 4 |
| BTS | 6,860 | 10,382 | 4,923 |

Re:Scene's English Wikipedia readership averaged 11 views a day across the entire window. BTS, the group the reputation index ranked one place below Re:Scene, averaged 6,860 views a day — 624x that.

## What this does and doesn't show

A reputation index built for a Korean audience, from Korean-language sources, can be a real and accurate read of who a domestic audience is talking about most right now — a debut-era rookie group riding a wave of local media coverage can genuinely outscore a group with over a decade of global reach on that specific measure. That's not a contradiction with our numbers; it's a different question answered by a different instrument, pointed at a different readership.

What our count says is narrower: as of mid-September 2026, Re:Scene has not yet become a name that English-speaking readers look up in meaningful numbers. Whether that changes is not something a 29-day window can tell us.
