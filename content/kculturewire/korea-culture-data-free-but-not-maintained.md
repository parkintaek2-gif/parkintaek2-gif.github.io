Korea publishes culture data — tourism flows, leisure spending, the culture industry, overseas Hallyu
audiences — on a public platform. There are **1,344 datasets** on it, and most of them are free.

That is the sentence you will find if anyone describes it at all. Here is the one that goes with it.

## What it costs

| | | |
|---|---:|---:|
| Free | 1,075 | 80% |
| Paid | 239 | 17.8% |
| Price on request | 30 | 2.2% |

## When it was last touched

| | | |
|---|---:|---:|
| Updated within twelve months | 322 | 24% |
| **Not updated in twelve months** | **1,022** | **76%** |
| Update date unreadable | 0 | 0% |

Every listing carried a readable date, so none of this is guesswork about age.

**Free and live are not the same thing.** Four datasets in five cost nothing; three in four have not moved
in a year. Printing the first figure without the second is how a catalogue gets described as a resource.

## The platform says so itself

| Stated update cycle | Datasets |
|---|---:|
| Cannot be updated (갱신불가) | 590 |
| Yearly | 273 |
| No fixed cycle (비주기) | 257 |
| Monthly | 110 |
| Quarterly | 82 |
| Half-yearly | 21 |

**590 datasets — 43.9% — are labelled as unable to be updated at all.** That is often the honest label
for a one-off survey rather than a failing, and we read it that way. It does mean nobody should reach
here for a question about this month.

## What they are about

Tourism 436 · leisure 338 · culture industry 318 · travel 266 · sport 244 · arts 232 · local 207 ·
culture consumption 191. One dataset can carry several tags.

Almost all of it is CSV — 1,299 of 1,344 — with 263 also offering JSON.

## The one that matters to readers of this site

The oldest listings have been sitting still for six years. Among them:

| Dataset | Last updated | Months ago |
|---|---|---:|
| 지역별 청소년활동 관심지도 | 2020-06-22 | 74.5 |
| 베트남의 한류(K-POP) 주제 한류 커뮤니티 키워드 데이터 | 2020-07-01 | 74.2 |
| 케이스타라이브의 국가별 월별 순 방문자 데이터 | 2020-07-01 | 74.2 |

The second one is a Korean dataset about **Vietnamese K-pop community keywords**, and it stopped in
July 2020. Anyone reaching for official Korean data on overseas Hallyu audiences will find a shelf that
ends before the last several years of it — which is a large part of why we measure that audience
ourselves, from Wikipedia, one language at a time.

## What this is not

It is not a criticism of any dataset. We count what the listing says about each one, not what is inside
it, and a survey of 2019 is supposed to stop in 2019. It is also one shelf, not the whole library —
Korean data exists in many other places.
