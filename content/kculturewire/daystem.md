**IU** and **Jimin** were born on the same day stem. **RM** and **Song Joong-ki** share a
different one. **Jennie**, **Psy** and **Lee Min-ho** share a third.

That is a fact about dates, and it is the whole of what we are claiming.

## What we counted

Every Korean actor, singer and songwriter we hold a full date of birth for — **9,249 people** —
was placed in one of the ten day stems of the sexagenary cycle. The stem comes from the date
alone. It needs no hour, no place and no interpretation.

The names inside each stem are ordered by how often their English Wikipedia article was opened,
not by anything about the stem. Each stem has [its own page](/day-pillar) with every name in it.

## The number that argues against us

We expected the ten cells to come out flat, and to be able to say the spread was
indistinguishable from chance. That is not what we got.

**Chi-square 17.9, against the usual 16.92 threshold at nine degrees of freedom.** It crosses.
Read the way that statistic is normally read, it says *not chance*.

**We do not believe it means anything about the people**, and we are not going to pretend the
number says something softer than it does. Here is why we distrust our own figure.

- **An even split was never the right expectation.** Day stems run in a ten-day cycle. Calendars
  do not divide into ten, birth dates cluster by season and by year, and a decade of Korean
  entertainment debuts is not a random draw across the calendar.
- **9,249 is a large sample.** With numbers this size, a gap of 984 against 832 — about 18% —
  is enough to cross a threshold that was never built for this question.
- **Who gets a full birth date is not random.** Wikidata holds a complete date for some people
  and only a year for others. The ones with complete dates skew towards those who have been
  documented longest. That alone can bend a spread.

Any one of those is a better explanation than the day someone was born deciding anything.

## What this is not

This is a count of birth dates. It is not a reading of anyone, and it is not a horoscope.
A day stem is one of four pillars, and the other three need the year, month and **hour** of
birth. We do not hold hours, and we would not publish a reading from them if we did.

**This is statistics, not you.**

## One more honest note

Before building these pages we measured what people actually search for. The phrase
*"byeong day stem"* returned **zero** autocomplete suggestions. Star names return ten.
So we do not expect this page to be found by anyone typing the words in its title, and we are
not going to claim otherwise. It exists because the ten stem pages are a real index of
9,249 people, and because the honest version of a number that crosses a threshold is worth
writing down.
