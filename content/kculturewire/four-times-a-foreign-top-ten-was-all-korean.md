A Netflix country chart has ten places. In **50,034** country-week charts — every country Netflix
publishes, every week since July 2021, both the film and the series list — Korean titles have held
**all ten** of them **8 times**.

Four of those eight were Korea's own chart, which is the ordinary half of the finding. The other
four were not.

## The four

| Country | Week beginning | Chart | Different shows in the ten places |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Vietnam | 15 August 2021 | Series | 9 |
| Vietnam | 22 August 2021 | Series | 9 |
| Indonesia | 31 July 2022 | Series | 10 |
| Malaysia | 31 July 2022 | Series | 10 |

Indonesia and Malaysia did it in **the same week**, with the same shows at the top of both:
*Alchemy of Souls*, *Extraordinary Attorney Woo*, *Café Minamdang*, *My Roommate Is a Gumiho*,
*Ghost Doctor*, *Police University*, *Remarriage & Desires*, *Sell Your Haunted House*,
*Backstreet Rookie* and *Dali & Cocky Prince*.

Vietnam's two weeks were a year earlier and a different kind of list — *Crash Landing on You*,
*Reply 1988*, *Hospital Playlist*, *Vincenzo*, *Prison Playbook*, *Descendants of the Sun*,
*Vagabond*, *Lawless Lawyer* and *Nevertheless,*. Nine different shows in ten places, because
*Hospital Playlist* was there twice, as two seasons.

## Eight is too few to name a country. So we counted the near misses

Eight cells cannot tell you which countries this happens in — with numbers that small, one more
week either way changes the list. So we counted the charts that came **within one place**: nine of
the ten held by Korean titles. There are **43** of those.

Outside Korea, they land in four countries.

| Country | All ten places | Nine of ten |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Vietnam | 2 | 10 |
| Indonesia | 1 | 6 |
| Malaysia | 1 | 1 |
| Singapore | 0 | 1 |

**Every one of them is in Southeast Asia.** No European, North American, Latin American or African
chart has ever come within one place of a fully Korean top 10, in any of the 50,034 charts we read.

## Why this is not the same as "Korea's share is high"

Korea's share of chart places is highest in Korea itself (53.6%), then Vietnam (30.2%), Indonesia
(28.9%), Malaysia (25.1%) and Taiwan (24.2%). If a full chart were just a high share turning up, the
list above would follow that order and include Taiwan.

It does not, and the reason is a separate measurement we already publish: Korean titles do not
arrive in a country's chart one at a time. [They arrive together](/clumping). Ranked by how much
more clumped a market is than chance allows, the top four are Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia and the
Philippines — not the four with the largest shares. A market can take a lot of Korean content
steadily, or take it in bursts, and only the bursts produce a week like these.

Taiwan has the fourth-largest Korean share of any market and has never had nine of ten. Malaysia has
the fifth-largest and has had both.

## What we are not saying

We are not saying more people in Malaysia watched Korean shows those weeks than in Taiwan. **Netflix
publishes the position, not the audience**, and a chart place is a rank. Ten Korean titles in the
top ten of a small market and ten in a large one are the same ten places in this data.

We are also not saying anything about why the bursts happen. A single release week, a school holiday,
a platform promotion and a slow week for everything else would all produce the same chart. We can
say when it happened, in which country, and with which shows. The reason is not in the file.

[the all-ten page](/all-ten), with the row counts printed beside them.
