Star News publishes the running results of its STARPOLL fan vote. In the male idol category, the same name led every time we looked from mid-September: **Sunghyun of Cortis**. On 24 October that changed. His bandmate **Keonho** is now first.

## One day, two members

| | 23 October | 24 October | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keonho (Cortis) | 9,859 | **34,375** | **+24,516** |
| Sunghyun (Cortis) | 15,418 | 25,487 | +10,069 |
| Jin (BTS) | 2,520 | 6,166 | +3,646 |

In a single day Keonho's fans added more than twice as many votes as Sunghyun's and moved him from second to first. It is the only change of leader in any STARPOLL category across the six snapshots we have taken since 16 September.

## Outside the vote, interest in Cortis is falling

In the fan vote, Cortis members hold the top two male idol places. On English Wikipedia, where readers outside Korea look up a name, reading of the Cortis article has fallen month by month:

| Month | Cortis article, median daily reads |
|---|---|
| August | 2,328 |
| September | 1,724 |
| October (to the 22nd) | **1,394** |

That is a fall of about 40% since August. Neither Keonho nor Sunghyun has an English Wikipedia article of his own under that name, so the group page is the only English entry for either.

## What this does and does not say

A fan vote and a reading count measure different things. [We found the same split for trot singers](/article/korea-fan-vote-king-is-trot-english-wikipedia-barely-reads-them): the names Korea's fans vote for hardest are often the ones least looked up in English. STARPOLL votes show how hard a fandom is pushing this week, not how many people follow an artist.
