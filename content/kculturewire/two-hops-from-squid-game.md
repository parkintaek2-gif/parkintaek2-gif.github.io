*Squid Game* charted in 94 countries, more than any other Korean title we track. Start from it and
walk outwards — step to every title that shares a credited actor, then to every title those share an
actor with — and the Korean catalogue closes up fast.

| Hops from *Squid Game* | Titles |
| --- | ---: |
| 0 (itself) | 1 |
| 1 | 69 |
| 2 | 432 |
| 3 | 112 |
| 4 | 4 |
| never reached | 17 |

**502 of the 635 titles — 79.1% — are within two hops.** Take one more step and you have 614, or
96.7%. Four steps exhausts everything the walk can reach.

## What this does and does not mean

It does **not** mean these works resemble each other. A hop is one shared credited actor. A character
actor with a scene in a 2013 thriller and a scene in a 2024 romance joins them here, and nothing
about the two productions is alike.

What it does mean is that the Korean output Netflix has charted is drawn from **one pool of people,
not several**. There is no cluster of films that recruits from a separate population, no television
world sealed off from cinema. If there were, the walk would stall — and it does not stall until it
has covered almost everything.

We tested the obvious alternative explanation, that a handful of very prolific actors are carrying
the whole thing, and it did not survive: removing the hundred busiest people leaves 94.2% of titles
still connected as one body. The closeness is produced by the many, not the few.

## The seventeen it never reaches

Seventeen titles share no credited actor with anything else in the set, so no walk from anywhere
arrives at them. They are not far away; they are disconnected, which is a different fact.

Some are genuinely standalone. Some are titles where Wikidata records one or two cast members and
those people appear nowhere else — which makes them look isolated when the entry, not the production,
is what is thin. We cannot separate the two cases from this data, so we report the seventeen and
say why the number should not be read as a count of self-contained productions.

## The bigger absence

**266 Korean titles have no cast recorded in Wikidata at all.** They are not at distance infinity;
they are outside the walk entirely. Every figure above describes the 635 that could be walked, and
the true structure of the whole 906 is something this data cannot show.

If anything, the direction of that error is knowable: adding cast can only create hops, never remove
them. The catalogue is at least this connected, and probably more.
