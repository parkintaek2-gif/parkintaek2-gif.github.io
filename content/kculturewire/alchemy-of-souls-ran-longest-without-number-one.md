Netflix has published a global weekly top 10 for **268 weeks**. In that window **237 Korean titles**
entered it, together drawing **24.0 billion hours**.

Rank those titles by hours and look at the top ten. **Nine of them reached number one.** The
exception charted longer than eight of the nine.

| Title | Weeks on the chart | Peak | Hours |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Squid Game | 42 | **1** | 5.05bn |
| Extraordinary Attorney Woo | 21 | **1** | 0.66bn |
| All of Us Are Dead | 11 | **1** | 0.66bn |
| Queen of Tears | 15 | **1** | 0.65bn |
| The Glory | 13 | **1** | 0.56bn |
| Bon Appétit, Your Majesty | 10 | **1** | 0.51bn |
| When Life Gives You Tangerines | 9 | **1** | 0.48bn |
| King the Land | 11 | **1** | 0.45bn |
| **Alchemy of Souls** | **27** | **3** | 0.42bn |
| Agent Kim Reactivated | 8 | **1** | 0.37bn |

**Alchemy of Souls** is second only to Squid Game in weeks charted, and it never took the top place.
**Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha**, just outside this table, lasted 16 weeks and peaked at two.

## Two different things get called "biggest"

A peak of one is a fact about a single week. Twenty-seven weeks is a fact about five months.

**All of Us Are Dead** and **Alchemy of Souls** are within 60% of each other on hours — 0.66bn
against 0.42bn — and the first did it in **11 weeks** while the second took **27**. Per week on the
chart, All of Us Are Dead drew 60 million hours and Alchemy of Souls drew 16 million.

Neither number is wrong. They answer different questions, and a headline that says "biggest Korean
drama" almost never says which one it means.

## One title is a fifth of everything

The gap at the top is not a gradient. **Squid Game drew 7.6 times the hours of the title ranked
second** and holds 21% of all 24.0 billion hours by itself. The other 236 titles share the rest.

The top 50 titles together account for **72.5%** of the hours — and **49 of those 50 are series**.
One film makes the list.

## What this cannot tell you

Netflix publishes hours only for the weeks a title was inside the global top 10. Everything watched
before a title entered, or after it fell out, is invisible here. Every figure in this piece is a
floor on how much a title was watched, not a total.
