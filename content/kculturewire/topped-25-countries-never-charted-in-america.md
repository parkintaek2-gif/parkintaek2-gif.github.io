Two Korean dramas were reported this week in the same words. *Teach You a Lesson* "tops Netflix's
non-English chart for a second week." *Agent Kim Reactivated* "tops Netflix non-English chart for
a third week." Both sentences describe **one worldwide list.** What nobody reports is the part a
viewer actually sees: their own country's chart.

We hold those. Ninety-three country tables, weekly, back to 2021. Counting country by country,
"topped the chart" turns out to mean anything from **31% to 82%.**

## Number one is not one number

| Title | Countries that carried it | Reached No.1 | Share | Carried but never No.1 | Weeks in our tables |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| KPop Demon Hunters | 93 | **76** | 81.7% | 17 | 62 |
| Teach You a Lesson | 91 | **46** | 50.5% | 45 | 12 |
| Our Sticky Love | 68 | **27** | 39.7% | 41 | 3 |
| **Agent Kim Reactivated** | 81 | **25** | **30.9%** | 56 | 9 |

*Agent Kim Reactivated* was on 81 country charts and reached the top of **25** of them. In 56
countries it was carried and never got there. The headline number and this one are not the same
measurement, and the gap is not small.

## Neither drama reached number one in America

This is the part the single number hides completely.

| | United States | United Kingdom | Japan | Brazil | India | France | Germany |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teach You a Lesson | 9th | 9th | **1st** | **1st** | **1st** | 2nd | 3rd |
| Agent Kim Reactivated | **never charted** | **never charted** | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 5th |

*Agent Kim Reactivated* does not appear in the US or UK tables at all — not at rank ten, not once,
in any week we hold. Its 25 number-ones are elsewhere. *Teach You a Lesson* did reach American and
British charts and stalled at **ninth** in both, while topping Japan, Brazil and India.

**Both were "number one." Neither was number one where the largest English-language audiences
are.** That is a different story from the one the wire copy tells, and it is in the same tables the
wire copy is drawn from.

## Home is not a signal

All four titles reached number one in **Korea**. Every one of them. So a Korean drama topping the
Korean chart tells you nothing about whether it will top anyone else's — the four then spread from
31% to 82% abroad.

| Title | Peak rank in Korea | Weeks in Korea |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| KPop Demon Hunters | 1 | 32 |
| Teach You a Lesson | 1 | 9 |
| Our Sticky Love | 1 | 3 |
| Agent Kim Reactivated | 1 | 8 |

## What this does not say

It does not say *Agent Kim Reactivated* was watched less than *Teach You a Lesson*. Rank measures a
position against whatever else is available in that country that week, not an audience. A title can
sit at ninth in a huge market and be seen by more people than a number one in a small one, and
nothing here separates those.

It also does not say the wire copy is wrong. "Tops the non-English chart" is a claim about a
worldwide list we do not hold, and it may be perfectly accurate. **Our point is narrower: that
sentence is compatible with never charting in America, and readers cannot tell from it.**

Our tables end **2026-08-23**. Anything after that is unmeasured, and we will not write it down as
a zero. Two titles in this week's news — *Perfect Crown* and *Mousetrap* — are absent from our
tables entirely, so we say nothing about them.
