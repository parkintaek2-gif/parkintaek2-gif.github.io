Singer Song So-hee released a new single, "NANSEN," on 13 September 2026. Her English
Wikipedia reading peaked the very next day — 573 views on 14 September, against a 30-day
average of 93 views a day.

## Before and after the release

| | Views/day |
|---|---:|
| First 7 days of our window (21-27 Aug) | 59 |
| Last 7 days (13-19 Sep, release week) | **176 (3.0x)** |
| Single highest day (14 Sep) | 573 |

A 3.0x week-over-week rise is a real, measurable move, not the panel's largest this month —
several K-pop names we track rose further off a larger base. What makes this one worth
reporting on its own is the timing: her highest single day in a 30-day window lands on the
calendar day immediately after a dated, sourced release, with no other announced event in the
same window that we found.

## What we are not claiming

We do not have streaming numbers, chart position, or sales figures for "NANSEN," and we are not
reporting on how well the single performed commercially. English Wikipedia reading is one
narrow measure of English-language reader interest; it does not tell us whether Korean-language
audiences, streaming platforms, or sales moved at all.

## Method

We compared the first 7 days of a 30-day window (21 August-19 September 2026) against the last
7 days for a 2,371-person K-pop reading panel, and checked whether the single highest-viewed
day for this name fell on or after a dated public release. Not investment advice.
