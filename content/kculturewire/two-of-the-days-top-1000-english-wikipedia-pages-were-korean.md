On 30 August 2026, English Wikipedia's daily list of its 1,000 most-read articles included two Korean titles.

## What we found

| Rank | Title | Reads that day | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 186 | Mousetrap (TV series) | 21,033 | Netflix's #1 global non-English show that week |
| 340 | Four Hands, Two Sonatas | 15,420 | Same drama as our separate Yekwon Sunwoo cameo spike piece |

Of the roughly 1,009 daily-top-1,000 entries we could resolve to a Wikidata item (992 matched, 17 unresolved), these were the only two we could identify as Korean film or television titles.

## Why one day, and why it matters anyway

We are not claiming this is a typical day — we have only run this specific check once, on the day we happened to pull the full top-1,000 list, and a title's global Netflix chart run does not map neatly onto any single day's Wikipedia ranking. What the snapshot does show is scale: even a Netflix #1 global show (Mousetrap) sat at rank 186 of the world's most-read English Wikipedia pages that day, not near the top. Attention that looks dominant on one chart can still be a small slice of English Wikipedia's much larger, much broader daily reading list.
