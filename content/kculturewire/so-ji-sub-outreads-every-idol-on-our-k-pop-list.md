Ask who the most-read Korean music figure is and the honest answer, on our own numbers, is
awkward: **So Ji-sub**, with **156,817** article opens in thirty days. That is more than
**Jennie** (119,891), more than **V** (118,449), more than **Jung Kook** (103,735).

So Ji-sub is known to most people as an actor.

## He is on the list for a reason, and the reason is the problem

Our K-pop panel is not hand-picked. It is everybody Wikidata records as a Korean citizen with an
occupation of singer, rapper, composer or musician — 2,372 names in all. So Ji-sub is in it
because that field records a music occupation for him. We did not add him, and we are not going to
quietly remove him: **the moment we start deciding by hand who counts as K-pop, the number stops
being a measurement and starts being an opinion.**

But leaving him in has a consequence worth putting a number on.

## 9.8% of the names, 29.3% of the attention

| | Names | Share of names | Share of reads |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Also recorded as actors | 232 | 9.8% | **29.3%** |
| Music occupations only | 2,140 | 90.2% | 70.7% |

Nearly **a third of everything read on a K-pop list is being read about people who also act**, and
they are one name in ten. Four of the ten most-read individuals — So Ji-sub, Steven Yeun, Jisoo,
Cha Eun-woo — carry an acting credit in the same record.

## So what is a "most-read K-pop star" ranking measuring?

Three answers, and we are not choosing between them for you.

1. **It is measuring exactly what it says.** These people are recorded as musicians. If a drama
   sends people to an idol's page, the idol was still looked up.
2. **It is measuring drama, wearing a music label.** A quarter of the attention arrives through
   work that is not music, and the ranking never says so.
3. **The question is broken.** In Korean entertainment the categories genuinely overlap — the same
   person releases an album and carries a series — and any list that forces one label will
   misdescribe somebody.

Our own reading is the third, which is why this page exists at all. But the first two are
defensible and we have no measurement that settles it.

## What we did about it

We publish the overlap figure beside the ranking rather than hiding it, and we keep a
music-occupation-only list next to the full one so both are visible. **A ranking that cannot show
you what it swept in is not a ranking you should trust — including ours.**

The tables, both versions, are at
[which K-pop names are looked up most](https://www.kculturewire.com/kpop-attention).

**So: should a K-pop chart count So Ji-sub?** Leave him in and the list is honest about the record
but misleading about the genre. Take him out and somebody is deciding by hand who is really an
idol. We would rather show you both lists than make that call in private.
