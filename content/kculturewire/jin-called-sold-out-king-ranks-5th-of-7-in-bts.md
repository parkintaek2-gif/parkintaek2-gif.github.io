Korean entertainment outlets reported this week that BTS's Jin had been named a "sold-out king" —
a Pony plush toy tied to his name sold out, and he was ranked first on real-time, daily, weekly
and monthly charts wherever those figures are kept. We cannot check that: it sits on a retail
platform's own ranking system, and we do not hold a way into it.

What we can check is something adjacent and already open — how many people go and look each BTS
member up on English Wikipedia, over a full year and in the most recent single month. We publish
that count [every time it's relevant](/article/one-member-outdraws-her-own-group-by-04-percent),
and it happens to answer a version of this week's question: among Jin's own six bandmates, where
does he sit?

## By the most recent month, Jin is 4th of 7

| BTS member | August 2026 lookups |
|---|---|
| V | 118,194 |
| Jungkook | 106,474 |
| Jimin | 68,076 |
| **Jin** | **63,443** |
| RM | 63,019 |
| Suga | 61,419 |
| J-Hope | 52,476 |

Jin edges RM by 424 lookups — inside rounding error for a monthly count — and sits well behind
Jimin, and further still behind Jungkook and V.

## Across the full twelve months, he is 5th of 7

| BTS member | Twelve-month lookups |
|---|---|
| V | 1,350,428 |
| Jungkook | 1,155,142 |
| RM | 711,190 |
| Jimin | 706,366 |
| **Jin** | **670,861** |
| Suga | 664,049 |
| J-Hope | 579,762 |

Over the longer window RM and Jimin both pass him, so Jin's rank drops one place, to fifth. He
stays ahead of Suga and J-Hope in both windows.

## What this does and does not tell you

A Wikipedia pageview and a sold-out merchandise chart are not the same kind of number, and we are
not arguing that one disproves the other — a plush toy can sell out completely while the person
it is shaped like ranks fifth of seven in encyclopaedia lookups; the two facts do not conflict,
because they count different things. What we can say is narrower and, we think, more useful:
whichever chart produced this week's "sold-out king" headline, it is not a chart we can open and
check ourselves. This one is, and on this one, Jin is not first in his own group, in either
window we have.
