**Hanwha Life Esports** are still in the 2026 World Championship. They beat **G2 Esports** 2-0 at 08:00 Korea time on 31 October, an elimination match between two teams at 1-2, and move to **2-2**. G2 leave the Swiss Stage at **1-3**.

Hanwha Life had lost their second and third matches, to Gen.G and Invictus Gaming, after opening with a win over Top Esports. They are the fourth Korean team still standing; Dplus KIA, Gen.G and T1 are already through.

## Bilibili Gaming, again

Hanwha Life's last match of the Swiss Stage is against **Bilibili Gaming** at 08:00 KST on 1 November. The winner goes to the quarter-finals and the loser goes home.

| Year | Hanwha Life's Swiss record | Knocked out by |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 3-1 | **Bilibili Gaming**, 1-3, quarter-final |
| 2025 | 3-1 | Gen.G, 1-3, quarter-final |
| 2026 | 2-2 so far | — |

Bilibili Gaming went on from that 2024 quarter-final to the final. They are at 2-2 this year after losing 1-2 to Top Esports on 30 October.

## Where 1-2 teams have ended up

Twelve teams stood at 1-2 in 2024 and 2025. **Three** reached the quarter-finals, all of them at 3-2: Bilibili Gaming in 2024, who reached the final; Weibo Gaming in 2024, a semi-final; and T1 in 2025, who won the title. The other nine went home. Three teams that came back is a short list, not a trend, and it says nothing about who wins on 1 November.
