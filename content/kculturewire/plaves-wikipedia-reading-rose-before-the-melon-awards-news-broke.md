The 18th Melon Music Awards announced the first five acts on its 2026 lineup on 16 September: Plave, Riize, NCT Wish, Alpha Drive One and Lngshot, according to Soompi. The show runs 14-15 November at Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul.

Plave is a virtual K-pop group — its five members perform as motion-captured 3D characters rather than appearing as themselves. We checked whether the awards-lineup news moved how often English-language readers looked the group up on Wikipedia.

## What we measured

We pulled Plave's daily English Wikipedia pageviews for the 29 days ending 17 September 2026, and split the window into a 22-day baseline (20 August-11 September, minus one excluded day — see below) and the five days spanning the announcement (13-17 September).

| | Average views/day |
|---|---|
| Baseline (20 Aug-11 Sep, 22 days) | 139 |
| 13-17 Sep (announcement window) | **211 (1.52x)** |

Reading was already above baseline on 13 September — 223 views, three days before Soompi's report ran. It stayed elevated through 17 September, the last day our data covers. We didn't find a news item naming Plave on 13 September itself, so we're not attaching the early rise to a specific cause — only noting that it preceded the public announcement rather than followed it.

## What we're not counting

One day in our window, 23 August, spiked to 649 views — nearly five times the 139-view baseline we calculated from the other 22 days. We found no news event that day naming Plave and are excluding it from the baseline above, rather than let one unexplained day move it.
