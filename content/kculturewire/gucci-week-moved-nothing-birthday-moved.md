Korean entertainment pages carried four separate items about BTS's Jin on 6 September. One of them
reported that Gucci's Primavera campaign had made him **the most-searched person in the world**.

We have no way to count Google searches, and repeating a figure we cannot check is not something we do.
But there is one number we can count exactly: how many people opened his English Wikipedia article.

## The method is the bandmates

Measuring one person alone is how ordinary drift gets reported as an event. If everyone in a group is
read less in a given week, nothing happened to the one you were watching.

So each event is measured twice — the person, and the rest of BTS over the same days. What matters is
what is **left over**.

## Jin, the week of the Gucci campaign

| | |
|---|---:|
| Seven days before | 1,958 a day |
| Seven days from 25 August | 1,794 a day |
| His own change | 0.92× |
| Bandmates, same weeks | 0.91× |
| **Left over** | **1.01×** |

His reading fell about 8%. So did everyone else's — RM 0.90, Suga 0.87, J-Hope 0.90, Jimin 0.95,
V 0.91. Once that is accounted for, the campaign week leaves **1.01×**.

Nothing.

## Jung Kook, the week of his birthday

| | |
|---|---:|
| Seven days before | 3,758 a day |
| Seven days from 1 September | 5,043 a day |
| On the day itself | 7,996 |
| His own change | 1.34× |
| Bandmates, same weeks | 0.99× |
| **Left over** | **1.35×** |

He turned 29 on the night BTS opened four shows at SoFi Stadium, and the band sang happy birthday on
stage. On the day, his article was opened 7,996 times against 3,758 a day the week before — **2.1 times**
the ordinary rate — while the rest of the group sat flat at 0.99.

## What the two together say

A global luxury campaign, reported in Korea in the largest possible terms, left no trace we can measure.
A birthday left a clear one.

We are not saying the campaign failed. A sold-out $9,900 jacket and an encyclopedia page open are
different things, and only the second is ours to count. What the pair does show is narrower and worth
holding on to: **a superlative in a headline and a measurable rise are not the same object, and only one
of them can be checked.**

[Both events, with every control figure and the ones we dropped, are here](/what-moves-english-reading).

## One thing we had to fix mid-count

Our first pass put Jung Kook in Jin's control group. His birthday falls inside Jin's window, so his 1.34×
dragged the control average up to 0.98 and made Jin look like he had fallen *behind* his bandmates. He
had not. A control with an event of its own inside the window is not a control, so he is dropped and
named on the page rather than quietly removed.
