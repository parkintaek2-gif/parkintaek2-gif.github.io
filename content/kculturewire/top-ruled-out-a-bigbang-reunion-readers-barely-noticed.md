BIGBANG's T.O.P used a solo fan meeting in Yokohama on 7 September to address the group's 20th anniversary directly: he would not be standing on stage with G-Dragon, Taeyang and Daesung for the trio's anniversary activities, even as the group continues to use his recorded vocal on "Still Life" during their tour. "Although I can no longer stand on the same stage side by side with the members, I will continue to support and cheer for their activities and music from behind the scenes," he said. STARNEWS's English-language desk carried the story on 15 September.

We checked English Wikipedia reading of T.O.P on both dates. Over the 30 days ending 16 September, his page averaged 1,448 views a day. On 7 September, the day of the statement, it drew 1,751 — 1.21 times average, a mild rise but well inside the range we'd call ordinary variation. On 15 September, when the story actually reached English-language readers, it drew 1,055 views — 0.73 times average, below normal.

The two days that stand furthest from baseline in our window, 19 August (1.60x) and 6 September (1.53x), sit before either date in this story, and we found no report naming T.O.P on those specific days. We're recording them as unexplained rather than folding them into a narrative they may not belong to.

A member confirming he won't rejoin a group he already left in practice is, in a sense, a non-event for anyone who has followed BIGBANG's last several years — which may be exactly why it didn't send new readers to look him up.
