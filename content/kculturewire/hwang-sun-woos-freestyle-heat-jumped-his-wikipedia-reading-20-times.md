Hwang Sun-woo averaged 14.9 English-language Wikipedia reads a day for the nine days from 10 to 18 September. On 20 September — the opening day of swimming at the 2026 Asian Games, when he finished second in his 100m freestyle heat to advance to the final — that reached 307 reads, 20.6 times his baseline.

## What happened

Sports Kyunghyang reported that Hwang swam the men's 100m freestyle heats in 48.13 seconds, finishing second overall and advancing to the final, at the Tokyo Aquatics Centre. It was his first race at the 2026 Asian Games (Aichi-Nagoya), five years after he first drew international attention at the same event.

## Why the timing points at the heat, not a single article

Reading rose modestly the day before his race — plausibly early Games-adjacent interest — then jumped sharply on the exact day his heat was swum and reported, and stayed at a similar level the following day when his relay event ran. The size and timing of the jump line up with the swimming schedule itself, not one isolated article.
