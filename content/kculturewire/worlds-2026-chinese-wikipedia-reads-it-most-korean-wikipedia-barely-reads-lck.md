Who is reading about the 2026 World Championship? Wikipedia counts its readers by language, so we added up the first two days of the Swiss Stage, Korean-time 24 and 25 October, which are UTC days 23 and 24.

## The tournament article: Chinese first

| Edition | 23 Oct | 24 Oct | Two days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chinese | 7,473 | 11,244 | **18,717** |
| English | 4,951 | 5,295 | **10,246** |
| Spanish | 595 | 730 | 1,325 |
| Vietnamese | 196 | 185 | 381 |

The Chinese article drew **1.8 times** the English one. On 16 October, the first day of the play-ins, the two were close: 4,747 against 4,365. Only these four editions had an article on the tournament.

## The LCK's four teams, by language

| Team | Reads, all editions | Most-read edition | Korean edition |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 4,472 | English, 40.5% | 43 (1.0%) |
| Gen.G | 2,056 | Chinese, 38.1% | **5 (0.2%)** |
| Dplus Kia | 1,365 | Chinese, 29.0% | 38 (2.8%) |
| Hanwha Life | 1,208 | English, 35.0% | 75 (6.2%) |

Over two days in which Gen.G won twice, Gen.G's Korean Wikipedia article was read **five times**. French readers looked up every LCK team more than Korean readers did: 13% to 23% of each team's reads came from the French edition.

This does not mean Korea is not watching. Korean fans mostly use **Namuwiki**, a separate Korean wiki that publishes no reading counts. What Wikipedia measures here is the world outside Korea looking Korean teams up, mostly in Chinese, English and French.

## The most-read team was from Taiwan

**CTBC Flying Oyster**, from the LCP (Taiwan, Vietnam and the wider Asia-Pacific), drew **6,678 reads** over the two days, more than T1. Its usual day is about 152. On 24 October alone it drew 4,631, and 72% of the two days' reads came from the Chinese edition, which serves readers in Taiwan as well as elsewhere. We did not check what drove that day. CTBC Flying Oyster play T1 at 02:00 KST on 27 October.

## What this does and does not say

Wikipedia reads count look-ups, not viewers. They say nothing about broadcast audiences or which team has more fans. Three teams could not be measured at all: LYON and LOS have no English Wikipedia article, and Team Secret Whales sit under a parent article that covers many games. Each team's full row is on [our Worlds 2026 page](/worlds-2026).
