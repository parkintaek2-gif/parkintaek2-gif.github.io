In its first week on Netflix's country charts, the tvN drama **100 Days of Deception** made the TV top 10 in three countries: Indonesia, South Korea and Vietnam. A week later it was in **22**, and fourth on Netflix's global non-English list with **2.8 million views**.

Six of the 22 are in the Middle East and North Africa: five Gulf states (Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar and Bahrain) and Morocco. In **Saudi Arabia** it ranked fifth, as high as in Malaysia, the Philippines and Singapore.

Latin America, where many Korean hits chart widely, gave it only four places so far, all near the bottom of the list. That is the opposite of [Doctor X: Mafia in White](/article/doctor-x-netflix-number-one-charts-in-only-2-of-35-european-countries), which charted in 17 of 21 Latin American countries the same week.

## What this does and does not say

Two weeks are an early read. A country's top 10 is a rank, not a count of viewers, and Netflix publishes no views by country. What the lists show is the shape of the audience so far: wide in Asia, strong in the Gulf, thin in Latin America and absent in Europe and North America.
