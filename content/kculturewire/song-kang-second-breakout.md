**Song Kang** was in *Sweet Home*, which peaked at 106,217 reads on English Wikipedia in December 2020. He is now in *Four Hands, Two Sonatas*, which entered the world's thousand most-read articles on 30 August 2026.

**Lee Kyu-hyung** was in *All of Us Are Dead*, which peaked at 225,923 in January 2022. He is now in *Mousetrap*, which hit rank 157 in the world on 29 August 2026.

Both of this week's Korean breakouts contain someone who has done this before. We wanted to know how unusual that is, so we counted.

## Sixteen out of 119

Across thirteen Korean titles that broke out on English Wikipedia — from *Kingdom* in 2019 to the two this week — Wikidata names **119 cast members who have their own English Wikipedia article**.

**Sixteen of them appear in two of the thirteen. Not one appears in three.**

That is **13.4%** of the pool. Say it the other way and it is sharper: **86.6% of the actors in a Korean global breakout have been in exactly one.**

## The ceiling nobody has passed

The number that surprised us is the empty row. **Nobody in these thirteen titles has been in three of them.**

Four actors came out of *Squid Game* alone into a second breakout, and *The Silent Sea* took three of them. *Parasite* connects to four other titles. There is plenty of overlap in the middle of the list — and then it stops.

We are not going to tell you what that means. Thirteen titles is a small number and we chose them; an absence in a hand-picked list of thirteen is weak evidence about anything. **What we can say is that the ceiling is where it is, and that it is worth watching whether the next breakout breaks it.**

Lee Kyu-hyung is the closest. If *Mousetrap* holds and his next series travels, he would be the first.

## The Silent Sea is the connector

One title links to five others: ***The Silent Sea*** — Gong Yoo, Heo Sung-tae and Kang Mal-geum from *Squid Game*, Kim Sun-young from *Crash Landing on You*, Bae Doona from *Kingdom*.

It also had the **smallest peak of the thirteen except this week's Mousetrap**: 59,760 reads. The most connected title in the set is one of the least read in it.

That pattern has an ordinary explanation — it was made and cast right after *Squid Game*, by people working in the same year — and we cannot separate that from anything else with this data. We are reporting the shape, not a cause.

## Why we counted this

"So-and-so is in everything" is a thing people say about Korean actors, and nobody attaches a number to it. **The number, inside thirteen global breakouts, is that sixteen people have two and nobody has three.**
