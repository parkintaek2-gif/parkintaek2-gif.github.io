Yesterday we reported that **Keonho** of Cortis had overtaken his bandmate **Sunghyun** in Star News's STARPOLL fan vote, after Sunghyun had led every snapshot we took since mid-September ([the first story](/article/cortis-keonho-overtakes-sunghyun-starpoll-wikipedia-falls)).

It lasted a day.

## Four snapshots

| Snapshot | Sunghyun | Keonho | First |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Oct | 152,428 | 126,351 | Sunghyun |
| 23 Oct | 15,418 | 9,859 | Sunghyun |
| 24 Oct | 25,487 | **34,375** | **Keonho** |
| 25 Oct | **64,617** | 49,264 | **Sunghyun** |

Between the 24 and 25 October snapshots, Sunghyun gained **39,130 votes**, nearly four times his gain of the day before (10,069). Keonho gained 14,889. The lead swung by 24,241 votes in one day.

The totals dropped sharply between 12 and 23 October, so the poll appears to have started a new period in between. We compare only snapshots inside the same period.

## Everyone else rose more slowly

| Place on 25 Oct | Name | 24 Oct | 25 Oct | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Jin (BTS) | 6,166 | 9,264 | +3,098 |
| 4 | Junho (2PM) | 4,584 | 6,528 | +1,944 |
| 5 | Jungkook (BTS) | 1,775 | 2,297 | +522 |

The two Cortis members together gained 54,019 votes in a day, nearly ten times the other three in the top five combined (5,564).

## What this does and does not say

A fan vote counts how hard fans are campaigning, not how many people care. A swing of this size in one day fits organised voting, but STARPOLL does not show who voted, so we cannot confirm it. It says nothing about which member more people listen to. When we checked on 24 October, neither member had his own English Wikipedia article.
