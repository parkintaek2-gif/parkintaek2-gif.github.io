On 5 September, Music Core carried two solo comeback stages by members of groups: Taemin of SHINee
with "Float", and TEN of NCT and WayV with "Outwest". Two people stepping out from under a group
name on the same afternoon.

That is a good day to ask a question we can actually answer: **when a member goes solo, do
English-language readers follow the person, or stay with the group?**

## Seven of eight stay with the group

| Soloist | Reads/day | Group | Reads/day | Times the group |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Hwasa | 795 | Mamamoo | 687 | 1.16 |
| Taemin | 414 | SHINee | 573 | 0.72 |
| Sunmi | 286 | Wonder Girls | 452 | 0.63 |
| Rosé | 2,461 | Blackpink | 4,337 | 0.57 |
| Jihyo | 597 | Twice | 1,572 | 0.38 |
| Baekhyun | 285 | EXO | 808 | 0.35 |
| TEN | 232 | NCT | 692 | 0.34 |
| Jimin | 2,091 | BTS | 7,869 | 0.27 |

Median daily English Wikipedia reads over the last 30 days.
[The full comparison, including four other languages, is here](/member-vs-group).

**Only Hwasa is read more than their own group**, and only just. Everybody else's group page is
opened more often than their own — in six of the eight cases, more than twice as often.

Scale does not rescue anyone. Rosé at 2,461 reads a day and Jimin at 2,091 are the two most-read
individuals in the set, and both sit well under their groups. Being widely read yourself and being
read more than your group turn out to be different things.

## The answer changes with the language

We have measured this comparison before, on the Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai and Malay Wikipedias.
There, several members are read more than their groups.

In English, one of eight is.

We do not know why, and this measure cannot tell us — it holds page names and counts, nothing about
who the readers are or what brought them. What it does establish is narrower and worth keeping:
**"readers" is not one group of people.** Any sentence of the form *the member is bigger than the
group* is incomplete until it says which language it was counted in. Ours now do.

## What this is not

It is not a contest, and we are not scoring anyone. A person's article and their group's article
answer different questions for whoever opened them, and the two counts cannot even be added — one
reader can open both.

It is also not about today. Wikimedia fills in its counts about a day late, so neither Taemin's nor
TEN's comeback is inside this measurement. The stages are why we asked, not part of the answer.

---

*This counts people who opened an English Wikipedia page. It says nothing about sales, streams,
charts, or audiences in Korea.*
