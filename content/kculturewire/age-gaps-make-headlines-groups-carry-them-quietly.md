Three separate items in this morning's Korean entertainment headlines described a couple by
the number of years between them: a wife **14 years younger**, a partner **9 years younger**,
another partner **9 years younger**. A fourth counted a relationship in years instead — a
decade together.

We hold no data about anyone's relationships and will not be measuring those. What we do hold
is the birth year of **1,464 people** across **428 Korean music groups**. So we asked a
question the same shape as the headline, about a place where the years are on the record:
**how many years separate the oldest and youngest member of a group?**

## The median is 4 years

Of the 428 groups, **337** have a recorded birth year for at least two members and can be
measured.

| Spread between oldest and youngest | Groups | Share of 337 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 14 years or more | 2 | 0.6% |
| 10 years or more | 21 | 6.2% |
| 9 years or more | 32 | 9.5% |
| Exactly 0 — all born the same year | 21 | 6.2% |

The median spread is **4 years**; the mean is **4.3**. A 14-year span, unremarkable enough in
a couple to be a headline label, is close to the outer edge of what Korean groups contain.

## The widest, and one we refuse to count

| Group | Spread | Oldest | Youngest | Members with a birth year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| After School | 14 years | 1980 | 1994 | 11 |
| NCT | 13 years | 1994 | 2007 | 26 |
| Secret Number | 13 years | 1995 | 2008 | 8 |
| god | 12 years | 1969 | 1981 | 5 |
| Pink Fantasy | 12 years | 1993 | 2005 | 7 |
| Stray Kids | 11 years | 1990 | 2001 | 10 |

One entry sits far above all of these: **Metafive, at 29 years**. We have left it out of the
headline because we could not confirm it is a Korean group, and a single unconfirmed row
should not be allowed to set a number. Naming it costs us the more dramatic figure and is
cheaper than being wrong.

## The man in today's headline is the eldest of a group spanning 12 years

The 14-year item concerns **Joon Park**, born **1969**, who is the oldest of god's five
recorded members. The youngest, Kim Tae-woo, was born **1981**.

So the same person appears twice in the same morning under two different framings of the same
kind of number — one treated as notable, one not mentioned at all. That is the whole point of
this comparison. A 12-year span inside a working group passes without comment; a 14-year span
in a marriage is a headline in three publications.

We are not arguing that either framing is wrong. We are putting the two numbers on the same
page, which is something a reader cannot do from the headlines alone.

**328 member slots have no sex recorded**, which is more than a fifth of the panel. Any
breakdown by sex here is a breakdown of what editors happened to fill in.

## What this is not

**Not about anyone's relationship.** We hold no relationship data. The couples in today's
headlines are the occasion for this count and appear nowhere in it.

**Not current line-ups.** Wikidata records people who were members. After School's 14-year
span includes members from across the group's history.

**Not complete.** 72 of the 409 groups could not be measured at all, and they are reported
as unmeasurable rather than folded in as zero.

**Not an explanation.** Nothing here says why groups are assembled from narrow age bands.
Training-cohort practice and the way Wikidata is written would both produce a median of four
years.

*This is a count of public records. It is a statistic, not a statement about any
individual.*
