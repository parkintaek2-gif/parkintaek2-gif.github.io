Fan-vote platforms built around head-to-head idol rankings are having a moment — KDOL's monthly
tables and StarNews Korea's live [star-ranking](https://www.starnewskorea.com/star-ranking) page
both pit individual K-pop idols against each other and publish a winner. We hold no data from
either platform and cannot verify their vote totals. What we can do is take the same kind of
pairing and check it against something we do hold: English Wikipedia's own reading data.

This is the first in an occasional series — **[VS News]** — that takes one such pairing and
measures it the only way we can independently redo ourselves.

## First pairing: BTS's Jung Kook and RM

## The freshest window we have: 15 August-13 September 2026

| | Member | Lookups (30 days) | Daily average |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jung Kook | 111,152 | 3,705 |
| 2 | RM | 63,723 | 2,124 |

Jung Kook drew **1.74 times** as many English Wikipedia lookups as RM over the same 30 days.

## The gap is not new — it holds in two other windows we already had on file

Our BTS member-vs-group work already carries two longer windows for the same two people, and
both point the same direction:

- **Twelve months, September 2025-August 2026:** Jung Kook 1,155,142 vs RM 711,190 — a 1.62x gap.
- **Most recent single month in that panel:** Jung Kook 106,474 vs RM 63,019 — a 1.69x gap.

Three separate windows — twelve months, one month, and the fresh 30 days measured for this
piece — all put the gap between 1.6x and 1.75x, in the same direction each time.

## What we are not saying

We are not saying which member "wins" a fan vote — we did not measure one. We are not saying
why the gap exists: solo schedules, appearances and news cycles all move Wikipedia lookups and
none of them were isolated here. What we can say is narrower: on the one axis we can
independently check ourselves, one bandmate consistently draws close to double the other's
English-language reading, in every window we hold.
