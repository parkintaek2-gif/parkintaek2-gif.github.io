An awards show for Korean artists is putting its 2026 lineup out under a line that names the
city: **IN KAOHSIUNG**. Kaohsiung is in southern Taiwan.

We have no inside knowledge of why. What we do have is a measurement of where Korean stars
are actually read, finished this week, and it points the same direction.

## The panel

Across **478 Korean entertainers**, **15 Wikipedia language editions** and **June to August
2026**, the edition that reads them most — with English set aside — is **Chinese for 306 of
them, 64.0%**. Japanese leads for 142.

**Chinese Wikipedia is blocked in mainland China.** So the readers producing that lead are in
Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia and the wider Chinese-speaking diaspora. That single
fact is why this article exists: a Chinese-edition lead is not a mainland audience, and it is
a Taiwan-and-Southeast-Asia one.

## The first name in today's posts

Two of today's lineup posts named the same artist, **Lee Da-hee**. We looked her up after
reading the posts, not before.

| Edition | Reads, June–August 2026 |
|---|---:|
| English | 51,941 |
| **Chinese** | **22,875** |
| Japanese | 9,477 |

Chinese reads her **2.4 times** as often as Japanese does.

She is not unusual in the panel. Of the twelve people with the largest Chinese-edition
counts, every one has Japanese in second place:

| Person | Chinese | Japanese |
|---|---:|---:|
| So Ji-sub | 330,748 | 97,731 |
| Park Eun-bin | 201,326 | 113,304 |
| Son Na-eun | 185,071 | 28,819 |
| Byeon Woo-seok | 179,973 | 87,529 |
| Lim Ji-yeon | 165,436 | 93,789 |
| Jung Hae-in | 155,431 | 94,032 |
| Hwasa | 112,332 | 16,451 |
| IU | 102,141 | 55,114 |

IU is in today's Korean headlines for something entirely unrelated — a haircut. She is also
read almost twice as much in Chinese as in Japanese.

## Where it goes the other way

The pattern is not universal, and the counter-examples matter. **Son Ye-jin**, also in
today's headlines, is read **77,502** times in Japanese against **50,424** in Chinese. So is
Hyun Bin, Song Joong-ki, Park Seo-joon and nine others in our Japanese-leading list.

142 of 478 is not a rounding error. Anyone planning around this should plan around a split
audience, not a single one.

## What this article does not claim

**It does not say the show chose Kaohsiung because of reading data.** We have no information
about the decision and did not ask for any. Two independent things point the same way; that
is all that is being reported.

**It does not say a language is a country.** Every figure above is a Wikipedia language
edition. Chinese spans Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia and the diaspora, and excludes
mainland China by blocking. Japanese is closer to a single country but is not identical to
one.

**It does not say why any edition leads.** Diaspora size, promotion history and the editing
culture of each Wikipedia would all produce this shape, and pageview counts cannot separate
them.

**It is not the whole roster.** Only people whose Wikidata entry links to at least 20
editions were measured, which is 478 of them.

*This is a count of Wikipedia reads. It is a statistic, not a statement about any
individual, and not a claim about any event's plans.*
