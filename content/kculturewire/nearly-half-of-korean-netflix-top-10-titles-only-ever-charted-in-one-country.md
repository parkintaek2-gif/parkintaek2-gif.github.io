Across 269 weeks of Netflix's public Top 10 charts, tracked in 93 markets from July 2021 to August 2026, 974 distinct Korean titles have placed in at least one country's weekly Top 10 at least once. Of those, 447 — 45.9% — never placed top 10 anywhere but a single country. The median Korean title on this list charted in just 2 countries. The widest-reaching title charted in all 93 markets at some point.

## What we counted

| Charted in exactly 1 country, ever | 447 (45.9%) |

## What "one country" does and does not mean

This counts chart placement, not catalogue availability. Netflix does not publish a per-country catalogue, so we can only see where a title was popular enough to place in a country's weekly Top 10 — not everywhere it was simply available to watch. A title charting in one country is a title that broke into that country's most-watched list at least once; it says nothing about whether the title was licensed, watchable, or simply present but outside the top 10 everywhere else, which is by far the more common situation for any title on a streaming service.

Read alongside a "K-content goes global" framing, this is a useful correction to scale, not a contradiction of it: a small number of Korean titles chart nearly everywhere, but the typical breakout title's confirmed chart reach is narrow — most of what shows up on this list at all shows up in one place.
