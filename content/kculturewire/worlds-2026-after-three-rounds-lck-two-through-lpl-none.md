Three rounds into the 2026 World Championship Swiss Stage, Korea's **LCK** already has two teams in the quarter-finals: **Dplus Kia** and **Gen.G**, both at 3-0. Its other two are still alive: T1 at 2-1 and Hanwha Life at 1-2.

China's **LPL** has no team through yet. Invictus Gaming, Top Esports and Bilibili Gaming are all at 2-1. Its fourth team, **Anyone's Legend**, who went 3-0 a year ago, lost 2-0 to Team Liquid on the morning of 27 October and are out at 0-3 ([the elimination](/article/anyones-legend-out-of-worlds-2026-at-0-3-a-year-after-going-3-0)).

## The same point in 2024 and 2025

We took the first 24 Swiss matches of each year from Riot's schedule, which is rounds one to three, and read off the LCK and LPL records.

| Year | LCK through after round three | LPL through after round three | LCK at the end of the Swiss Stage | LPL at the end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 1 (Gen.G) | 1 (LNG) | 3 of 4 qualified | 4 of 4 |
| 2025 | 1 (KT) | 1 (Anyone's Legend) | 4 of 4 | 2 of 3 |
| 2026 | **2** (Dplus Kia, Gen.G) | **0** | still playing | still playing |

In both earlier years the two leagues were level at this point, one team each. This year they are not. In both earlier years the LCK also had all four teams alive after round three, as it does now; in 2024 one of them, Dplus Kia, then lost twice and went out.

## What it does not say

Being through early is not the same as going far. LNG, first through in 2024, lost a quarter-final; Anyone's Legend, through at 3-0 in 2025, lost a quarter-final too. T1 won both of those years after qualifying later. This is a count of where teams stand, not a forecast. Every record, and where teams at 2-1, 1-2 and 0-2 ended up in past years, is on [our Worlds 2026 page](/worlds-2026).
