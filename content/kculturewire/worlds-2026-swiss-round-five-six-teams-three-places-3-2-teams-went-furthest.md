The 2026 World Championship Swiss Stage ends on **1 November**. Six teams are at **2-2**. They play three best-of-three matches, Korea time, and the three winners take the last places in the quarter-finals.

| KST | Match | Leagues |
|---|---|---|
| 02:00 | Movistar KOI v Karmine Corp | LEC v LEC |
| 05:00 | LOS v LYON | CBLOL v LCS |
| 08:00 | **Hanwha Life Esports v Bilibili Gaming** | LCK v LPL |

Because the pairings set league against league, some of the quarter-final line-up is already fixed: exactly one LEC team will be there, and one of LOS and LYON. The last match decides whether Korea's LCK ends with four quarter-finalists or China's LPL with three. Five are through: Dplus KIA, Gen.G and T1 from the LCK, Invictus Gaming and Top Esports from the LPL.

## How the last two years' deciders went

| Year | Decider | Winner went on to |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | Weibo Gaming 2-1 Dplus KIA | Semi-final |
| 2024 | Bilibili Gaming 2-1 G2 | **Final** |
| 2024 | FlyQuest 2-1 Team Liquid | Quarter-final |
| 2025 | CTBC Flying Oyster 2-0 FlyQuest | Quarter-final |
| 2025 | Top Esports 2-1 Bilibili Gaming | Semi-final |
| 2025 | T1 2-0 Movistar KOI | **Won the title** |

Four of the six went to a third game. Bilibili Gaming have played this round both years, winning in 2024 and losing in 2025; Movistar KOI lost theirs in 2025, to T1.

## The 3-2 teams went furthest

| Qualified at | Teams, 2024 and 2025 | Reached the semi-finals or further |
|---|---|---|
| 3-0 | 4 | 2 |
| 3-1 | 6 | 2 |
| **3-2** | 6 | **4** |

The last teams in did not finish last. Four of the six 3-2 teams reached the semi-finals or further, among them Bilibili Gaming, who reached the 2024 final, and T1, who won in 2025. The first teams in, at 3-0, did no better than the rest.

Six deciders over two years is a very small group. It does not mean a 3-2 team is likely to go far this year. It means the Swiss record is not a ranking, and the teams playing on 1 November have not been told anything about their ceiling.
