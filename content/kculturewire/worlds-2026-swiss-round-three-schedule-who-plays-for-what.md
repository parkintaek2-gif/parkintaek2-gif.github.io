Round two of the Worlds 2026 Swiss Stage ended on the morning of 25 October (KST) ([the results](/article/worlds-2026-swiss-round-two-gen-g-dplus-kia-win-lck-derbies)). Round three is next, over **26 and 27 October**.

In the Swiss Stage, teams only play others with the same record. So although Riot has not yet published the pairings, it is already settled **which teams play for what**.

## The three groups

| Record | What is at stake | Teams |
|---|---|---|
| 2-0 | Win: into the quarter-finals | Movistar KOI, LYON, **Gen.G**, **Dplus Kia** |
| 1-1 | Win: 2-1. Lose: 1-2, one loss from elimination | Top Esports, CTBC Flying Oyster, G2 Esports, Karmine Corp, Invictus Gaming, **Hanwha Life Esports**, **T1**, Bilibili Gaming |
| 0-2 | Lose: eliminated | Anyone's Legend, LOS, Team Secret Whales, Team Liquid Alienware |

Matches between 2-0 teams and between 0-2 teams are **best-of-three**. Matches between 1-1 teams stay **best-of-one**.

## The time slots (KST)

| Day | 02:00 | 03:00 | 04:00 | 07:00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon 26 Oct | Bo1 | Bo1 | Bo3 | Bo3 |
| Tue 27 Oct | Bo1 | Bo1 | Bo3 | Bo3 |

Riot listed all eight slots as **TBD** when we wrote this. Who plays whom, and on which day, has not been announced. We will add the pairings here when it is.

## For Korean fans

Two LCK teams, Gen.G and Dplus Kia, are one series win from the quarter-finals. The other two, Hanwha Life and T1, are in the 1-1 group, where a single game decides whether they move to 2-1 or fall to 1-2.
