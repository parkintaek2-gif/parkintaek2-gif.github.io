Between 24 July and 15 August, **BigBang**'s English Wikipedia page was opened about **1,321 times
a day**. In the seven days that followed it was opened **3,536 times a day** — **2.7 times as
often**.

Nothing else near the top of our list moved anything like that far.

| Act | 23 days before | Last 7 days | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **BigBang** | 1,321/day | **3,536/day** | **2.68×** |
| Illit | 1,586/day | 2,093/day | 1.32× |
| Stray Kids | 2,525/day | 2,748/day | 1.09× |
| Blackpink | 4,429/day | 4,731/day | 1.07× |
| Twice | 1,951/day | 1,633/day | 0.84× |
| Seventeen | 1,722/day | 1,110/day | 0.64× |

The second-biggest riser, **Illit**, moved a third. BigBang moved nearly three times as much as
that. On a list of 822 groups it is alone at that end.

## We do not know why, and we are not going to guess

This is a count of one thing: an encyclopaedia page being opened. **A comeback, a rumour, a
television rerun, an anniversary and a piece of bad news all look exactly the same in this
data.** Wikipedia records the open and nothing else. Anybody who tells you the cause from a
pageview chart is telling you a story, not a measurement.

What we can say is that the movement is real and it is not a rounding artefact. The two periods do
not overlap — the last seven days are set against the twenty-three days before them, not against
the thirty-day total that contains them, which would have flattened the rise to 1.9×. And only
acts with at least 20,000 opens across the window are ranked, so nothing here is a small number
pretending to be a trend.

## What a number like this is good for

Not for deciding who is popular. **BigBang is still ninth on the list by total opens** — Illit,
which moved a third as fast, is eleventh, and Twice, which fell, is eighth. Speed and size are
different questions and a single ranking answers neither on its own.

It is good for one thing: telling you **where to go and look**. Something happened around
BigBang in the third week of August, in English, outside Korea. Our data marks the spot on the
map. It does not say what is there.
