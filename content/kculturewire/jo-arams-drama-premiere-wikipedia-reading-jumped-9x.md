Jo Aram, a former member of the girl group Gugudan now working as an actress, averaged 313 English
Wikipedia reads a day in the eleven days before 12 September, 2026. The day her new KBS2 weekend
drama's second episode aired, her reading hit 2,877 -- 9.2 times that baseline.

## What happened

Nate News reported that "Melo Not Solo" (너 말고 다른 연애), a KBS2 weekend melodrama about a
ten-year couple confronting unfamiliar feelings, premiered 12 September at 9:20pm. Jo Aram plays
Park Su-ah, introduced as a new romantic variable alongside leads Seo Kang-joon and Ahn Eun-jin.

| Day | Views | Multiple of the 313.4/day baseline |
|---|---|---|
| 1-11 Sep average (baseline) | 313.4 | 1.0&times; |
| 12 September (episode 1) | 1,088 | 3.5&times; |
| 13 September (episode 2) | 2,877 | 9.2&times; |

## A second-episode peak, not a premiere-night peak

The pattern here is different from a single broadcast moment: reading rose on premiere night and
rose further the next day, when the second episode aired. That is consistent with a new cast
member's page getting a first look after episode one, then a second, larger look after viewers who
tuned in for episode two searched again -- though we cannot confirm word-of-mouth or search-carryover
as the specific mechanism, only that the two dates line up with the two broadcasts we can verify.
