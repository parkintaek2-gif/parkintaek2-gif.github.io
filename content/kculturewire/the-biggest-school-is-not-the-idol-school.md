Wikidata records where 4,535 of the Korean actors, singers and songwriters in our roster went to
school. It separately records which of them belong to a music group. Those two columns have never
been read together, so we read them together.

**1,106 of the 4,535 — 24.4 per cent — are in a music group.** That is the number to hold on to,
because almost every school sits a long way from it in one direction or the other.

## The largest school is an actors' school

| School | People recorded | In a music group | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Seoul Institute of the Arts | 352 | 28 | 8% |
| Chung-Ang University | 310 | 17 | 5% |
| Dongduk Women's University | 206 | 48 | 23% |
| Dongguk University | 200 | 25 | 13% |
| Kyung Hee University | 145 | 51 | 35% |
| Dankook University | 143 | 16 | 11% |
| **School of Performing Arts Seoul** | **125** | **102** | **82%** |
| **Hanlim Multi Art School** | **123** | **99** | **80%** |

Seoul Institute of the Arts is the most-recorded school in Korean entertainment by a wide margin.
It is also, on this column, one of the least idol-shaped places in the country: eight per cent,
a third of the roster average.

School of Performing Arts Seoul has 125 people recorded against its name — barely a third of Seoul
Institute's total — and **102 of them are in a group.** Hanlim Multi Art School is the same shape:
123 people, 99 in groups.

**Two schools account for 201 of the 1,106 idols with a school recorded — 18 per cent of them.**
Five schools account for 31 per cent. The other 632 schools that produced at least one share the rest.

## The highest shares belong to schools that are not really schools in the usual sense

| School | People recorded | In a music group | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Global Cyber University | 24 | 23 | 96% |
| Korea Art High School | 20 | 19 | 95% |
| Kyung Hee Cyber University | 35 | 29 | 83% |
| School of Performing Arts Seoul | 125 | 102 | 82% |
| Hanlim Multi Art School | 123 | 99 | 80% |
| Howon University | 29 | 18 | 62% |
| Dong-Ah Institute of Media and Arts | 82 | 45 | 55% |

Two of the top three are online universities. Six schools in the whole file have "Cyber" in the
name and together they account for 57 group members. That is not an incidental detail: a performer
already working full time cannot attend lectures, and the online degree is the form of higher
education that fits a schedule set by a company. The share does not measure teaching. It measures
who can enrol.

Korea Art High School sits at 95 per cent on 20 people. We are reporting it because the number is
what the file says, and flagging it because twenty is a small base — one different person moves it
five points.

## What this does not say

It does not say a school makes idols. Wikidata is filled in by volunteers, and a famous group
member is more likely to have a school recorded than a session musician who never charted. The
same bias runs the other way for actors, who are covered heavily by film databases.

It also does not say anything about 4,714 people — more than half the roster — because no school
is recorded for them at all. A school with three entries here may have taught three hundred people.

What the file does support is narrower and still worth saying plainly: **among the people whose
schooling is written down, the places that recur for idols and the places that recur for actors
are different places, and the largest name in Korean performing-arts education is on the actors'
side of that line.**
