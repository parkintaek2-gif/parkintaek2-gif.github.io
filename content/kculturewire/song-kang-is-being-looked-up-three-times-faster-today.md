Yesterday, **Song Kang**'s English Wikipedia page was opened **4,613 times**. Across the seven days
before it, the average was **1,411 a day**. That is **3.3 times** the rate, and it was the largest
single-day move of any Korean name our sweep could check.

We do not know why. We looked at what we hold about him instead.

## Three charting shows, three number ones

| Title | Countries | Weeks on a chart | Peak |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **My Demon** | 66 | 20 | **1** |
| **Sweet Home** | 42 | 12 | **1** |
| **Forecasting Love and Weather** | 24 | 16 | **1** |

Every title he has that entered a Netflix country top 10 **reached number one in at least one
country**. Between them the three took **911 chart places across 67 countries**.

That record is uncommon without being unique. Of the **389 actors** we hold at least three charting
titles for, **33 — 8.5% — have a clean sheet like this.** He is twenty-first among them by
countries reached, which is the honest way to put it: a strong record inside a group of thirty-odd
people, not a record nobody else has.

## What "number one" is worth knowing about

A peak of one is a fact about a single country in a single week. **My Demon** reached it and stayed
on charts for twenty weeks across sixty-six countries; **Forecasting Love and Weather** reached it
too, in twenty-four countries. The same words describe both, and the two runs are not the same
size. When you see "number one on Netflix" attached to a Korean drama, the country and the week are
usually missing, and they are where most of the meaning lives.

## What we could not check

Our sweep asked about 120 names across five Wikipedia editions. **538 of those requests came back
rate-limited, not answered.** Those names were not measured, and something bigger may be sitting
among them. We are reporting the largest mover *we could see*, which is a different claim from the
largest mover there was.
