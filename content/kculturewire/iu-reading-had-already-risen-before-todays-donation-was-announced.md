IU marked her 18 September debut anniversary with a 200 million won donation split across four organisations, given under the fan-tribute name "IUaena" — a combination of her own name and her fandom, Uaena. Maeil Business Newspaper reported the total for the year, including this gift, at 600 million won.

We can't yet check what the announcement itself did to her English Wikipedia reading — Wikimedia's pageviews API had not published 18 September's numbers as of this writing.

What we can check is the run-up. Over the eight days ending 17 September, IU's page averaged 2,297 views a day, about 19% above the 1,933 average of the 22 days before that. The single day before the announcement, 17 September, came in at 2,099 views — 1.08 times her fuller 30-day baseline, a mild reading rather than a spike.

We're not attaching either number to today's donation. The rise started roughly a week before the announcement, which is too early for it to be a reaction to news that hadn't broken yet, and we found no other single event in our own sources that explains it. It stays in the record as unexplained. We will check 18 September itself once the data allows.
