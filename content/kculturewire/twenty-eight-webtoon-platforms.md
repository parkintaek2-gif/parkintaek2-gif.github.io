Korea's webtoon industry, as its own government survey counts it, is **396 businesses**. Split them
the way the survey does and the sector turns out to be two things sharing a name.

| | Businesses | Average revenue | Under ₩1bn | ₩10bn+ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Platforms | 28 | **₩94.5bn** | 51.8% | 39.3% |
| Content providers | 368 | **₩1.5bn** | 78.3% | 5.3% |
| All | 396 | ₩8.1bn | 76.4% | 7.7% |

**A platform averages 64 times the revenue of a content provider.** The 28 of them are 7% of the
businesses in the survey.

## The shape underneath the average

An average of ₩8.1bn across the sector would suggest a mid-sized industry. The band columns say
something else: **76.4% of all webtoon businesses take less than ₩1bn a year**, and only 7.7% clear
₩10bn.

Sorted by staff instead, the same shape appears:

| | Businesses | Average revenue | Under ₩1bn |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Under 10 staff | 214 | ₩0.3bn | 92.8% |
| 10–49 staff | 129 | ₩1.3bn | 68.9% |
| 50+ staff | 53 | ₩56.1bn | 28.3% |

**214 of 396 businesses — more than half — have fewer than ten people.** Their average annual revenue
is ₩330m. The 53 businesses with fifty or more staff average ₩56.1bn, roughly 170 times as much.

## What each kind of business actually does

The survey also splits by main line of work, and the split tracks the platform divide almost exactly.

| Main business | Businesses | Average revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Webtoon service provision | 31 | ₩85.3bn |
| Other | 24 | ₩2.0bn |
| Distribution and brokerage | 65 | ₩1.6bn |
| Publishing | 28 | ₩1.5bn |
| Planning and production | 247 | ₩1.4bn |

**Planning and production is the largest group and the second-smallest by revenue.** 247 of the 396
businesses make the work; they average ₩1.4bn. The 31 that run the service where it is read average
61 times that.

## How much of this is actually webtoons

One column in the survey is easy to miss and worth more than most of the rest: what share of a
business's revenue comes from webtoons at all.

**59.5% of businesses take three-quarters or more of their revenue from webtoons.** Among the very
small — under ten staff — it is 67.5%. Among businesses over ₩10bn it drops to 41.3%.

So the large end is diversified and the small end is not. The businesses least able to absorb a bad
year are the ones with the least else to fall back on.

## What this survey does not tell you

**It counts businesses, not artists.** Nothing here says how revenue is divided between a platform, a
production studio and the person drawing. That is the question most often asked about this industry
and this dataset cannot answer it.

**It is 2024.** The survey runs behind, and 2024 is the most recent reference year published. We add
later years when they appear.

**And the two 28s are different sets.** Twenty-eight businesses are platforms; twenty-eight are
publishers by main line of work. They are separate cuts of the same 396 and the coincidence of the
number means nothing.
