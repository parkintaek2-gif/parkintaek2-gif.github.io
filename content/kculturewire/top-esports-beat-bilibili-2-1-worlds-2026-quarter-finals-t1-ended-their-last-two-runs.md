**Top Esports** are in the quarter-finals of the 2026 World Championship. They lost the first game to **Bilibili Gaming** at 08:00 Korea time on 30 October, then won the next two to take the series 2-1 and finish the Swiss Stage at **3-1**.

A day earlier **Invictus Gaming** beat LYON 2-1 to qualify at 3-1 too. After three rounds China's LPL had no team through; after four it has two. Bilibili Gaming drop to 2-2 and play once more on 1 November.

## The three games, from Riot's live stats

| Game | Winner | Kills | Gold | Towers | Barons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bilibili Gaming | 21–6 | 62,797–48,937 | 10–1 | 1–0 |
| 2 | **Top Esports** | 13–10 | 72,327–69,206 | 9–6 | 1–0 |
| 3 | **Top Esports** | 17–3 | 60,395–49,051 | 11–2 | 1–0 |

Figures are winner first. Game 1 was one-sided for Bilibili Gaming, and game 3 was one-sided the other way: Bilibili Gaming took two towers and three kills. Game 2 was the close one, about 3,000 gold apart.

## Top Esports and T1

This is the third year in a row Top Esports have come through the Swiss Stage. The last two ended against the same team.

| Year | Swiss record | Knocked out by | Round |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 3-1 | T1, 0-3 | Quarter-final |
| 2025 | 3-2 | T1, 0-3 | Semi-final |
| 2026 | 3-1 | — | — |

T1 also qualified at 3-1 this year. Whether the two meet again depends on the draw, which had not been published when we wrote.

## Who read Invictus Gaming

On 29 October (UTC), the day Invictus Gaming qualified, the **Chinese** edition of its Wikipedia article was read **773** times, about 18 times its usual 43. The English edition, normally the larger of the two, had 266. Top Esports' reading for its own qualifying day was not yet published.
