BTS's Jimin passed a streaming record on 17 September: Big Hit Music said his 2023 solo single "Like Crazy" became the first fully Korean-language song by a K-pop soloist to cross 1 billion streams on Spotify. The track had already made history at release, debuting atop Billboard's Hot 100 — itself a first for a Korean-language solo song — and topping iTunes charts in all 119 regions it tracks.

We checked what that announcement did to how often people looked Jimin up on English Wikipedia.

Nothing. Over the 29 days before the announcement (19 August-16 September), Jimin averaged 2,022 views a day. On 17 September, the day Big Hit Music's milestone went out, his page drew 1,801 views — 0.89 times his own average. That's a quieter day than usual, not a spike.

The one day that did stand out in our window was 23 August, at 2,661 views (1.32x baseline) — six days before the streaming announcement, with no news event we could find naming Jimin that day. We are not attaching it to this story or guessing at a cause; it goes in the record as unexplained.

The pattern matches what we found when Jin's "Don't Say You Love Me" crossed the same 1-billion mark in August: a Spotify-side number, tracking people already listening, doesn't require anyone to go look up who the artist is. Two data points from the same group is not a rule — it's two data points.
