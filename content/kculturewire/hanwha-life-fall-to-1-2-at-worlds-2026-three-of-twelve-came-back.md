**Hanwha Life Esports** are one loss from going home at the 2026 World Championship. Invictus Gaming beat them in a single game at 02:00 Korea time on 26 October, and Hanwha Life dropped to **1-2** in the Swiss Stage. An hour later **G2 Esports** lost to Top Esports and joined them at 1-2.

| Kills | **29** | 9 |
| Gold | **60,316** | 51,064 |
| Towers | **7** | 3 |
| Dragons | **4** | 0 |
| Barons | 0 | **1** |

## How 1-2 teams have done

From 1-2, a team needs two more wins to qualify; one more loss eliminates it. We put every Swiss Stage match of 2024 and 2025 in order from Riot's schedule and followed every team that reached 1-2.

| Year | Teams at 1-2 | Qualified | Who, and how far |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 6 | 2 | Bilibili Gaming (lost the final), Weibo Gaming (lost a semi-final) |
| 2025 | 6 | 1 | **T1 (won the title)** |
| Both | **12** | **3** | |

Nine of the twelve went out. Three of those nine won once more to reach 2-2 and then lost: Team Liquid in 2024, and Movistar KOI and Bilibili Gaming in 2025. The three who came back all went at least as far as a semi-final.

## Read this as a spread, not odds

Three of twelve is a description of two past fields. It is not a probability for Hanwha Life or G2, who play different opponents in a different year. Every 1-2 team's path is listed on [our Worlds 2026 page](/worlds-2026).

## Where the LCK stands

Dplus Kia are through at 3-0 ([the first team in](/article/dplus-kia-first-into-worlds-2026-quarter-finals-first-in-has-not-won)). Gen.G joined them at 3-0 by beating LYON 2-1 later that morning ([the result](/article/gen-g-beat-lyon-2-1-join-dplus-kia-worlds-2026-quarter-finals-lck-first-two)). T1 are 1-1 and play CTBC Flying Oyster at 02:00 KST on 27 October. Hanwha Life's next opponent comes from round four, which Riot had not yet paired when we wrote.
