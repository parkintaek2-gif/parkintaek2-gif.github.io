STARPOLL is a fan-voting app run by STARNEWS, official partner of the AAA 2026 awards. Its
public leaderboard, "Star Ranking," updates continuously and is visible without logging in — only
casting a vote requires an account. On 16 September 2026, its Female Idol category read: Minji
(NewJeans) first with 18,270 votes, Haerin (NewJeans) second with 15,716, and Red Velvet's Irene
third with 14,811.

**A fan vote counts people motivated to vote, repeatedly, for a name. It is not a measurement of
how many people look that name up.** We hold a different number for that: English Wikipedia
pageviews, which we've used all year to check reported K-pop news against actual reading. So we
checked whether the two numbers agree on who's "first."

They don't.

## What we measured

We pulled 30 days of daily English Wikipedia pageviews (24 July-22 August 2026, the freshest
complete window we had archived at the time of writing) for every name in STARPOLL's top five
we could match to an individual page.

| | STARPOLL vote (16 Sep) | Vote rank | 30-day Wikipedia total | 30-day rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minji | 18,270 | 1 | 12,075 | 3 |
| Haerin | 15,716 | 2 | 9,313 | 4 |
| Irene | 14,811 | 3 | **17,351** | **1** |
| Hyein | 12,491 | 5 | 9,069 | 4 (tied) |

Irene placed third in the vote, behind two NewJeans members. On our reading count, she leads all
three by a clear margin — 1.44x Minji's total, the vote's actual first-place name. Haerin,
the vote's runner-up, has the lowest reading total of the four names here.

We could not match STARPOLL's fourth-place name in this category to a specific English Wikipedia
page with confidence, so we've left that row out rather than guess.

## The men's category tells a different, messier story

STARPOLL's Male Idol vote on the same day: Sunghyun and Kunho, both of CORTIS, took first and
second by a wide margin (282,130 and 228,120 votes), ahead of 2PM's Junho, CORTIS's Juhoon, and —
fifth — BTS's Jin, with 4,574 votes.

Jin is the only one of the five with his own English Wikipedia page in our panel: 64,831 views
over the same 30 days, or 2,161 a day. That's a higher individual total than any of the three
NewJeans members above. But we have no individual figure for Sunghyun, Kunho, Junho or Juhoon to
set next to it — none of the four has an individual page in our panel yet, most likely because
CORTIS is a newer act and hasn't accumulated separate member pages the way longer-running groups
have.

What we can measure at the group level cuts the other way from the women's category: CORTIS's
group page drew 74,689 views over the same window — more than any other name or group in this
piece, BTS included. That's directionally consistent with two CORTIS members topping the men's
vote, even though we can't divide that number between Sunghyun and Kunho specifically.

## What this does and doesn't tell us

A fan vote and a pageview count measure different behaviors, and neither one is "the real"
popularity. In the women's category, the vote and our reading count disagreed on who's first. In
the men's category, the only comparison we could make individually (Jin) doesn't match his vote
placement either — but the group-level number for the vote's top act (CORTIS) points the same
direction the vote does. We're not resolving that into one ranking. We're reporting what each
number actually counts, and where they land in the same place and where they don't.
