Korean entertainment outlets reported this week that Jin of BTS leads one idol vote for a
67th consecutive week, and another for a 131st — 32 straight months at the top of its
monthly table. Jimin's birthday vote is running a sixth win in a row.

We cannot check a poll. What we can check is a different thing entirely: **when somebody
who is not in the fandom wants to know who these people are, whose page do they open?**

## For most Korean acts, the reading is on the members

We put each act's English Wikipedia page beside its members' pages — 380 acts, 1,247
act–member pairs, twelve months to August 2026.

| Set | Acts | Median member share |
|---|---:|---:|
| All acts we could measure | 380 | **75.7%** |
| Excluding suspected sub-units | 219 | **70.3%** |
| Suspected sub-units only | 161 | 84.9% |

For the median act, three reads in four land on a member rather than on the act. That is
worth stating plainly because the reverse would be the intuitive answer: a group is one
name to learn, and seven members are seven.

## BTS, member by member

BTS sends **65.7%** of its reading to member pages — below the median, because the group's
own page is one of the most-read Korean articles in English at 2.77 million reads over the
window.

| Member | Reads | Share of member reads |
|---|---:|---:|
| **V** | **1,235,936** | **23.2%** |
| Jungkook | 1,052,256 | 19.8% |
| RM | 650,334 | 12.2% |
| Jimin | 640,485 | 12.0% |
| Jin | 609,830 | 11.5% |
| Suga | 604,739 | 11.4% |
| J-Hope | 529,121 | 9.9% |
| *Group page* | *2,774,422* | — |

Jin sits fifth of seven, and the ordering matters less than it looks. The distance from
fifth to fourth is **0.6%** of the member total — 30,655 reads out of 5.3 million, closer
to a tie than to a rank. The distance from first to second is **3.5%**, nearly six times
larger. The bottom five sit inside a band from 9.9% to 12.2%; V and Jungkook are the two
that stand apart from it.

So the honest sentence is not "Jin is fifth." It is: **two members are read much more than
the other five, and those five are hard to separate.**

## The two counts are measuring different things

This is the part we would rather over-explain than let a reader guess at.

A vote is a count of **effort**. Somebody decided to open an app, log in, and press a
button, often daily, often organised. It measures how much a fandom is willing to do, and
a fandom can be very large without being very willing, or very willing without being very
large.

An encyclopaedia read is a count of **curiosity from outside**. Somebody heard a name and
went to find out. It is a low-effort act, it is mostly not fans, and nobody organises it.

So a person can lead a poll for 131 weeks and be fifth in reads without either number
being wrong. They answer different questions. We are not ranking popularity here, and
we do not have the data that would let us.

## What would change our mind

If Jin's read share moved after this run of poll wins, that would be evidence the two
measures are connected. We cannot say that yet: our window closes in August 2026 and this
week's reporting is newer than our data. We will be able to ask it in the October
recount, and we will report the answer whichever way it goes.
