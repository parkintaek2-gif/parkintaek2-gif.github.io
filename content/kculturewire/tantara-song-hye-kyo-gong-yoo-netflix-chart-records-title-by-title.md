**Song Hye-kyo** and **Gong Yoo** lead *Tantara*, a series set in the 1950s music business that Netflix has confirmed for **December 2026** ([What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/k-dramas/netflix-sets-december-2026-release-date-gong-yoo-k-drama-tantara/)).

What have their titles done on Netflix's weekly top 10? Netflix publishes a list for each of 93 countries. We have every week since July 2021.

## Song Hye-kyo

| Title | Type | Countries | Weeks on any chart | Best position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Glory** | Series | **91** | 29 | 1 |
| Now, We Are Breaking Up | Series | 1 | 5 | 1 |
| Descendants of the Sun | Series | 2 | 4 | 2 |
| All In | Film | 3 | 2 | 5 |

One title carries almost all of it. *The Glory* reached 91 countries; nothing else of hers on these charts reached more than three.

| **The Silent Sea** | Series | **76** | 5 | 1 |

Gong Yoo has a supporting role in *Squid Game*, whose 60 weeks also cover all of its seasons. Of the titles he leads, *The Silent Sea* went widest. *Guardian*, the 2016 drama better known as *Goblin*, never went beyond three countries but stayed 19 weeks.

## Against the usual Korean series

The typical Korean series on these charts reached **5 countries** and stayed **6 weeks**. *The Glory*, *The Silent Sea* and *The Trunk* each went at least eight times wider than that.

## What this does and does not say

These are positions, not audiences, and they cover only what charted from July 2021. A title is listed only when Wikidata records the actor in its cast, so a title could be missing. None of this predicts how *Tantara* will do. More Korean series, ranked by weeks and by countries, are on [our Netflix chart page](/kdrama-netflix).
