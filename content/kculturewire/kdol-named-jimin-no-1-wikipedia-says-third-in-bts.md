On 13 September 2026, StarNews Korea reported that BTS's Jimin had finished first in the
KDOL app's August 2026 monthly idol ranking — 1,519,172,298 "hearts," more than 850 million
ahead of second place, on top of a claimed 222 consecutive weekly No. 1 finishes and a
61-day daily streak. KDOL's own site was unreachable when we checked, so none of those
figures are ours to verify; they are reported news, and we treat them as exactly that.

We hold a different, independently checkable count for the same seven people: how many
times each BTS member's English Wikipedia article was opened.

## The freshest window we have: 1-13 September 2026

| | Member | Lookups |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jung Kook | 49,576 |
| 2 | V | 42,795 |
| 3 | RM | 28,921 |
| **4** | **Jimin** | **25,057** |
| 5 | Jin | 22,585 |
| 6 | Suga | 21,786 |
| 7 | J-Hope | 18,803 |

Twelve days into September, Jimin sits fourth of seven — **12.0%** of the combined lookups
across all seven members. Jung Kook alone draws almost double Jimin's total.

## This is not new — we published August's version of this table on 10 September

Our [10 September piece](/article/68-weeks-of-fan-votes-and-fifth-place-in-lookups) already
counted August 2026 lookups for the same seven members, in the context of a different report
(a Korean fan poll, not KDOL). Jimin ranked **3rd of 7** that month, at 68,076 lookups —
12.8% of the total. Across the full twelve-month window (September 2025 to August 2026), the
same piece has him **4th of 7**. We are not re-measuring those; the fresh number in this
piece is September's first 12 days, and it lands in the same place the other two windows do.

Three separate windows — twelve months, one full month, and twelve fresh days — all put
Jimin third or fourth among his own six bandmates by this measure. None of the three put him
first.

## What we are not saying

We are not saying KDOL's number is wrong. A vote inside a fan-voting app and a lookup on an
encyclopedia page are different acts, made by people who may not overlap at all, and
disagreement between the two counts is not evidence against either one. What we can say is
narrower: on the one axis we can independently check ourselves, the "runaway global No. 1"
framing does not repeat. Two of Jimin's own bandmates outdraw him on English Wikipedia in
every window we hold, including the freshest one.
