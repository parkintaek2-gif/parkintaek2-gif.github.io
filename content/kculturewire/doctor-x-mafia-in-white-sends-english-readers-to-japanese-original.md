**Doctor X: Mafia in White** premiered on SBS on **9 October 2026**. It is a Korean remake of TV Asahi's long-running *Doctor-X: Surgeon Michiko Daimon*, with Kim Ji-won as the surgeon who works alone and never fails. Within a week it led Netflix's global non-English TV list ([where it charted, and where it didn't](/article/doctor-x-netflix-number-one-charts-in-only-2-of-35-european-countries)), and in Korea Star News reported a rating of **17.5%**.

English Wikipedia shows what else it did: it sent readers looking for the Japanese show it came from.

## The original, the remake and the cast

| English Wikipedia page | Usual day (median, 1 Sep–8 Oct) | Opening weekend peak | 18 Oct |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doctor X: Mafia in White (the remake) | under 50 before launch | **21,479** (10 Oct) | 18,933 |
| **Doctor-X: Surgeon Michiko Daimon** (the Japanese original) | **1,059** | **9,288** (11 Oct) | 7,557 |
| Kim Ji-won (lead) | 1,055 | 6,940 (11 Oct) | 5,381 |
| Jang Seung-jo | 328 | 939 (10 Oct) | **5,493** |
| Kim Woo-seok | 212 | 4,205 (10–11 Oct) | 3,724 |
| Son Hyun-joo | 279 | — | 2,208 |
| Lee Jung-eun | 398 | — | 2,155 |

On 11 October the Japanese original's page drew **9,288** reads, nearly nine times its usual day, and more than Kim Ji-won's own page (6,940). A week later, on the second weekend, it was still at 7,557.

## Second weekend, new names

The second weekend moved readers to the supporting cast. **Jang Seung-jo**, whose character Kim Ji-won's surgeon brings down in the latest episodes (as Star News put it), went from a usual 328 reads to **5,493** on 18 October, about 17 times normal and more than the lead that day. Kim Woo-seok, playing the intern, rose by a similar multiple. Korean entertainment press reported Kim Woo-seok topping search keywords for two weeks running.

## What this does and does not say

A remake usually borrows an audience. Here, the traffic also ran the other way. English-language viewers met the Korean version first, then went to read about the Japanese series behind it, which began in 2012. Wikipedia reads count look-ups, not viewers. They cannot tell us whether anyone then went on to watch the original.
