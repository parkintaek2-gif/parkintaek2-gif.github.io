We track how much English-language attention K-pop performers get by pulling English Wikipedia
pageviews for a panel built from Wikidata: anyone with South Korean citizenship working as a
singer, rapper, composer or musician, plus members of groups registered as originating in South
Korea. It's a clean rule, and on 15 September 2026 it matched 2,371 people and groups with zero
left unresolved.

While reviewing a current list of prominent K-pop acts this week, we checked whether Lisa — of
Blackpink, one of the most internationally visible K-pop performers by any casual measure — was
in that panel. She wasn't.

## What we found when we pulled her by hand

Her real English Wikipedia title is "Lisa (rapper)." Over the same 30-day window our panel uses
(17 August-15 September 2026), her page drew **151,508 views** — an average of 5,050 a day.

That is higher than every individual BTS member our panel tracks over the identical window:

| Name | 30-day total | Daily average |
|---|---|---|
| **Lisa (pulled by hand)** | **151,508** | **5,050** |
| Jung Kook | 110,341 | 3,678 |
| V | 107,190 | 3,573 |
| Jennie (Lisa's Blackpink bandmate) | 100,000 | 3,333 |
| RM | 62,637 | 2,088 |
| Jimin | 61,069 | 2,036 |
| Suga | 53,713 | 1,790 |
| Jin | 55,153 | 1,838 |
| J-Hope | 45,931 | 1,531 |

Lisa's total is not higher than BTS's seven members combined (496,034 added by hand from the
table above). But individually, she outreads all seven of them, and her own bandmate, over the
same 30 days.

## Why she wasn't in our panel

Not an oversight — a rule doing exactly what we told it to do. Our Wikidata query screens for
South Korean citizenship. Lisa, born Lalisa Manobal, holds Thai citizenship. She debuted, trained
and built her career inside a South Korean group, but our citizenship filter has no way to know
that; it only checks the passport field.

We haven't yet gone back and counted how many other non-Korean members of Korean-registered
groups this same filter drops. Lisa surfaced because we happened to look for her by name this
week, not because we ran a systematic check. That check is next; we're not estimating a number
for it here.

Our running method for checking what actually moves English Wikipedia reading is on
[what-moves-english-reading](/what-moves-english-reading).
