SM Entertainment said on 17 September that Riize's 2024 single "Love 119" had passed 100 million streams on Spotify — the second song by the six-member group to reach that mark, after "Get a Guitar." The group marked the news at a fan meetup in Incheon held for their third anniversary.

We checked what the announcement did to how often people looked Riize up on English Wikipedia. It didn't move the number up. It coincided with the lowest reading day in the 30-day window we checked.

Over the 29 days before the announcement (19 August-16 September), Riize's English Wikipedia page averaged 411 views a day. On 17 September, the day of the streaming news, it drew 301 views — 0.73x average, and the single quietest day of the entire month we measured.

The busiest day in that same window was 29 August, at 947 views (2.30x baseline) — we found no news event naming Riize that day, so it stays in our record as unexplained rather than attached to any story.

Two Spotify milestones from two different acts landed on the same day this week — Jimin's "Like Crazy" and Riize's "Love 119." Neither showed up as a rise in English Wikipedia reading. For Riize, the milestone day was, if anything, the quietest one we measured.
