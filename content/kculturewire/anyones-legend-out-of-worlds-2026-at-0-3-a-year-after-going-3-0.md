**Anyone's Legend** are out of the 2026 World Championship. **Team Liquid Alienware** beat them 2-0 in a best-of-three between two winless teams at 07:00 Korea time on 27 October, and Anyone's Legend leave the Swiss Stage at **0-3**.

A year ago they went the other way. In 2025 Anyone's Legend beat Hanwha Life, Gen.G and CTBC Flying Oyster to qualify at 3-0, the second team into the quarter-finals, where they lost. This year they lost to Karmine Corp, Top Esports and now Team Liquid.

## The two games, from Riot's live stats

| Game | Winner | Kills | Gold | Towers | Barons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Team Liquid** | 16–5 | 69,657–55,504 | 9–2 | 2–0 |
| 2 | **Team Liquid** | 18–18 | 91,629–82,484 | 11–3 | 2–0 |

Game 2 was level on kills, 18 each. Team Liquid led by about 9,000 gold and took 11 towers to 3 and both barons. Team Liquid, with CoreJJ at support, move to 1-2.

## Who has gone 0-3

| Year | Out at 0-3 | League |
|---|---|---|

| 2025 | Fnatic, PSG Talon | LEC, LCP |
| 2026 | Team Secret Whales, **Anyone's Legend** | LCP, **LPL** |

In the two earlier years we can count, no team from Korea's LCK or China's LPL went 0-3. Anyone's Legend are the first in Riot's records for these three Swiss Stages. Two years is a short record; 2023 is missing from the feed.

## How 0-2 teams have done

Eight teams fell to 0-2 in 2024 and 2025. **None qualified.** Two came closest, winning twice to reach 2-2 before losing: Team Liquid in 2024 and Movistar KOI in 2025.

Team Liquid were one of those eight in 2024, and they are at 1-2 again now. LOS, who knocked out Team Secret Whales earlier the same morning, are also 1-2. That count describes two past fields; it is not odds for either team.

The LPL now has three teams left, all at 2-1 ([where every league stands](/article/worlds-2026-after-three-rounds-lck-two-through-lpl-none)). Every team's record, and where teams at 2-1, 1-2 and 0-2 ended up in past years, is on [our Worlds 2026 page](/worlds-2026).
