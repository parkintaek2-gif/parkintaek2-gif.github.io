**Go Min-si** plays Yeon-joo, an investigator, in ***Dead-End Job***, the Netflix horror series that arrived on **30 October 2026** with Lee Jae-wook in the lead. It is her second series in a row from creator **Han Jun-hee**, after *Tastefully Yours* in 2025 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead-End_Job)).

We matched every title in her English Wikipedia filmography against Netflix's weekly top 10 for 93 countries, every week since July 2021.

Not on any list: *Youth of May*, *Secret Boutique*, *The Smile Has Left Your Eyes*, *Meloholic*, *Hello, My Twenties! 2* and the series *Live*.

Three series sit close together at the top, from 39 to 54 countries. Then the record drops to 8, and after that to single countries. Her films reached one country each, though *Smugglers* was number 1 in Thailand.

## Tastefully Yours went widest, fastest

*Tastefully Yours* was in **53 countries in a single week**, the week ending 18 May 2025, and number 1 somewhere in 17 of them. That is the widest any of her titles has been at one time. Its creator, Han Jun-hee, is also the creator of *Dead-End Job*.

In the seven days to 29 October (UTC), the last day Wikimedia had published, the **Go Min-si** article was read **5,084** times across 21 language editions. ## What this does and does not say

Chart positions are not audiences, and the lists start in July 2021. A wide record does not say how *Dead-End Job* will do, and its first eligible Netflix week ends on 1 November.
