We hold a panel of **634 people** who appear in Korean titles that charted on Netflix. It is the
spine of most of what we publish about performers.

Separately, we rank **300 Korean entertainers** by how many people opened their English Wikipedia
page in the 30 days to 20 August 2026.

Put the two side by side and the panel has a hole in it.

| Rank by reads | Name | Reads, 30 days | In our Netflix panel |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | So Ji-sub | 170,257 | yes |
| 2 | Jung Hae-in | 164,142 | yes |
| 3 | **V** | 126,321 | **no** |
| 4 | Park Eun-bin | 124,423 | yes |
| 5 | **Jennie** | 121,980 | **no** |
| 6 | **Ken Jeong** | 119,370 | **no** |
| 7 | **Jungkook** | 113,190 | **no** |
| 8 | **Steven Yeun** | 113,106 | **no** |
| 9 | **Arden Cho** | 98,410 | **no** |
| 10 | **Eric Nam** | 91,525 | **no** |
| 11 | **Jisoo** | 89,061 | **no** |
| 12 | **Nam Joo-hyuk** | 84,955 | **no** |

**Nine of the top twelve** are outside it. So are 13 of the top 20, and 115 of the 300 — together
**3,411,955 reads, or 38.8% of all the reading** in this set.

## Two different reasons, and they should not be mixed

The names outside the panel are not one group.

**Idols.** V, Jennie, Jungkook, Jisoo, ROSÉ, Jimin. They are read heavily and they do not appear in
the cast of Korean titles that reached a Netflix weekly top 10. Our panel is built from those casts,
so it cannot see them.

**Korean-American actors.** Ken Jeong, Steven Yeun, Arden Cho, Daniel Dae Kim. They work mostly
outside Korean productions. The panel is Korean titles, so it cannot see them either — for a
completely different reason.

Reporting one figure for both would be wrong. The first group is a gap in what Netflix charts
measure. The second is a gap in what "Korean title" means.

## The hole is not made of small names

If the people outside the panel were minor, this would be a footnote. They are not.

**ROSÉ has an article in 86 Wikipedias.** **Jennie has 78.** The median across all 300 names we
measure is **25**. Taking the two groups as wholes, the 115 outside the panel sit at a median of
**27 editions** and the 185 inside at **24** — the outside group is, if anything, written about in
slightly more languages.

## What we are going to do about it

Nothing yet, and we would rather say so than publish a page with four facts on it.

We hold reads, edition counts, birth dates and rank for these 115 names. We do not hold what we
hold for the other 185 — chart runs, countries reached, weeks on a list, who they appeared beside.
A page built only from the first set would be thin, and thin pages are how a data site stops being
worth reading.

Our panel's shape came from the data that was easiest to get, not from what people actually look
up. Writing that down is the first step to fixing it.
