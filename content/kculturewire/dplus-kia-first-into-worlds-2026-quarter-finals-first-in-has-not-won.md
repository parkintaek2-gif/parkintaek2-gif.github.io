**Dplus Kia** are the first team into the quarter-finals of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship. They beat Movistar KOI 2-0 in a best-of-three at 04:00 Korea time on 26 October, and finished the Swiss Stage at **3-0**, without dropping a series.

Yesterday's records put them and Gen.G one win away ([round three pairings](/article/worlds-2026-swiss-round-three-schedule-who-plays-for-what)). Dplus Kia got there first.

## The two games, from Riot's live stats

| Game | Dplus Kia side | Kills | Gold | Towers | Barons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Red | **16**–8 | **79,778**–69,681 | **9**–2 | **2**–0 |
| 2 | Blue | **17**–5 | **56,758**–43,670 | **9**–1 | **1**–0 |

In game 2 Dplus Kia also took all three dragons. Over the two games Movistar KOI destroyed three towers.

## What "first in" has meant before

Riot's schedule lets us put every Swiss Stage match in order and see when each team reached its third win. In the two Swiss Stages we could count fully, 2024 and 2025, this is how the first team in fared, and where the champion came from.

| Year | First in | Record | Went out in | Champion's place in the order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | LNG | 3-0 | Quarter-final | T1, **fifth**, at 3-1 |
| 2025 | KT Rolster | 3-0 | Final (lost to T1) | T1, **eighth and last**, at 3-2 |

Of the four teams that went 3-0 in those two years, two went out in the quarter-finals, one in the semi-finals and one in the final. None won the title.

## This is a count, not a forecast

Two years is a very small sample. It does not say a 3-0 start is a disadvantage, and it does not say anything about Dplus Kia's chances. It says that in the last two fields, the order in which teams qualified and the order in which they finished were different lists. The full order for both years is on [our Worlds 2026 page](/worlds-2026).

## Who is next

Gen.G, the other LCK team at 2-0, play LYON at 07:00 KST on 26 October for the second quarter-final spot. Hanwha Life lost to Invictus Gaming and are 1-2; T1 are 1-1 and play CTBC Flying Oyster on 27 October.
