Netflix marked ten years in Korea with a fan event in Seoul on 24 and 25 October 2026, and one of the titles it brought out was **All of Us Are Dead season 2**. The cast reunited on stage and the character Cheong-san was confirmed alive, but Netflix gave **no teaser and no release window** ([What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/k-dramas/all-of-us-are-dead-season-2-korea-10-year-anniversary-event/)).

So how big was season 1? Netflix publishes a weekly top 10 for each of 93 countries. We have every list since July 2021.

## All 93 countries in one week

In the chart week dated **6 February 2022**, its second on the charts, All of Us Are Dead was in the TV top 10 of **all 93 countries** Netflix publishes. Among the 401 Korean series that have reached any of these charts, only one other did that:

| Korean series | Most countries in one week | Weeks on any chart |
|---|---|---|
| **All of Us Are Dead** | **93** | 34 |
| Squid Game | 93 | 60 |
| Hellbound | 92 | 9 |
| My Name | 91 | 9 |
| The Glory | 91 | 29 |

Squid Game's 60 weeks include all of its seasons, which share one title on the chart.

## Most Korean series reach a handful of countries

The typical Korean series on these charts reached **5 countries** and stayed **6 weeks**. 119 of the 401 reached only one country. Five reached 90 or more.

## Where it stayed

In 66 of the 93 countries it stayed six weeks or fewer; the median was six. A few kept it much longer:

| Country | Weeks in the top 10 | Last week |
|---|---|---|
| India | 28 | 28 May 2023 |
| Pakistan | 24 | 21 May 2023 |
| Bangladesh | 22 | 9 October 2022 |
| Bolivia | 16 | 4 February 2024 |
| Philippines | 11 | **5 January 2025** |

Its last chart week, in the Philippines, came almost three years after release. Of its 619 country-weeks on the chart, 607 were in 2022.

## What this does and does not say

A top-10 place is a position, not an audience: third in Belgium and third in Brazil are not the same number of people. The chart shows how widely and how long season 1 was watched relative to everything else, not how many watched it. It says nothing about how season 2 will do.
