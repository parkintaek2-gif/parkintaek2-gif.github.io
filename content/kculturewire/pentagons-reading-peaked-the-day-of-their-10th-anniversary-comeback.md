Pentagon released "Coward" (겁쟁이) on 11 September 2026 — their first group single since 2023,
timed to their 10th anniversary. That same calendar day was also the group's single highest
English Wikipedia reading day across our full 30-day window: 595 views, against a 30-day
average of 272 a day.

## Before and after the comeback

| | Views/day |
|---|---:|
| First 7 days of our window (21-27 Aug) | 189 |
| Last 7 days (13-19 Sep, comeback week) | **488 (2.6x)** |
| Single highest day (11 Sep, release date) | 595 |

A three-year gap between group activities makes this a different kind of comeback than a
routine annual release, and the reading data lines up with a real audience response on the day
itself rather than building gradually beforehand or after.

## What we are not claiming

We do not have streaming numbers or chart data for "Coward," and cannot say how the single
performed commercially. We also cannot separate, with this measure alone, how much of the rise
is the song itself versus the "10 years together" framing that accompanied it — both arrived on
the same date.

## Method

We compared the first 7 days of a 30-day window (21 August-19 September 2026) against the last
7 days for a 2,371-person K-pop reading panel, and checked whether the single highest-viewed
day for this act fell on a dated public release. Not investment advice.
