Samsung Electronics published its full third-quarter results on **Thursday 29 October**. Operating profit was **107.4 trillion won** on revenue of **195.3 trillion**, the figures it had previewed on 8 October. Etoday reported the split:

| Division | Q3 2026 operating profit (trillion won) |
|---|---|
| Device Solutions (chips) | **106.5** |
| Device eXperience (phones, TVs, appliances) | **−0.8** |
| Samsung Display | 1.1 |
| Harman | 0.6 |
| **Company total** | **107.4** |

Chips made **99%** of the total. Memory revenue alone was 140.7 trillion won.

## What the shares did

| Date | Close (won) | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Wed 23 Sep (highest in our record) | 285,500 | |
| Thu 15 Oct (lowest) | 210,000 | |
| Tue 27 Oct | 222,000 | +4.23% |
| Wed 28 Oct (SK hynix results) | 242,000 | +9.01% |
| **Thu 29 Oct** (Samsung results) | **232,000** | **−4.13%** |

The stock opened higher on Thursday and reached 249,000 won before turning down, Electronic Times reported. The preferred shares fell 3.26%. The record profit had been public since the 8 October preview. Electronic Times quoted Toss Securities' Lee Young-gon pointing to the shareholder-return plan: Samsung said 2026 returns will total **90 to 110 trillion won**, but the size and method of further buybacks will be set at a board meeting in **late January 2027**.

On the same two days SK hynix rose 10.89% and 2.62% ([SK hynix after its results](/article/sk-hynix-target-prices-after-record-q3-results-october-2026)).
