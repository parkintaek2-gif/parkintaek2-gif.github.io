Which Korean companies do brokers write about most? We counted a month of research in the Hankyung Consensus list: **710 reports** from 12 publishers, **313** of them about a single company.

They covered **172 companies**. The answer at the top is not Samsung Electronics.

## The most-covered, 23 September–24 October 2026

| Company | Reports | Brokers | Target raised | Target cut | Unchanged | No prior listed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LS Electric** | **9** | 6 | 0 | **4** | 4 | 1 |
| Samsung E&A | 8 | 7 | **6** | 0 | 2 | 0 |
| Samsung Biologics | 8 | 5 | 0 | 2 | 5 | 1 |
| Hyundai Rotem | 8 | 6 | 0 | 4 | 1 | 3 |
| Samsung Electro-Mechanics | 6 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 2 | 3 |
| HYBE | 6 | 6 | 0 | 4 | 1 | 1 |

| Korean Air | 5 | 4 | 3 | 0 | 2 | 0 |

| LG Display | 4 | 3 | 0 | **4** | 0 | 0 |

Samsung Electronics also drew four reports, like SK hynix ([what they said](/article/samsung-electronics-sk-hynix-target-prices-october-2026)).

## Two directions at the top

**Samsung E&A**, the plant engineering company, got **six raises and no cuts**. One title on 23 October read "반복되는 서프라이즈", a repeated surprise. **LS Electric**, the power-equipment maker, got **four cuts and no raises** even though its titles were bullish ("AI발 배전시장 초호황", an AI-driven boom in power distribution). Its new targets run from 235,000 to 310,000 won.

**LG Display** was cut in all four of its reports.

**106 of 172** covered companies, 62%, got exactly one report in a month. Coverage clusters on a few names. Most listed companies get none in a given month; Korea has more than 2,500 listed stocks.

And across all 710 reports, **none said Sell**. (On how rare Sell ratings are in Korea, see [twenty-six sell ratings](/article/twenty-six-sell-ratings).)
