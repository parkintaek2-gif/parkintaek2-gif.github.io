Gold on the Korea Exchange closed at **175,250 won a gram** on 21 October, the lowest close in the 75 trading days we hold since 1 July. It peaked at **208,300 won on 25 August**. From that peak the price is down **15.9%**.

A falling gold price on the KRX is not new. [In August we wrote](/article/korea-exchange-gold-down-from-january-peak) that Korean gold had fallen 31% from January while trading in it went up, not down. This time the volume is falling too.

## Price and volume, month by month

| Month (2026) | First close | Last close | Average daily volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| July | 197,190 | 187,460 | 219kg |
| August | 186,430 | 196,480 | 246kg |
| September | 196,280 | 182,940 | 197kg |
| October (to 21st) | 183,480 | 175,250 | 162kg |

August was the busiest month of the four: the price rose into the 25 August peak and the exchange traded an average of 246kg a day. Since then both lines have gone down together. October's average, 162kg a day, is **a third below August's**. The value traded in the first three weeks of October, 378 billion won, is less than half of September's full-month total.

## The big days came in late summer

The five largest one-day moves in the window were all in July, August and September. The biggest fall was **−3.22% on 31 August**, a week after the peak, and the biggest rise was +3.09% on 20 August. October has had none of that size: the price has slid without a single large day.

## What this does not say

This is the Korean exchange's own won price. It moves with the dollar price of gold and with the won–dollar exchange rate, and we have not split the fall between the two. Part of the drop in volume may also be calendar: the Chuseok holiday fell inside this window and shortens trading. The count does not say where gold goes next.

What it does show is a change in behaviour. In the earlier decline, Korean buyers traded more as prices fell. Since late August they have been trading less.
