A Korean company can list two kinds of share. The common carries a vote. The **preferred** (its Korean name ends in the character for "pref"; of the 111 on the KOSPI, 88 have a code ending in 5, 7 or 9 and 23 end in a letter) normally carries no vote and a dividend preference. Ask how much cheaper the preferred is and most answers give one example, usually Samsung Electronics.

We measured all of them. On 23 October 2026 the KOSPI had **111 preferred listings** worth **157.9 trillion won**, 3.45% of the market.

## The spread of discounts

We divided each preferred's closing price by its common's.

| Preferred ÷ common | Listings |
|---|---|
| below 0.5 | 27 |
| 0.5 – 0.6 | 10 |
| 0.6 – 0.7 | 14 |
| 0.7 – 0.8 | 9 |
| 0.8 – 0.9 | 10 |
| 0.9 – 1.0 | 5 |
| **above 1.0** | **36** |

The median is **0.748**: the typical KOSPI preferred sells for about three quarters of its common. But there is no typical shape. A quarter of them are below 0.531, and a quarter are above 1.158.

## Big preferreds sit at a discount; the premiums are small and quiet

| Group | Listings | Median ratio | Above the common |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worth 100 billion won or more | 29 | **0.535** | **0** |
| Worth less | 82 | 0.870 | 36 |

Every preferred that trades **above** its common is small. The median one of the 36 was worth 4.6 billion won and traded **6 million won** on 23 October. The highest ratios, 6.49 for Namsun Aluminum's preferred and 6.44 for Tongyang's, came on 8 million and 17 million won of trading. A price that barely trades is a weak signal of value.

Some large ones, on 23 October:

| Preferred | Ratio to common | Market value (trillion won) |
|---|---|---|
| Samsung Electronics | 0.779 | 134.7 |
| Hyundai Motor (2nd pref B) | 0.540 | 5.46 |
| Hyundai Motor | 0.535 | 3.61 |
| LG Electronics | 0.371 | 1.00 |
| Samsung Electro-Mechanics | 0.348 | 1.14 |
| Amorepacific | 0.294 | 0.40 |

The deepest discount was Doosan Fuel Cell's first preferred, at **0.167**.

Samsung Electronics' preferred alone is 85.3% of the value of all 111, which is why "the preferred discount" in Korea often just means Samsung's ([Samsung's preferred-common gap in October](/article/samsung-electronics-preferred-common-gap-narrowest-q3-results-2026)).

## How to read it

A ratio below 1 is the usual price of giving up a vote. A ratio far above 1 on a few million won of trading is mostly a statement about how few shares changed hands. Neither is a signal to act on. This is the distribution, not a recommendation.
