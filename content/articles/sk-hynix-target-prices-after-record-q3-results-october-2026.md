SK hynix reported on **Wednesday 28 October**. YTN put its third-quarter operating profit at about **83.3 trillion won** on revenue of about **104.6 trillion**, the first quarter above 100 trillion. Brokers had expected about **76 trillion**. The operating margin was **79.7%**.

It also declared a Q3 dividend of **20,000 won a share**, half regular and half special, and a new **20 trillion won** share buyback.

Two days took the stock up **13.8%** from Tuesday's close. It was still **18.3%** below its 21 September close. The KOSPI rose 6.64% on Wednesday, and SK hynix is about a fifth of the index's market value ([the weighing](/article/sk-hynix-q3-results-share-of-kospi-value-and-trading-october-2026)).

## The distance to the price

Against the 29 October close of 1,526,000 won, the median new target is **2.08 times** the price. The lowest, DS Investment's 2,640,000, is 1.73 times. The highest, 4,000,000 from SK Securities and Kyobo, is 2.62 times.

The gap is not only a product of October's fall. Before results, the eight brokers in our [SK hynix company file](/company/sk-hynix) had a median latest target of 3,400,000 won. Even at the 21 September close of 1,868,000, the highest in our record, that was **1.82 times** the price.

The post-results median is lower than the pre-results one, but mostly because the set of brokers changed: the targets that moved went both ways, and three of the eleven reports came from brokers with no earlier SK hynix target in our list.

## The dividend date

To receive the 20,000 won dividend, a shareholder must be on the register on the **30 November** record date. YTN reported that this means buying by **26 November**, because Korean trades settle two business days later. Korea now lets companies set the record date after the dividend is announced ([how that works](/article/korea-quarterly-dividend-record-date-rules-foreign-investors)).
