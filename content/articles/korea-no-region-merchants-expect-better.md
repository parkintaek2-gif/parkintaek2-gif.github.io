Each month, Korea's small merchants are asked a simple question: do you expect next month to be better or worse? Turn the answers into an index where **100 is neutral**, break it down by province, and the latest regional data says something stark: **in not one of Korea's 17 regions do small merchants, on balance, expect improvement.**

## Everywhere below neutral — by different amounts

The regional expectation readings run from **80.6 at the top (South Jeolla)** down to **64.5 at the bottom (Daejeon).** Every province sits below 100. Even the most hopeful region is nearly twenty points under neutral; the least hopeful is more than thirty.

The pattern cuts against the easy assumption that the capital does best. **Seoul is second from the bottom**, with Daejeon, Incheon and Sejong — all cities — clustered at the pessimistic end, while more rural Jeolla provinces sit relatively higher. Whatever is weighing on small merchants, it is not lighter in the big cities.

## About that "year-high" headline

You may have seen a cheerier number this week: a national expectation of **95.1**, a year-high. That figure is real, and it is *one month newer* than the regional breakdown here — it is the freshly released reading, not yet split by province. It is also still **below 100.** On the reproducible series, the month underneath it (national ~71) lines up with these regional readings; the 24-point jump to 95.1 is a hope for the month after. A record that still sits under neutral, and a regional map with no province above it, are the same story told at two zoom levels.

## Why we publish the map, not the headline

A single national number — 71, or 95.1 — flattens a country into one mood. The regional breakdown shows the mood is bad everywhere and *unevenly* bad, in a pattern (cities low, some provinces higher) that a headline erases. As with our [national expectation-vs-reality read](https://seoulmarkets.com/article/korea-small-merchants-never-neutral), the honest version keeps what the average throws away. **This is a map of expectations, not a ranking of places.**
