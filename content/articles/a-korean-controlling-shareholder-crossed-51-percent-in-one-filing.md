A 5%-ownership disclosure filed with Korea's Financial Supervisory Service on 8 September, 2026 shows
Hantop's (KOSDAQ: 002680) reporting shareholder group, led by Ryu Won-gi, crossing 51% of the company
in a single filing &mdash; a 9.16-percentage-point jump from the prior reported level.

## What the filing says

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Shares held after change | 4,192,386 |
| Change in shares held | +1,292,671 |
| Stake after change | 51.56% |
| Change in stake | +9.16 points |
| Related-party shares (subset of total) | 1,430,641 (17.59%) |

## What the filing's own reason line says

The disclosure gives one combined reason, translated: "acquisition of new shares by a related party
through a third-party private placement, and a change in the number of that related party's shares
pledged as collateral for a stock-backed loan." Those are two different kinds of event &mdash; a
purchase of new shares, and a change in how many already-held shares are pledged against debt &mdash;
reported together in a single filing, without the percentage-point increase split between them.

## What we're not saying

We have not obtained the private placement's price or size, or the terms of the loan the pledged
shares secure — both would require separate filings. We also are not characterizing this as a formal
change of controlling shareholder; that designation, where it matters for Korean disclosure rules, is
made in a different filing type, not this ownership report.
