Cellid (KOSDAQ: 299660) filed a decision to raise capital through a third-party share placement with
Korea's Financial Supervisory Service on 21 September, 2026. The filing adds 4,796,163 new common
shares to a pre-issuance base of 29,502,977 &mdash; 16.26% dilution by the filing's own two figures.

## What the filing says

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| New shares issued | 4,796,163 |
| Shares outstanding before issuance | 29,502,977 |
| Dilution | 16.26% |
| Face value per share | &#8361;500 |
| Issuance method | Third-party allotment (제3자배정증자) |
| Use of proceeds — operating funds | &#8361;9,999,999,855 |
| Use of proceeds — every other listed category | &#8361;0 |

Every won of the roughly 10 billion won raised is booked to one line: general operating funds. The
filing's use-of-proceeds table lists five other categories &mdash; facility investment, business
acquisition, debt repayment, other securities purchase, and a miscellaneous line &mdash; and all five
read zero.

## What we did not verify

The structured API response we pulled this from does not carry the name of the party receiving the
new shares, or the issue price. Both are disclosed elsewhere in the full DART filing body, which we
did not parse for this piece. We also have no information here on why Cellid needs the funds, or how
this raise compares to its recent cash position — a single capital-increase filing describes one
event, not the company's overall financial health.
