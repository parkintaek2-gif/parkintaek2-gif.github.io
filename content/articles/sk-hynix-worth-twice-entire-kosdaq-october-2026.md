KOSDAQ fell **3.26%** on Monday, 26 October, to **727.21**, its lowest close since 31 July, according to Newspim. KOSPI fell 1.46% the same day. Institutions sold a net 131.8 billion won of KOSDAQ shares; individuals and foreigners were small net buyers.

For most of October, the order had been the other way: KOSDAQ held up better. Here is what our daily KRX files show up to Friday.

## The whole market versus one company

On Friday, 23 October, the **1,824 KOSDAQ listings** were worth **425.8 trillion won** together. SK hynix, one KOSPI company, was worth **932.8 trillion**: 2.19 times the entire KOSDAQ ([SK hynix's place in the KOSPI](/article/sk-hynix-q3-results-share-of-kospi-value-and-trading-october-2026)).

KOSDAQ's ten biggest names are 17.5% of its value. The largest, Alteogen, was 15.6 trillion won, less than SK hynix's daily swing on some October days.

## October: held value, lost trading

| | 30 Sep | High in October | 23 Oct |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSDAQ market value (trillion won) | 481.9 | 518.3 (6 Oct) | 425.8 |
| KOSDAQ share of KOSPI + KOSDAQ value | 7.87% | 8.79% (20 Oct) | 8.50% |
| KOSDAQ share of combined trading | 27.5% | 35.6% (2 Oct) | 25.1% |

From 30 September to 23 October, KOSDAQ's market value fell **11.6%** and KOSPI's **18.7%**. KOSDAQ's slice of the combined market grew.

Trading went the other way. In the first days of October, more than a third of all won traded on the two boards went through KOSDAQ. By mid-October the share had dropped to **19.7%** (15 October). That was the day SK hynix fell 11.06% and Samsung Electronics 9.48%, and KOSPI trading reached 28.64 trillion won, its October high in our files. It was 25.1% on Friday.

Breadth was weak even before Monday. On 23 October, **1,248** KOSDAQ listings fell and **404** rose.

## What Monday changed

A 3.26% fall in KOSDAQ against 1.46% in KOSPI narrows the gap that built up through October. We will have Monday's stock-by-stock file in our next update; until then, the Monday figures above are the wire services', not ours.
