The KOSPI closed at 5,533.77 on 22 October, **19.1% below** where it ended September. That is a large fall in three weeks. But it did not fall evenly.

## Big stocks fell five times as far as small ones

| 30 September → 22 October | Change |
|---|---|
| KOSPI Large Cap (100 stocks) | **−20.1%** |
| KOSPI | −19.1% |
| KOSPI Mid Cap (200 stocks) | −8.1% |
| KOSPI excluding KOSPI 200 (625 stocks) | −6.7% |
| KOSPI Small Cap (526 stocks) | **−4.1%** |

The 100 largest companies lost a fifth of their value. The 526 smallest lost a twenty-fifth. The KOSPI is weighted by market value, so it moves with the large caps, and in October [the large caps are mostly chips](/article/kospi-is-a-semiconductor-index).

## Where the falls were

| Index | October |
|---|---|
| KOSPI 200 Information Technology | **−24.7%** |
| KOSPI 200 Heavy Industries | −23.7% |
| Electrical & Electronics | −23.6% |
| Machinery & Equipment | −21.9% |
| Finance | −15.8% |
| Chemicals | −9.3% |
| Pharmaceuticals | **+1.2%** |
| Food, Beverages & Tobacco | **+4.1%** |
| Entertainment & Culture | **+6.9%** |

Of 53 KOSPI-series indices, **three rose**. Across all 171 Korea Exchange indices we compared, seven did.

## The October winners were this year's losers

Entertainment and culture is the best KOSPI index of October, up 6.9%. It is also down **40.5% for the year**. Electrical and electronics, one of October's worst, is still up **73.1%** for the year. That pattern holds across all 53 KOSPI-series indices:

| Index's year to date at 30 September | Indices | Median change in October |
|---|---|---|
| Up for the year | 37 | **−15.0%** |
| Down for the year | 16 | **−3.6%** |

The October fall has landed hardest on what had risen, and least on what had already fallen.

[We also counted](/article/kospi-up-four-in-five-stocks-fall-breadth-october-2026) that most KOSPI stocks have fallen far less than the index since late August. The sector and size indices show the same thing from another side.

## What this does not say

Index changes over three weeks say where the losses were, not why, and not what comes next. A small-cap index that fell 4% still fell. And a sector that rose in October can be far below where it started the year.
