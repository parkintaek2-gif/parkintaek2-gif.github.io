SK Biopharmaceuticals (KRX: 326030) filed a single sale/supply contract disclosure on 23 September,
2026, worth 9.0% of its most recent annual revenue by the filing's own stated ratio. The filing names
the counterparty's relationship to the company directly: the buyer is SK Biopharmaceuticals' own
US subsidiary.

## What the filing says it's for

Field 9 of the disclosure ("other matters material to investment judgment") states the contract
supplies cenobamate — marketed as XCOPRI® — for sale in the US market. XCOPRI is SK
Biopharmaceuticals' own epilepsy treatment, and SK Life Science, Inc. is the subsidiary through which
the company markets it in the United States. Read alongside the "subsidiary" relationship field, this
reads as an intercompany manufacturing-to-marketing supply arrangement rather than a new external
customer.

## What a related-party contract does and doesn't tell you

A contract between a parent and its own subsidiary is disclosed under the same 2.5%-of-revenue rule
as a contract with an unrelated buyer, but it does not represent new demand from an outside customer
in the way an arm's-length contract would — the revenue ultimately depends on how much product the
subsidiary itself sells onward in the US market, which this filing does not report. The filing states
payment terms (no advance, balance due 120 days after delivery) but not a pricing methodology, so we
cannot independently assess whether the transfer price reflects an arm's-length rate.
