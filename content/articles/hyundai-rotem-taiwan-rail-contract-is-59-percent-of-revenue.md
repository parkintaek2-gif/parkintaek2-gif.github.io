Hyundai Rotem (KRX: 064350) filed a single sale/supply contract disclosure with Korea's Financial
Supervisory Service on 23 September, 2026, naming Taiwan's Taoyuan Department of Rapid Transit
Systems as the counterparty. The contract amount, stated in the filing itself, is 5.9% of the
company's most recent annual revenue.

## The filing, as stated

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Contract amount | ₩344,500,969,560 |
| Most recent annual revenue (filing's own figure) | ₩5,839,031,553,013 |
| Ratio | 5.9% |
| Counterparty | Department of Rapid Transit Systems, Taoyuan (Taiwan) |
| Contract start | 19 August 2026 |
| Contract end | 4 October 2034 |

The ratio in the table is the filing's own disclosed percentage — we recomputed it independently from
the two raw figures in the same document and it matches to one decimal place.

## An eight-year window, not a single delivery

The contract runs from 19 August 2026 to 4 October 2034, just over eight years. Korea's disclosure
rules require this kind of filing once a single contract crosses 2.5% of a company's most recent
revenue; this one clears that bar more than twice over. The filing does not provide a year-by-year
delivery or revenue-recognition schedule, so we cannot say how the ₩344.5 billion lands across those
eight years.

## What the filing does not resolve

The disclosure references an earlier, related filing from 28 July 2026 described as
"investment-judgment related" — Korean disclosure practice often has a preliminary announcement
followed later by a formal signed-contract filing. We did not open that July filing for this piece,
so we cannot say whether today's disclosure is the first public confirmation of new business or the
contract-execution step of something already flagged two months ago.
