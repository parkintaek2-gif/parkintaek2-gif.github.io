Korea's fund registry lists 183,351 funds. That number is less impressive than it looks — much of it is the same fund registered several times for its different share classes, and codes for funds long closed are never cleanly retired. But one thing the registry does record honestly is *when* each fund was set up, and read that way it shows an industry that has stopped multiplying the way it used to.

## The launch curve has rolled over

| Funds established | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| 2020 | **5,990** |
| 2021 | 4,060 |
| 2022 | 3,285 |
| 2023 | 3,197 |
| 2024 | 2,312 |
| 2025 | **2,698** |

New registrations peaked in 2020 near 6,000 and have run at roughly half that since. Some of that is share classes no longer being spun up as freely; some is genuinely fewer new products. Either way the direction is clear — the years when Korean asset managers launched a new fund every ninety minutes are behind them.

## What actually gets launched

Ask what a Korean fund is and the intuitive answer — a stock fund — is the wrong one. By count, the register is dominated by derivatives and bonds.

| Type | Funds |
| --- | ---: |
| Derivatives | 49,114 |
| Bond | 47,606 |
| Mixed-bond | 32,235 |
| Equity | 18,366 |
| Fund-of-funds | 13,956 |

Equity funds are just **one in ten**. The bulk of what the industry registers is fixed income and structured product — the plumbing of yield and hedging, not the stock-picking that gets the coverage.

## Where the themed money points

Most funds carry no theme at all; 108,496 of the 183,351 leave the field blank. Of those that do name something, the pattern is a map of where Korean savers look outward — and it points at America.

- **United States: 8,500** funds
- China: 3,202
- Japan: 864
- ESG: 949
- AI and robotics: 459
- Semiconductors: 133

The US-themed count is more than double China's and nearly ten times Japan's. And the themes that dominate the financial press — AI, robots, semiconductors, ESG — are, in the register, a rounding error next to a plain American index fund. What the industry talks about and what it actually stamps a code on are not the same list.
