HYBE filed its FY2025 annual report with Korea's Financial Supervisory Service on 20 March 2026. Revenue, on a consolidated basis, was ₩2.65 trillion — more than SM Entertainment, YG Entertainment and JYP Entertainment combined. Operating profit was ₩49.3 billion, positive. Net income was **negative ₩254.4 billion.**

## The four biggest listed K-pop agencies by revenue

| Company | Revenue (FY2025) | Operating profit | Net income |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| HYBE | ₩2.65tn | ₩49.3bn | **−₩254.4bn** |
| SM Entertainment | ₩1.17tn | ₩183.0bn | +₩359.4bn |
| JYP Entertainment | ₩821.9bn | ₩155.2bn | +₩160.6bn |
| YG Entertainment | ₩545.4bn | ₩71.3bn | +₩53.7bn |

*Consolidated (CFS) figures, DART FY2025 annual reports. HYBE's revenue alone exceeds SM, JYP and YG's combined.*

Three of the four turned a profitable year into a profitable bottom line. HYBE turned a profitable year — operating profit was positive, same as the other three — into the only loss among them, and a large one: bigger in magnitude than YG's entire net income, more than two-thirds the size of SM's.

## Where the ₩49.3bn went

DART's filing lets you walk the full distance from operating profit to net loss, line by line, and every number below is filed, not estimated:

| Line (filed) | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Operating profit | +₩49.3bn |
| Other income (기타수익) | +₩9.7bn |
| **기타비용 (other expenses)** | **−₩295.0bn** |
| Equity-method income | +₩4.1bn |
| Equity-method loss | −₩15.9bn |
| Financial income | +₩231.0bn |
| Financial cost | −₩160.6bn |
| = Pre-tax loss | −₩177.4bn |
| Tax | −₩77.0bn |
| = Net loss | **−₩254.4bn** |

Add the seven lines above operating profit's row and they reconcile exactly to HYBE's own filed pre-tax figure — we checked the arithmetic against DART's reported total, not the other way around. Financial income and financial cost roughly offset (net +₩70.4bn), and the equity-method lines are a minor net drag (−₩11.7bn). **One line does almost all of the work: ₩295.0 billion of "other expenses" — six times the size of operating profit itself.**

## The question the filing doesn't answer

DART's summary financial statement reports 기타비용 (other expenses) as a single total. It does not break out what is inside it — an impairment, a write-down, a legal settlement, a restructuring charge, or some combination — at the level of the filing we read. We looked for an itemisation in the same filing and did not find one; we did not go further into footnote-level disclosure to itemise it ourselves.

That is not a criticism of HYBE's disclosure — "other expenses" is a standard line in a Korean consolidated income statement, and plenty of large one-off items get filed under it before more detail follows in the full annual report's notes. It is simply the honest state of what a reader gets from the headline financial statement: a company that made money running its business, and a single unitemised line six times that size that took the year into loss.

This is a record of what four companies filed with DART for fiscal year 2025, not investment advice, and not a judgement on any of them — the open question, for now, stays open.
