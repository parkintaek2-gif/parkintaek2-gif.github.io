In most bond markets the longest bond pays the most. Japan's does not. On Japan's official yield curve the highest point has sat at **25 years**, above both the 30-year and the 40-year, on **30 of the 33 business days** since 1 September.

We can count that because we kept the numbers. Japan's Ministry of Finance publishes its constant-maturity yield curve as a file that holds only the current month; when a new month starts, the old days go. We have saved it every day since 1 September.

## Where the curve peaks

On 21 October the long end looked like this:

| Maturity | Yield, 21 Oct 2026 |
|---|---|
| 20 years | 3.726% |
| 25 years | **4.008%** |
| 30 years | 3.980% |
| 40 years | 3.994% |

The 25-year pays about 3 basis points more than the 30-year and 1 more than the 40-year. The gap is small, but it has held: on 30 days of 33, the 25-year was the top of the curve. On the other three, the 40-year was.

## Short end up, long end down

Over the same seven weeks the curve flattened from both ends.

| Maturity | 1 Sep | 21 Oct | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 year | 1.527% | 1.657% | +13.0bp |
| 2 years | 1.802% | 1.914% | +11.2bp |
| 5 years | 2.280% | 2.309% | +2.9bp |
| 10 years | 2.987% | 2.923% | −6.4bp |
| 20 years | 3.859% | 3.726% | −13.3bp |
| 30 years | 4.131% | 3.980% | −15.1bp |

One- and two-year yields rose by more than ten basis points. Twenty- and thirty-year yields fell by more than ten. The pivot sits between five and ten years. The gap between the 30-year and 10-year narrowed from 114 to 106 basis points.

## Why keep a file the ministry throws away

This is not hidden data; the ministry publishes it daily. But it publishes it one month at a time, and a question such as "how often did the 25-year top the curve this autumn?" needs every day, including the ones that have already dropped off the official file. Keeping them is the whole of the method.

We do not say here why the curve has this shape. Issuance by maturity, central-bank buying and the demand of long-term investors all play a part, and this count does not separate them. It establishes the shape and how steady it has been.
