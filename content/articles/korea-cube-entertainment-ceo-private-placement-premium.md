On 31 August 2026, Cube Entertainment filed a correction to a capital-raise decision it first made on 12 June. The company will issue 1,607,717 new shares in a third-party allotment (제3자배정증자) — a private placement rather than an offering to the public or existing shareholders — for total proceeds of ₩9,999,999,740, filed entirely as working capital. The allotment goes to one person: **Kang Seung-gon, one of the company's two co-CEOs, and the person the filing itself identifies as Cube Entertainment's largest shareholder.**

The pre-issue share count in DART's filing matches the Korea Exchange's own listed-shares figure for the same ticker on the same basis date exactly. The ₩6,220 placement price is not a number we calculated from a range: ₩9,999,999,740 divided by 1,607,717 shares comes out to precisely ₩6,220, meaning the round number is the filed price itself.

## The part that runs the other way

Private placements to a company's own executives and controlling shareholders are common in Korea, and the recurring concern with them — documented across many issuers, not specific to this one — is pricing: a placement priced at a discount to market hands the insider shares more cheaply than an outside investor would pay on the same day, diluting everyone else's stake in the process.

This one runs the other way. Cube Entertainment's KOSDAQ close on 3 September was ₩5,670. Kang Seung-gon's shares are priced at ₩6,220 — **9.7% above** that close, not below it. Measured on price alone, the company's own largest shareholder is paying more per share than the market was asking on the day we checked, to put ₩10 billion of his own money into the company he already controls.

## What the correction changed

The 31 August filing is not the original decision — it corrects one filed on 12 June. The filing's own change log states the correction is narrow: a change to the payment date, from 31 August to 30 October 2026. We did not find a stated reason for the two-month delay in the same document, and the number of shares, the price, and the use of proceeds are unchanged from what we read.

This is a record of what Cube Entertainment and its largest shareholder filed with DART, not a judgement on the placement or investment advice. The open items — Kang Seung-gon's resulting ownership share, and why the payment date moved — are not in this filing, and we have not gone further to find them.
