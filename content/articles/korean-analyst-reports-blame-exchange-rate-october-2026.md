The quarter's results are arriving with one word attached. In the month to 23 October, **17 company reports** from Korean brokers put the **exchange rate** (환율) in their title. None of the 17 raised a target price.

We searched every title in our archive of 710 broker reports, the same list behind the [target-change tape](/data/target-changes). On Friday 23 October the won closed the Seoul session at **1,324.6** per dollar, according to Korean wire reports.

## What the 17 did to targets

| Target | Reports |
|---|---|
| Cut | **4** |
| Unchanged | 8 |
| No prior target listed | 5 |
| Raised | **0** |
| **Total** | **17** |

For comparison, across all 313 company reports in the same list, targets were raised 62 times and cut 69 times. The currency reports leaned one way.

Five titles say the rate **fell**, meaning fewer won per dollar. For a company that sells in dollars and reports in won, that shrinks revenue on translation.

## Who reports this week

Two of the twelve companies report third-quarter results this week, according to the calendar published by The Asia Business Daily: **HD Hyundai Electric on 27 October** and **Samsung Heavy Industries on 29 October**. Hana Financial is not on that calendar. HD Hyundai Electric is the only company with three currency titles, and its four reports in the month span **650,000 to 1,165,000 won** ([the full earnings-week count](/article/korea-q3-earnings-week-october-26-30-2026-analyst-target-prices)).

The Taihan Cable cut from 61,000 to 33,000 is as the list shows it. Whether the prior target predates a change in share count, the list does not say.
