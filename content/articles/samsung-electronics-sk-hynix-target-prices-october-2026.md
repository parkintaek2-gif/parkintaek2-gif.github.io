Samsung Electronics and SK hynix carry the KOSPI. In the month to 24 October they drew **four company reports each** in the Hankyung Consensus list. LS Electric drew nine ([the most-covered list](/article/ls-electric-most-covered-korean-stock-october-2026-analyst-reports)).

Memory is mostly covered in industry reports, which this count leaves out. Here is what the company reports said.

## Samsung Electronics

| Date | Broker | Rating | Prior target (won) | Target (won) | Title |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29 Sep | Eugene | Strong Buy | 560,000 | 560,000 | 긴 호흡으로 (take the long view) |
| 2 Oct | IBK | Buy | — | 460,000 | 분기 영업이익 100조 시대가 열렸다 (the 100-trillion-won quarterly profit era has begun) |
| 6 Oct | SK | Buy | **300,000** | **610,000** | 현저한 저평가 (markedly undervalued) |
| 7 Oct | Meritz | Buy | 500,000 | 500,000 | 2027년 메모리 판가·실적 상향 조정 (raising 2027 memory prices and earnings) |

SK Securities **more than doubled** its target, the largest single change in either company's list. The four targets now run from **460,000 to 610,000 won**, a spread of a third between the lowest and highest.

| 20 Oct | Hanwha | — | — | — | SK하이닉스 솔리다임 상장 (on the Solidigm listing) |

Daishin's cut, from 3.9 million to 3.2 million won, is the only change with a prior target listed. The three targets run from **3.2 million to 4.0 million won**.

## How to read it

Two companies that make up a large share of the KOSPI drew eight company reports between them in a month, the same as Hyundai Rotem alone ([its targets were halved](/article/hyundai-rotem-target-prices-halved-october-2026)). The Meritz title gives one firm's expectation for SK hynix's third-quarter operating profit, 80.1 trillion won. It is a forecast, not a result.
