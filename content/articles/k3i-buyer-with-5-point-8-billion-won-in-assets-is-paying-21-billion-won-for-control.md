K3I (KRX: 431190) filed a change-of-control disclosure on 23 September, 2026. The buyer named in the
filing, J's Commerce Co., Ltd., is required by the same disclosure rules to report its own balance
sheet as part of the filing — and that balance sheet shows total assets of ₩5,836 million, well below
the ₩21 billion purchase price it has agreed to pay.

## The filing, as stated

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Shares transferred | 2,916,667 (39.96% of K3I's 7,486,442 shares outstanding) |
| Price per share | ₩7,200 |
| Total transaction value | ₩21,000,000,000 |
| Seller (pre-change largest shareholder) | Lee Jae-young, 37.40% pre-transfer |
| Buyer (prospective largest shareholder) | J's Commerce Co., Ltd., to hold 27.83% |
| Co-buyer | Galaxy No.1 Investment Association, to hold 11.13% |
| Deposit (10%) | ₩2.1 billion, paid 23 Sep 2026 |
| Balance (90%) | ₩18.9 billion, due 26 Nov 2026 |

## What the buyer's own numbers say

The filing includes a required section on the prospective largest shareholder's finances, and this
is where the gap shows up. J's Commerce, founded in May 2018, reports for fiscal 2025: ₩5,836 million
in total assets, ₩4,275 million in liabilities, ₩1,561 million in equity, and ₩6,869 million in
revenue. Against those figures, the ₩21 billion agreed purchase price — and specifically the
₩18.9 billion balance due by late November — is several times the buyer's own reported asset base.
The filing does not state how that balance will be funded.

## What a filing like this does and doesn't tell you

The disclosure confirms the transaction's terms, the parties, and the buyer's own recent financial
statements, all as self-reported to the regulator. It does not confirm whether external financing has
been arranged for the balance payment, nor whether the deal closes on schedule — the filing itself
notes that terms "may change" based on how the parties' discussions and related procedures proceed,
with any material change to be separately disclosed.
