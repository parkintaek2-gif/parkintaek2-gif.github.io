CMES Robotics (KRX: 475400) filed an amendment on 23 September, 2026 to a warehouse-automation
supply contract with Coupang that it originally disclosed on 27 February, 2026. The number moved in
one direction: down. The confirmed contract amount fell from ₩1,188,000,000 to ₩712,800,000 — a
40% reduction — and the stated ratio to revenue fell from 17.2% to 10.34%.

## The filing, as stated

| Field | Original (Feb 2026) | Revised (Sep 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed contract amount | ₩1,188,000,000 | ₩712,800,000 |
| Ratio to revenue | 17.2% | 10.34% |
| Original total contract value | Unchanged | Unchanged |
| Counterparty | Coupang Corp. | Coupang Corp. |
| Contract period | 28 Nov 2025 - 30 Jun 2026 | Same |

We recomputed the revised ratio from the revenue figure in the same filing (₩6,888,405,852) and it
matches the filing's own stated 10.34% to within normal rounding.

## What the filing says happened

The contract's stated end date, 30 June 2026, has passed. Per field 10 of the amendment, the company
could not confirm when the remaining balance would be paid, so it revised the disclosed contract
amount down to what has actually been collected so far — with the note that "the original contract
amount is unchanged" and remaining payments will follow the original payment terms (30% deposit,
30% on delivery/commissioning, 40% on final acceptance). In other words: the deal itself was not
renegotiated to a smaller size. What changed is which portion of it the company is now willing to
report as confirmed, given that the contract's own end date arrived before full collection.

## What a downward revision like this does and doesn't tell you

A revised-down disclosure amount, filed specifically because payment timing slipped past a
contract's end date, is a data point about cash collection pace on this one contract — not
necessarily about the underlying relationship or Coupang's total order book with CMES Robotics.
The filing does not say whether the remaining ~40% will be collected and, if so, when it would be
separately disclosed.
