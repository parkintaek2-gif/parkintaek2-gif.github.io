Third-quarter results are coming for Korea's K-pop agencies, and the brokers have written their previews. We counted every report on **HYBE, YG Entertainment, JYP Ent. and SM Entertainment** in the Hankyung Consensus list for the month to 24 October.

There were **15**. Every one said **Buy**.

## What happened to the targets

| Company | Reports | Target cut | Unchanged | Raised | No prior target listed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HYBE | 6 | **4** | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| YG Entertainment | 5 | **1** | 3 | 0 | 1 |
| JYP Ent. | 2 | **2** | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| SM Entertainment | 2 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| **Total** | **15** | **7** | **6** | **0** | **2** |

Seven cuts, no raises, no change of rating.

Both JYP reports now sit at **50,000 won**. One Eugene report on JYP was titled simply "보릿고개", a lean season.

## A Buy that stays a Buy

Cutting a target by a third and keeping the Buy rating is normal in Korean research. In our wider count, **not one of 728 reports** over a month said Sell ([as KOSPI slid in October, analysts cut more targets than they raised](/article/korea-analyst-target-price-cuts-october-2026)). A rating here is closer to a default than a call. The target price is where the opinion actually moves.

The same weeks were good for the sector on the market: the KOSPI entertainment index rose 6.9% in October while the KOSPI fell 19% ([details](/article/korea-entertainment-stocks-rose-october-2026-while-kospi-fell-19)). Analysts cut while prices held up.
