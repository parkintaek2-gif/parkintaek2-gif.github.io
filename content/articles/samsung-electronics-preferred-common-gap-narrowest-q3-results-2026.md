Samsung Electronics publishes its full third-quarter results on **Thursday, 29 October**. On 8 October it gave preliminary figures: about **195 trillion won** of revenue and **107.4 trillion won** of operating profit. Hankook Ilbo reported that the profit figure was above the 105.4 trillion market estimate.

Samsung has two listed share lines, and in October they did not fall together.

## Two lines, two falls

| | 30 Sep (won) | 23 Oct (won) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common (005930) | 268,500 | 215,500 | **−19.7%** |
| Preferred (005935) | 195,200 | 167,900 | **−14.0%** |

The preferred carries no vote. It usually trades at a discount to the common. In October the discount shrank.

## The ratio, session by session

We divided the preferred's close by the common's on each of the 38 sessions in our record, which starts on 27 August.

| Date | Preferred ÷ common |
|---|---|
| 31 Aug (lowest) | 0.717 |
| 30 Sep | 0.727 |
| 15 Oct | 0.758 |
| 19 Oct | **0.784** |
| 20 Oct | **0.784** |
| 23 Oct | 0.779 |

The monthly average went from **0.725** in late August to **0.749** in September and **0.757** in October. The two highest readings in our record came on 19 and 20 October, in the middle of the market's slide. On Monday the order flipped for a day: the common fell 1.16% and the preferred 2.20%, according to Newspim.

## Why it matters for the weighing

Counted as one company, Samsung Electronics was **30.43%** of KOSPI's market value on 23 October: 27.49 points from the common and **2.94** from the preferred. The preferred line alone is larger than every other KOSPI company except SK hynix, a point we [first counted in August](/article/korea-largest-listed-companies). A narrower discount means a slightly larger share of the market rides on Samsung than the common shares alone suggest ([the full earnings-week weighing](/article/kospi-earnings-week-28-companies-63-percent-of-market-value-october-2026)).

The ratio is a fact about two prices. Whether the gap should be wider or narrower is a judgement, and not ours to make.
