SK hynix reports third-quarter results on **Wednesday, 28 October**. The FnGuide consensus, as reported by Financial News on 18 October, is **76.96 trillion won** of operating profit on 98.23 trillion of revenue. Three months earlier the same consensus stood at 81.51 trillion. It came down 5.6%, and Financial News put that down to the won's strength cutting the won value of dollar sales.

We looked at a different number: how much of the Korean market SK hynix now is, by value and by trading.

## Value: down to a fifth

| Date | SK hynix close (won) | Share of KOSPI market value |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Sep | 1,776,000 | 23.01% |
| 20 Oct | 1,223,000 | **19.49%** |
| 23 Oct | 1,277,000 | 20.36% |

SK hynix fell **28.1%** from 30 September to 23 October. KOSPI's total market value fell 18.7%. So its share of the market shrank by 2.65 points. On Monday it closed at 1,275,000 won, little changed.

## Trading: still a quarter

Over the 15 KOSPI sessions from 1 to 23 October, **309.3 trillion won** of shares changed hands on the Korea Exchange. **77.4 trillion** of it, 25.0%, was SK hynix. Samsung Electronics, common and preferred together, was 74.0 trillion, or 23.9%. Samsung is the larger company by half again; SK hynix traded more.

| | Share of KOSPI value (23 Oct) | Share of KOSPI trading (1–23 Oct) |
|---|---|---|
| SK hynix | 20.36% | **25.0%** |
| Samsung Electronics (common + preferred) | 30.43% | 23.9% |

SK hynix's daily share of trading ran from **21.9%** on 1 October to **29.6%** on 21 October, the day KOSPI rebounded. It out-traded Samsung on 10 of the 15 sessions.

A stock that is a fifth of the market's value and a quarter of its turnover is where the market's argument is happening. Wednesday's numbers land on that.

*This counts value and trading, not what the results will be. Statistics, not advice.*
