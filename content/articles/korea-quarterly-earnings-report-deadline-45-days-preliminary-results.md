Korea's earnings season has two clocks. One is set by law. The other is set by companies.

## The legal clock: 45 days

Under Article 160 of the Financial Investment Services and Capital Markets Act, a company that files annual business reports must also file a **semi-annual report** and **quarterly reports** within **45 days** after the period ends. They go to the Financial Services Commission and the exchange, and are published on DART, the Financial Supervisory Service's disclosure system.

| Period | Ends | 45 days later |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 2026 | 31 Mar | 15 May |
| Half-year 2026 | 30 Jun | 14 Aug |
| **Q3 2026** | **30 Sep** | **14 Nov** |

Two allowances: consolidated filers get 60 days in their first two years, and Article 165 lets a company extend once a year by up to five business days if its auditor's review needs it. The annual business report runs on a separate, 90-day clock.

## The company clock: preliminary results

Many large Korean companies do not wait 45 days; every company on this week's calendar announces by day 30. A common route is to publish **preliminary results**, a short disclosure of revenue and operating profit, through the exchange's fair-disclosure system, and file the full quarterly report with the regulator later.

For the third quarter of 2026:

| Event | Date | Days after 30 Sep |
|---|---|---|
| Samsung Electronics preliminary results | 8 Oct | **8** |
| This week's 28 reporters (Asia Business Daily calendar) | 26–30 Oct | 26 to 30 |
| SK hynix results | 28 Oct | 28 |
| Samsung Electronics full results | 29 Oct | 29 |
| Legal deadline for the quarterly report | 14 Nov | 45 |

Samsung's preliminary figure, **107.4 trillion won** of operating profit, has been public for almost three weeks before its full results on Thursday ([Samsung's preferred-common gap, narrowest in our data](/article/samsung-electronics-preferred-common-gap-narrowest-q3-results-2026)).

## Why the calendar bunches

This week's calendar has 3 companies on Monday, 7 each on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and 4 on Friday. By market value it is far more lopsided: the Wednesday and Thursday reporters alone are 58.3% of KOSPI ([the full weighing](/article/kospi-earnings-week-28-companies-63-percent-of-market-value-october-2026)).

For a reader outside Korea, the practical point is the gap. A result announced on day 8 or day 28 is a company's own preliminary number. The quarterly report that the law requires, with full statements and notes, can arrive up to two weeks later, on DART.
