Korea Advanced Materials (KOSDAQ: 062970) filed two convertible bond issuance decisions with Korea's
Financial Supervisory Service on 2 September, 2026, both approved by the board the same day. Combined,
the two bonds are 20.62% dilution by their own stated figures &mdash; the fourth dilution filing we
checked in a three-week span that ranges from 8% to a bond worth more than the company's own share
count.

## The two Korea Advanced Materials filings, side by side

| Field | Bond A | Bond B |
|---|---|---|
| Face value | &#8361;5,000,000,000 | &#8361;9,700,000,000 |
| Conversion price | &#8361;2,366 | &#8361;2,366 |
| Convertible shares | 2,113,271 | 4,099,746 |
| Dilution (filing's own figure) | 7.01% | 13.61% |
| Maturity | 10 September 2029 | 10 September 2029 |

Same board date, same conversion price, same maturity &mdash; only the size differs. Combined, the
two filings' own percentages add to 20.62%.

## The other three, for comparison

We did not go looking for a pattern here; we found each of these independently while checking DART's
daily disclosure feed and its convertible-bond archive for material events. Laid side by side, the
range is wide:

| Company | Event | Combined dilution |
|---|---|---|
| Koramco Life Infra REIT | Two same-day convertible bonds, 21 Sep | 8.13% |
| Cellid | One third-party placement, 21 Sep | 16.26% |
| Korea Advanced Materials | Two same-day convertible bonds, 2 Sep | 20.62% |
| THE CODI | Two convertible bonds filed a day apart, 7-8 Sep | 176.78% |

THE CODI's is the outlier by a wide margin — its larger bond alone is worth more new shares than the
company had outstanding before the filing, a figure explained in full in the linked piece. The other
three sit in a range that, while material, does not approach that scale.

## What this is, and isn't

This is four verified DART filings, not a claim about how common multi-bond, same-day dilution events
are across the roughly 2,500 companies that file with DART. We have not built a base rate for that.
What we can say is narrower: in the same three-week window, we found four such filings, and their
disclosed dilution ranged more than twentyfold.
