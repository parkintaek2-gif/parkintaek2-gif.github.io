October was a hard month for Korean stocks. From the last close of September to 22 October, the KOSPI fell **19.07%**, from 6,838.04 to 5,533.77.

One sector index went the other way.

## Entertainment against the market

| | 30 Sep | 22 Oct | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 6,838.04 | 5,533.77 | **−19.07%** |
| KOSPI entertainment & culture (13 stocks) | 789.29 | 843.55 | **+6.87%** |
| KOSDAQ | 855.91 | 764.10 | **−10.73%** |
| KOSDAQ entertainment & culture (55 stocks) | 282.36 | 289.27 | **+2.45%** |

That is a gap of about **26 percentage points** on the main board and **13 points** on KOSDAQ.

## The worst days

| Date | KOSPI | KOSPI entertainment & culture |
|---|---|---|
| 8 Oct | −2.62% | −1.27% |
| 12 Oct | −3.65% | −2.37% |
| 13 Oct | −5.46% | **+2.11%** |
| 15 Oct | **−7.57%** | **+0.22%** |
| 20 Oct | −3.73% | −1.56% |
| 22 Oct | −3.52% | −1.70% |

On the two biggest down days, 13 and 15 October, the entertainment index rose. Its two best days came on 16 and 19 October (**+4.01%** and **+4.24%**).

## Why this is not a comeback story

The rise starts from a low base. On 22 October the KOSPI entertainment index was still **40.48% below** where it began 2026, and the KOSDAQ version **42.57%** below. The KOSPI itself was **31.31% up** on the year even after October.

So October narrowed a very wide gap. It did not reverse it. A sector that had already fallen 44% had less of the year's gains to give back when the market turned.
