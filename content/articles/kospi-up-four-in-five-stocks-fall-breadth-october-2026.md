The KOSPI closed **0.30% higher** on Friday, 23 October, at 5,550.30. On the same day, according to Yonhap, **716 KOSPI stocks fell and 173 rose**. Four in five stocks were down on a day the index was up.

That looks like an odd day. In the data we have collected since late August, it is closer to the normal pattern.

## Up days when most stocks fell

We counted advancers and decliners on every KOSPI session from 27 August to 22 October, 37 trading days.

| | Sessions |
|---|---|
| Index rose | 17 |
| … and more stocks fell than rose | **9** |
| Index fell | 20 |
| … and more stocks rose than fell | 4 |

On **more than half of the up days**, most KOSPI stocks were down. The most lopsided before Friday was 16 September: the index rose 1.37% while 578 stocks fell and 304 rose. Friday's 716 to 173 is wider than any up day in our window.

## Two companies move the index

The reason is concentration. On 22 October, **Samsung Electronics and SK hynix together made up 47.0% of the KOSPI's market value**. When both rise, the index can rise with most of the other 900-odd stocks falling. [In August we found](/article/korea-everything-comes-down-to-four) that four companies made up half of the market; the breadth count shows what that does day to day. On Friday, SK hynix rose 4.42% and Samsung 0.47%.

It works the other way too. On 28 September the index fell 2.70% while 611 stocks rose and 249 fell; Samsung lost 5.43% and SK hynix 5.05% that day.

## The index fell further than the typical stock

| 26 August → 22 October | Change |
|---|---|
| KOSPI index | **−18.7%** |
| SK hynix | −27.5% |
| Samsung Electronics | −18.0% |
| Median KOSPI stock (940 listings) | **−6.9%** |

Most KOSPI stocks are lower than in late August: 721 of 940. But the typical one has fallen about a third as far as the index. The headline number since August is mostly a chip number.

## What this does not say

Breadth counts each stock once, whether it is worth 1 trillion won or 1 billion. It says how many stocks moved each way, not how much money did. A wide gap between the index and the median stock tells you the index is carried by a few names; it does not tell you which way either goes next.
