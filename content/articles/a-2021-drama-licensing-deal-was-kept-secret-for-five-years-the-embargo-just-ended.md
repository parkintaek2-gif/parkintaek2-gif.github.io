Samhwa Networks (KRX: 046390) filed an amendment on 23 September, 2026 to a contract it originally
disclosed on 27 September, 2021 — a global streaming license for its 16-episode drama *Now,
Showtime!*, sold to PCCW Vuclip, a Singapore-based OTT operator. The original filing withheld the
contract amount under a confidentiality request from the counterparty. This amendment discloses it,
five years later, because the embargo period is ending.

| Contract amount, now disclosed | ₩6,617,520,000 (stated as USD 5,600,000) |
| Revenue base used | ₩29,296,600,231 (2020 consolidated) |

We recomputed the ratio independently from the two raw figures in the filing and it matches the
filing's own stated 22.6% to one decimal place.

## Why a 2021 number is showing up in 2026

Korean disclosure rules let a company withhold a contract amount for a period when the counterparty
requests confidentiality, with a stated deadline for when the embargo lifts. This filing is that
lift: the original 27 September 2021 contract for *Now, Showtime!* stayed on the books as "amount
withheld" for nearly five years. The amendment filed 23 September 2026 — three days before the
26 September 2026 deadline stated in the original filing — fills in the number.

The ratio itself (22.6%) is measured against Samhwa Networks' 2020 revenue, because that was the
company's most recent annual revenue figure at the time the original contract was filed in 2021. It
is not a comparison to the company's current-year revenue.

## What this filing does and doesn't tell you

The filing confirms a ten-year global license (2021-2031) and its original value, and states the
counterparty relationship as "none" beyond the licensing deal. It does not report how *Now,
Showtime!* performed on PCCW Vuclip's platform, nor whether Samhwa Networks has signed comparable
licenses since. What it does confirm, directly from the filing's own text, is the mechanical reason
a five-year-old contract amount becomes public on a specific date: not a new event, but an embargo
clock running out.
