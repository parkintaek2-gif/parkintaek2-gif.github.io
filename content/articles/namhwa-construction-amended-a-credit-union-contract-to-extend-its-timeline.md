Namhwa Construction (KRX: 091590) filed an amendment on 23 September, 2026 to a previously disclosed
supply contract with Daegwang Community Credit Cooperative. The amendment's stated reason is narrow:
a change to the contract period. The financial terms — a total contract amount of ₩7,681,818,182,
equal to 13.56% of Namhwa Construction's revenue by the filing's own stated ratio — are not listed
among the amended items.

## What a period-only amendment does and doesn't tell you

Korean disclosure rules require a company to file an amendment whenever a previously disclosed
material contract changes in a way the rules define as reportable — and a schedule change, on its
own, qualifies even when the deal's value is untouched. This filing is that narrower case: the record
shows a contract worth more than a tenth of the company's revenue is still active and still valued
the same, with only its timeline moved. The filing does not state why the timeline changed or by how
much.
