On 3 September 2026, Korea Economic Daily reported that Samsung Fire and Samsung Life were pursuing acquisitions of large insurers in the UK and US. The same day, Samsung Life filed a "rumor or report clarification" with DART — Korea's official disclosure system — and its own wording draws a line between what it will say and what it will not.

## What the filing actually says

| Field | Content (from the filing) |
| --- | --- |
| Triggering report | Korea Economic Daily, 2026-09-03: "Samsung Fire and Samsung Life to acquire large UK/US insurers" |
| Company's clarification | Reviewing domestic and overseas investment targets to secure new growth drivers; nothing concrete decided as of this filing |
| Re-filing deadline | 2026-10-02, or sooner if something concrete is decided |
| Disclosure officer | 이완삼(Lee Wan-sam), Head of Management Support |

The clarification does not deny that Samsung Life is looking at insurers abroad. It denies only that anything has been decided. Those are different claims, and DART's own filing keeps them separate: "considering" is the word used, not "confirmed" and not "false."

## The distinction this piece is built to hold

A rumor that a company later confirms and a rumor a company denies look identical for a few weeks: both start as a headline, both get a company response, both wait. What separates them is what happens by the re-filing date the company itself sets. Samsung Life's is 2 October 2026 — five weeks from the original report. Until then, DART's own record shows a company reviewing options, not a company that has bought, or ruled out buying, anything.

This is the first "rumor or report clarification" filing Samsung Life's corporate registration number has made in DART's public disclosure list going back to January 2020 — checked directly against the list, not assumed. There is no prior instance at this company to compare against for how these clarifications here typically resolve.

This is a record of what Samsung Life told DART, not a prediction of what it will decide. Not investment advice.
