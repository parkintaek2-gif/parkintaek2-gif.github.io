Korea's third-quarter reporting week is over. **SK hynix** reported on Wednesday 28 October, **Samsung Electronics** on Thursday 29 October, and the KOSPI swung with them: **+6.64%** to 6,012.44 on Wednesday, **−1.24%** on Thursday, then **−3.48%** to 5,730.74 on Friday, according to Korean wire reports.

Before the results, we counted the targets brokers set going in ([the earnings-week preview](/article/korea-q3-earnings-week-october-26-30-2026-analyst-target-prices)). This is the other half: what the same brokers wrote **after** the numbers came out.

## What we counted

We took the 24 companies on The Asia Business Daily's calendar that reported from Monday to Thursday, and every broker report on them dated **after** their results day, up to Friday 30 October. That is **138 reports from 13 brokers**. Where the same broker had set a target earlier in our archive, we compared the two.

| | Reports |
|---|---|
| Target **cut** from the same broker's previous one | **19** |
| Target **raised** | **5** |
| Target unchanged | 32 |
| No earlier target from that broker in our archive | 79 |
| No target given | 3 |
| **Total** | **138** |

Of the 56 reports we could compare, a third were cuts. The 19 cuts averaged **−19.4%**. The five raises ran from +8.1% to +11.1%.

## One thing did not move

All **135** targets published after results were above the 29 October share price. The lowest, Mirae Asset's 190,000 won on LG Electronics, was **1.22 times** the price. The median was **1.58 times**. **29** were at least double. The highest was Hana Securities' 550,000 won on OCI Holdings, **3.05 times** the price.

Of the 138 reports, 135 said Buy. One said Neutral (Shinhan Securities on Hanwha Systems, with no target), and two gave no rating. The pattern is not new: in the Hankyung Consensus list we counted before results, all 42 rated reports on these companies were Buy, and over twelve years Korean brokers have issued [very few Sell calls](/article/korea-stock-rating-with-no-downside).

## Friday's four: not measured yet

Kia, APR, Hyundai E&C and Hyundai Mobis reported on Friday 30 October, and no report on them dated after that is in our archive yet. Kia was the day's largest move: Hankyung reported operating profit of 1.82 trillion won, 22.9% below the brokers' consensus of 2.36 trillion, and the shares closing at 95,600 won, down 11.73%. Before results, Kia's six firms in our file had a median target of 167,500 won ([Kia and Mobis before results](/article/hyundai-mobis-kia-target-prices-before-q3-results-october-2026)). What brokers do next we will count when the reports arrive.

## How to read this

A target is one broker's 12-month price figure. A gap of 1.58 times means the median target is 58% above the price, not that the price will go there. Korean brokers' targets [have been met about one time in five](/article/korean-broker-price-targets-one-in-five) in our earlier count. The same gap shows across the whole market: on 29 October, [none of 315 Korean companies traded above its median target](/article/korean-stock-target-prices-above-share-price-315-companies-2026).

*Statistics, not advice. This counts what brokers published; it is not a view on any company.*
