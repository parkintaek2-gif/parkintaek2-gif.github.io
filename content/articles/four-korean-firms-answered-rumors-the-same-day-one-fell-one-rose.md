Four separately listed Korean companies — Daol Investment & Securities, Jinheung, Hyosung Heavy
Industries, and HD Hyundai Heavy Industries — each filed the identical DART disclosure type on
18 September 2026: a response to a market rumor or press report, with their answer marked
"unconfirmed" (미확정). On every other business day in our 17-day scan, zero companies filed
this report type.

## Zero for 17 days, then four at once

| Date range | Rumor-response filings (companies) |
|---|---:|
| 25 Aug – 15 Sep, 19 Sep (17 scanned days) | 0 every day |
| **18 Sep** | **4** |

A KRX rumor-response inquiry is a request a company cannot decline: when trading volume or
price moves in a way exchange rules flag, or press coverage raises a question the exchange
wants addressed, the listed company must file an answer within a set window. Answering
"unconfirmed" means the company is not denying the report, not confirming it, and is not yet
able to say more — often because a matter is still under internal discussion.

## Two names, reportedly opposite reactions

Press coverage of the two heavy-industry names describes different outcomes the same day.
Jaekyung Ilbo reported HD Hyundai Heavy Industries closed down more than 4 percent amid the
uncertainty around its unconfirmed answer. The same outlet and Topstar News described Hyosung
Heavy Industries trading higher intraday on the same day. We have not independently recomputed
either figure from our own price archive, which does not yet extend to this date — we are
reporting what the press recorded, attributed as such, not a number we measured ourselves.

We found no same-day price reporting in our search for Daol Investment or Jinheung, so we are
not characterizing their market reaction.

## What this figure cannot tell you

DART's disclosure type confirms that a company was asked to respond to a rumor or report and
answered without confirming or denying it — it does not tell us what the underlying rumor was,
whether it will later prove true, or whether the two heavy-industry names' price moves were
driven by this filing specifically rather than something else happening the same day.

## Method

We scan DART's daily filing list and tag each report by matching its title against a fixed set
of category patterns, including the rumor-or-press-report response type. The 17-day comparison
window is every business day for which we hold a complete daily scan between 25 August and
19 September 2026. Price-move figures are quoted from press reporting, not recalculated from
our own KRX archive. Not investment advice.
