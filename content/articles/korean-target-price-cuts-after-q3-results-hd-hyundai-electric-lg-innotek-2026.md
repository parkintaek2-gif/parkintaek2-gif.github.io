In 56 of them we could see the same broker's previous target in our archive. **19 were cuts.** Five were raises ([the full count](/article/korea-q3-results-broker-target-price-cuts-raises-after-earnings-october-2026)).

The previous targets were set between 28 September and 19 October. The average cut was **−19.4%**.

## HD Hyundai Electric: five of eight

The power-equipment maker reported on Tuesday 27 October. Financial News, citing Shinhan Securities, put its operating profit at **284.5 billion won**, about 7% below expectations, with orders of **980 million dollars**, down 19% from a year earlier. Eight reports followed on 28 October. Five cut, one held (SK Securities at 650,000), and two came from brokers with no earlier target in our list.

Our list misses at least one more cut. Financial News reported Shinhan Securities going from 1,000,000 to 850,000 won; we hold only the 850,000. **Six of eight** is therefore the better count, and the gap is a limit of our archive, not of the brokers.

The shares closed at 440,500 won on 29 October. The new targets run from 650,000 to 1,000,000, with a median of 850,000, **1.93 times** the price.

## Shipbuilding and defence

Samsung Heavy Industries took two cuts of more than a fifth, from Mirae Asset and SK Securities. Hanwha Aerospace took one, from Mirae Asset; the seven other Hanwha Aerospace reports came from brokers with no earlier target in our archive. Both groups have their own target pages: [Korean shipbuilding](/data/korea-shipbuilding-target-prices) and [Korean defence](/data/korea-defense-target-prices).

## A cut is still above the price

Every cut target was still above the company's 29 October close. The smallest gap among the 19 was iM Securities' 127,000 won on Shinhan Financial Group, **1.44 times** the 88,500 won price. Every cut kept a Buy rating. In Korea, a cut usually means a lower Buy, not a change of view ([the rating scale has almost no downside](/article/korea-stock-rating-with-no-downside)).
