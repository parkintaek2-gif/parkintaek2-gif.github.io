Two 5%-ownership disclosure filings landed with Korea's Financial Supervisory Service on 7 September,
2026, both naming POSCO Holdings as the reporting shareholder, both showing a stake decline of 15 or
more percentage points, and both landing at or within one hundredth of a percentage point of the 50%
line.

## The two filings, side by side

| Field | POSCO International | POSCO DX |
|---|---|---|
| Shares held after change | 87,984,395 | 76,017,365 |
| Change in shares held | -36,434,963 | -23,385,917 |
| Stake after change | 50.01% | 50.00% |
| Change in stake | -20.71 points | -15.38 points |
| Reason code given | "Change in number of held shares" | "Change in number of held shares" |

Both filings use the same generic reason code, which describes that a change happened without
specifying how — a market sale, an off-market block trade, and a subsidiary's own new share issuance
would all be reported this same way. We did not find a separate, more specific disclosure for either
move in the filings we checked.

## Why the round numbers stand out

A stake landing at 50.01% or exactly 50.00% is either a coincidence or the result of a transaction
sized to land there — Korean corporate law and several regulatory thresholds treat 50% as a
control-defining line. The filings alone do not tell us which. What they do establish, independently,
is that both changes were filed by the same reporting entity on the same calendar day, each moving by
double-digit percentage points.
