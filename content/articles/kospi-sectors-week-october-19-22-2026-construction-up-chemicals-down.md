The KOSPI's week ran **up, down, up, down**: +1.76% on Monday 19 October, −3.73% on Tuesday, +3.29% on Wednesday, −3.52% on Thursday. From Friday 16 October's close to Thursday's, the index lost **2.37%**, to 5,533.77.

That is mild by October's standards. The month to date is **−19.07%**.

## What rose

| KOSPI index | Stocks | 16→22 Oct | October to date |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI 200 construction | 10 | **+5.50%** | −9.97% |
| General services | 32 | +3.02% | −0.61% |
| Pharmaceuticals | 49 | +1.88% | +1.21% |
| Insurance | 11 | +1.24% | −9.45% |
| KOSPI 200 healthcare | 14 | +0.46% | −0.38% |
| Electricity & gas | 10 | +0.30% | −3.23% |

## The big names held up better than the middle

| KOSPI size index | 16→22 Oct | October to date |
|---|---|---|
| KOSPI large caps (100) | −2.31% | −20.09% |
| KOSPI mid caps (200) | −3.13% | −8.11% |
| KOSPI small caps (526) | −2.07% | −4.09% |
| KOSPI 200 information technology | −3.18% | **−24.69%** |

This week the gap between large and small was small. Over the month it is wide: large caps down a fifth, small caps down 4% ([why October split the market by size](/article/kospi-october-2026-fall-large-caps-small-caps-sectors)). KOSPI 200 information technology, the index that carried the year (**+92.40%** year to date as published), is down **24.69%** in October.

## One index that has gone its own way all month

The entertainment & culture index was flat this week (+0.08%) but is **up 6.87%** for October while the KOSPI is down 19% ([the story](/article/korea-entertainment-stocks-rose-october-2026-while-kospi-fell-19)).
