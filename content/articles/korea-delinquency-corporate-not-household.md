Search for Korean credit in English and you will find household debt. The ₩2,000-trillion total, the debt-to-GDP rank, the warnings. It is a real number and we have written about it.

But the Bank of Korea publishes a monthly delinquency series split three ways, and over the last thirty-two months **the line that moved is not the household one.**

## The three lines

Bank loan delinquency, one month or more overdue:

| Month | Corporate | Household | Credit-card |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023-12 | 0.4% | 0.3% | 1.6% |
| 2024-06 | 0.5% | 0.4% | 1.7% |
| 2024-12 | 0.5% | 0.4% | 1.9% |
| 2025-06 | 0.6% | 0.4% | 1.9% |
| 2025-12 | 0.6% | 0.4% | 1.6% |
| 2026-07 | 0.8% | 0.4% | 1.9% |

From **2023-12 to 2026-07**:

- **Corporate loans: 0.4% → 0.8%.** That is a 2.00x move, and it is the highest reading in the series.
- **Household loans: 0.3% → 0.4%.** One reported step, with a peak of 0.5% in 2025-05.
- **Credit-card loans: 1.6% → 1.9%.** The highest level of the three throughout, but **below** its 2.3% peak of 2025-02.

Households are the story everyone writes. Corporates are the line that doubled.

## Why the gap is easy to miss

Household debt is a **stock** story — the total is enormous and it keeps rising, so there is always a number to put in a headline. Delinquency is a **behaviour** story: it only moves when borrowers stop paying.

Korean households, on this measure, have not stopped paying. Korean companies, on this measure, increasingly have — from a very low base, to a level that is still low in absolute terms. **0.8% is not a crisis number.** Doubling in under three years is still the fastest-moving thing in the table.

## What this does not say

- **Banks only.** This table is domestic bank lending. The non-bank lenders — mutual credit cooperatives, savings banks, card issuers' own books — are not here, and historically that is where Korean credit stress appears first. We cannot see them in this series and we are not going to infer them.
- **One month overdue is early, not final.** It is not a write-off rate. Some of it cures.
- **One decimal place.** ECOS reports these rounded, so a 0.4-to-0.5 move is a single reported step. We have not tried to read anything finer.
- **The series starts in 2023-12.** It cannot be compared to 2008 or 2020 from this table, and we did not splice a different series onto it to pretend otherwise.
- There is **no sector, firm-size or region split** in this table. We looked.

On the stock rather than the repayment: [Korea's household debt quadrupled in 24 years and who holds it barely changed](/article/korea-household-debt-who-holds-it), and [the mortgage gap between men and women that went from 1.3% to 14.0%](/article/korea-mortgage-gap-men-women).
