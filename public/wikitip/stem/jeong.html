丁 (jeong) — 940 Korean entertainers were born on this day stem

IU, Jimin, Kang Seul-gi and 692 others. Ordered by how many Wikipedia editions carry an article about them, so the name you are most likely to know comes first.

245 of these 940 people are counted but not listed. Wikidata holds no English name for them, only a Korean one. This is an English-language site, so putting a name our readers cannot read next to a date would help nobody — but dropping them from the count would be worse. They are inside every figure on this page and in the totals.

This is a count, not a reading. A day stem is one character of the four pillars, and the hour pillar cannot be built for anyone here: public records carry birth dates and almost never birth hours.

Across all 9,249 people we counted, the spread over the ten stems came out at chi-square 17.91 against a 0.05 threshold of 16.92. It crossed by a hair, we tried four ways to kill it, and we do not call it a finding. The tests are here.

Nothing on this page says what a 丁 day means for anyone.
