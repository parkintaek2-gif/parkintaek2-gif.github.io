Weeks are labelled the way Netflix labels them. A chart place means one country's weekly top 10 listed the title once — not a count of viewers.

Each page is the Korean slice of that week. We do not republish the whole chart. What is inside Netflix's two files is written up separately, and the files themselves are Netflix's to publish, not ours.
